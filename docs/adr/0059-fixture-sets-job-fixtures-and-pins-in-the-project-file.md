# ADR 0059: Fixture sets, job fixtures and pins in the project file: set nodes with an On-this-page register, job fixtures beside them with a lock, the pin kind and pin and screw grids, two fixed jobs per pin or screw, format 47

**Status:** Proposed 2026-11-12, `needs-customer` (document model and file format). Built on the customer's decisions of 2026-11-12: Frage 207 B (a fixture set is a named group of the machine; a laser page switches on any number of them; plus the page's own job fixtures), 208 A (pin: a round fixture with a diameter, single or gridded), 209 A (centre mark: a cross in a circle with a settable size, for pins and screws; plus engravable diameters), 210 (job fixtures are never mirrored; "Apply fixtures from page" copies another page's job fixtures), 211 B (a fixture in a set keeps its own On the bed check), P16 reduced (the screw keeps its Head and Thread presentation). **Amends** [ADR 0049](0049-laser-fixtures-in-the-project-file.md) §1 (a sixth kind), §2 (parent, parameters, a lock), §7 (format) and [ADR 0052](0052-laser-fixture-jobs-in-the-project-file.md) §1 to §3 (a role register, new part keys); notes go on both on acceptance. The profile record is [ADR 0060](0060-fixture-sets-in-the-machine-profile-record.md). Spec: [`0113`](../../specs/0113-fixture-sets-and-pins/specification.md) criteria 1 to 40, 42. Feature notes: [`0113` adrs.md](../../specs/0113-fixture-sets-and-pins/adrs.md).

## Context

Today a laser page holds one flat list of fixture nodes, each a child of the manufacturing node, copied from the profile. `0113` needs named sets that the page switches on or off, fixtures of this page only that a profile action never touches, a lock for those, a round pin, pins and screws laid out as a grid, and two fixed jobs (Centre mark, Diameters) per pin or screw whose Output is the toggle in the fixture row.

## Options

**How a set is stored**

- **A. A set node, `shape = "fixture_set"`, child of the manufacturing, holding its fixtures as children (chosen).** Order of sets and of their fixtures is the tree's child order; removing a set removes its fixtures, slots and jobs in one tree delete, revived by undo (ADR 0014); no sweep.
- **B. A `fixture_set` register on each fixture naming its set.** A set delete must sweep its fixtures; set order needs a second order. Rejected (ADR 0052 option B for the same reason).
- **C. Sets as an atomic list on the manufacturing.** Two peers editing two sets lose one edit. Rejected.

**Job fixtures**

- **A. Fixture nodes directly under the manufacturing, beside the sets (chosen).** After the migration (decision 7) every direct fixture child is a job fixture, so no marker is needed.
- **B. A job-fixture container node.** One more shape and level for no gain. Rejected.

**Old files (page-level fixtures, no set)**

- **A. Migrate at open into one set "Set 1", one `migrate_format` commit (ADR 0042 §9) (chosen).** Readers and commands know one shape.
- **B. Read them as a virtual "Set 1" and create the set node at its first edit.** Every set command and reader carries a second path forever. Rejected; the spec's "nothing written on open" (Proposal) changes (feature notes S1).

**Which jobs are Centre mark and Diameters**

- **A. A `job_role` register on the job (chosen).** The toggle finds its job by role; commands refuse to delete or re-tick a role job.
- **B. Derived from the job's ticks.** A merge or a hand-made file could make two jobs match. Rejected.

## Decision

1. **Set node** (`shape = "fixture_set"`, child of the manufacturing, sets first in child order): `name` (existing; 1 to 40 characters, need not be unique) and `set_on_page` (boolean, absent = on). Its children are fixture nodes (ADR 0049 §2). A `fixture_set` elsewhere, or a non-fixture child of a set, is kept and never read (as the slot orphan of ADR 0049).
2. **Job fixture** = a fixture node that is a direct child of the manufacturing. It carries `fixture_locked` (boolean, absent = off; a separate key, not the drawing `locked` of ADR 0015, because a locked fixture stays selectable). `fixture_locked` on a set fixture is kept and ignored.
3. **On the bed** (Frage 211 B): a set fixture lies on the bed when its set's `set_on_page` and its own `active` are on; a job fixture when its `active` is on. Only a fixture on the bed is drawn as bed, hit, a snap or fit target and runs jobs (`0103` criteria 13 to 15 gain the set condition). Order everywhere (Layers, jobs, summary): sets in child order with their fixtures in child order, then job fixtures in child order. Pure rule in `curvyo-laser-core/src/fixture_sets.rs` ("which fixtures of a page lie on the bed and in what order").
4. **Pin and grids** (amends ADR 0049 §1, §2). New kind `curvyo.laser/pin`, `fixture_params`: `diameter_mm` (0.5 to 1,000, default 6). Pin and screw both gain `columns`, `rows` (integers 1 to 50, product at most 400), `column_spacing_mm`, `row_spacing_mm` (1 to 1,000, centre to centre, default 20) and `mark_mm` (the Centre mark size, 0.5 to 50, default 4); absent keys read as their defaults, so an old screw is a 1 x 1 grid. The origin is the centre of the first pin or screw; columns run in +x, rows in +y of the page (down on the screen). Their `fixture_matrix` is a translation: a rotation or anything else reads as its translation part and counts one damaged value (they never turn, `0113` criterion 21). Damage per field as ADR 0049 §6; a product above 400 reads both counts as 1 and counts one damaged value. Centres: `curvyo-laser-core/src/fixture_grid.rs` ("the centres of a pin or screw grid and its limits").
5. **Fixed jobs** (amends ADR 0052 §1 to §3). A pin and a screw are created with two `fixture_job` children, kind `score`, `op_output` = `false`, and `job_role` = `centre_mark` (tick `target.<id>.marks`) or `diameters` (pin: `.circle`; screw: `.head` and `.thread`). New part suffixes: `.circle` (pin) and `.marks` (pin, screw); one key covers every centre of the grid. Commands refuse to delete a role job, to change its ticks, and to add a job to a pin or a screw; the role jobs are deleted with their fixture. Reads: a `job_role` that is not one of the two, on a kind other than pin or screw, or the second job of the same role on one fixture, counts one damaged value and the job reads as an ordinary job. Jobs a screw got under `0103` stay and run as before. Geometry of the two parts: `curvyo-laser-core/src/fixture_marks.rs` ("the lines of centre marks and diameters"): a circle of `mark_mm` and two cross lines spanning it at every centre; the pin's circle, the screw's head and thread circles at every centre.
6. **Commands** (`curvyo-document-core`, one commit each, labels in the feature notes): add, rename, remove, move a set; set on page on and off; add a fixture into a set or as a job fixture (the add labels gain `add_pin`); lock and unlock; **Apply fixtures from page**: deep copies of every job fixture of the source manufacturing (fixture, slots, jobs, registers) appended after this page's job fixtures with fresh node ids, the same `fixture_matrix` (work-area position), name, `fixture_locked`, `visible` and `active`, every job's `op_output` = `false`, and every `target.` key remapped to the copy's own node ids; never mirrored (Frage 210). **Pick profile** and **Reset from profile** (ADR 0050 §4, ADR 0060) replace the set nodes only; job fixtures stay. Commands check the shape and the parent: a set id passed to a fixture command is refused.
7. **`format_version` 47.** A build of 46 refuses a manufacturing child of shape `fixture_set` as damage (the manufacturing validator, ADR 0046 §6 and the format-39 note of `CURRENT_FORMAT_VERSION`); the bump makes it a version refusal, as ADR 0033 §7 requires. A `pin` kind or `fixture_locked` alone would not need it. **Migration from 46 and below** (`curvyo-document-core`, at open, one `migrate_format` commit, no undo step, the file on disk unchanged until saved): per manufacturing with direct fixture children, one set node "Set 1" (on) is created at the first place and those fixtures move into it in order, jobs and ticks unchanged (target keys are node ids, which a move keeps); every screw without role jobs gets the two (decision 5, Output off). Two peers migrating one shared document offline get two "Set 1" nodes, and the merged tree may split the fixtures between them (a tree move is last-writer-wins per node) (development build, compatibility waived by the customer 2026-10-29). The validator accepts a set node and its children.
8. **Limits** (UI add refusals; the reader reads everything): 16 sets per page, 64 job fixtures per page, and the slot limit of ADR 0049 §3 per holder (`0113` criterion 17, confirmed).
9. **Goldens** in `curvyo-document-core/tests/fixtures/`: `fixture_sets_v47.curvyo` (`0113` criterion 38: two sets, one off; a grid, a holder with two slots, a 4 x 1 screw grid with Centre mark on and `mark_mm` 3, a fixture with `active` off; a locked 2 x 2 pin grid with Diameters on, a single pin; a hand-set tick on a holder job); `fixture_sets_damaged_v47.curvyo` (criterion 40: text in `diameter_mm`, a NaN spacing, a missing set name, a pin with no diameter, a count of 0, a product of 401, a third role job, a rotated pin matrix); `fixtures_pre_sets_v46.curvyo` (criterion 39: flat fixtures with jobs and a screw, opens as "Set 1" with role jobs added by the migration). The existing fixture goldens (`fixtures_*_v40`, `fixture_jobs_*_v41`, `fixtures_grid_v39`) open through the migration; their "nothing written on open" assertions become "only the migration commit".

## Consequences

- Every walk of a manufacturing's children now meets sets: the skip predicate of ADR 0032 already covers the whole subtree, so drawing walks stay safe; the two raw walks (`read_fixture`, `validate`) and the fixture-job reads must follow the set level. A fixture is at depth 2 and a job at depth 3 under the manufacturing.
- A set that is off hides its fixtures from every reader but the Fixtures tab; `0029` must use the decision 3 rule, never the raw list.
- Apply copies are independent nodes; nothing links them to their source.
- Published keys: `fixture_set`, `set_on_page`, `fixture_locked`, the kind `pin`, the parameters of decision 4, `job_role` and its two values, the part suffixes `.circle` and `.marks`. Renaming one is a format change.
- Rejected and why: a set register on fixtures (B, sweep and second order), an atomic set list (C, lost edits), a container for job fixtures (B), a virtual legacy set (B, two paths), roles derived from ticks (B, ambiguous).
