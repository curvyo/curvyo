# ADR 0081: Print and cut: registration marks as a leaf kind, measures on the placement, and a least-squares similarity fit in `curvyo-geometry-core`

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-24 with PR #457 (proposed 2026-11-23, `needs-customer`: document model, file format, a new leaf kind and a new register on placements). **Frage 333** PNC-MODEL at its default A ([`0121` adrs.md](../../specs/0121-print-and-cut/adrs.md)). Spec: [`0121`](../../specs/0121-print-and-cut/specification.md) slice 1, criteria 1 to 22, Fragen 328 to 332 at their defaults (A each). Builds on [ADR 0045](0045-clones-of-groups-texts-images-and-movable-placed-pages.md) §7 (placements move by one `placement_link` write), [ADR 0064](0064-laser-job-builder-and-output-writers.md) (job, `Frame`), [ADR 0065](0065-laser-output-settings-and-air-assist.md) (Start), [ADR 0027](0027-linked-dimension-objects.md) (a leaf kind that never burns).

**In plain words (for the customer).** A registration mark is its own kind of object, like a dimension: you place it, move it and print it, but the laser never burns it, and its centre is exact because Curvyo draws it, not you. The numbers you measure on the bed are saved with the placed sheet on the laser page. Apply only moves, turns and scales the placed sheet, in one undo step; your drawing is never changed. The calculation is plain arithmetic in the geometry library and gives the same answer on every computer. Files with marks need the new app version (the file format number goes up).

## Context

Slice 1 needs three stored things (marks on the sheet, measures on the laser page, the placement after Apply) and one computation (the fit). A placed page already stores a full matrix from source page-local mm to output page-local mm (`placement_link`, ADR 0045 §7; `PlacedPage::matrix`), so Apply needs no new placement field, and a similarity, a mirror or a full affine fit can all be written there. The job maps a laser-page point to G-code by `Frame::machine_point` (origin corner, bed size; `curvyo-laser-core/src/job_moves.rs`), an involution, and writes absolute `G90` moves in work coordinates unless Start is Head position (`G91`). The connector's `Status.position` is already the work position (`curvyo-connector-io` takes `WPos`, or `MPos − WCO`), the frame the job's `G90` moves use. Groups have no matrix (transforms are baked into leaves), so a mark's centre cannot be "the group origin".

## Options

**Marks in the document model**

| Option | Why it won or lost |
|---|---|
| **A. A new leaf kind `registration_mark` (centre, number, diameter, line width), the object an ordinary group tagged `registration_marks` (chosen)** | The centre is a stored point, never derived from edited geometry (criterion 2); "never burns" is one arm in the job's leaf read, as for `Dimension`; selection, entering, Delete, copy, paste and group clones come from the group (criteria 1, 3, 6). Cost: a new `ObjectSnapshot` arm in document, ui, render, svg and editor crates, and a format bump. |
| B. Ordinary shapes (circle primitive, cross path, digit text) in a tagged group per mark | No new kind, but the maker can enter and edit the circle so the centre becomes ambiguous, the digit needs a font, and every job, hit and export path must walk up to a tag to know a shape is a mark. |
| C. A style flag "registration" on any shape | Any shape becomes a mark with no defined centre; contradicts criterion 2 (one mark shape). |
| D. One leaf holding all marks as a list | Moving one mark needs a new sub-edit mode; A gets it from group entering. |

**Where the measures live**

| Option | Why it won or lost |
|---|---|
| **A. One atomic register on the placement node (chosen)** | A measure belongs to one physical sheet = one placement; two placements of the same page measure separately; deleting the placement drops it. |
| B. On the laser page's manufacturing node (as ADR 0065's process registers) | Needs a placement id inside and a rule for two placed sheets; no gain. |

**Where the fit lives**

| Option | Why it won or lost |
|---|---|
| **A. `curvyo-geometry-core`, module `point_fit.rs` (chosen)** | Machine-neutral geometry (point pairs to a map); the cutting plotter (slice 4) and the camera calibration (`0120`) can call it without depending on `laser-core`; already pure, wasm32, on `document-core`'s newtypes. |
| B. `curvyo-laser-core` | Ties a geometric fit to one machine family. Laser-specific reading (machine frame, warnings) goes there instead (decision 5). |
| C. A new crate | One function; no ADR-worthy boundary (`CLAUDE.md` §5). |

## Decision

1. **Mark leaf.** Shape `registration_mark`, registers: `mark_number` (integer 1 to 4), `mark_centre` (`[x, y]`, document mm), `mark_diameter` (mm, 3 to 20, default 6), `mark_line` (mm, 0.05 to 1, default 0.25). Drawn lines (one module `registration_mark_lines.rs` in `document-core`, "the drawn lines of a registration mark"): circle of the diameter, crosshair of the diameter plus 2 mm (8 mm at 6), the number as a fixed stroke digit half the diameter high (3 mm at 6) from a built-in table (no font), its box's lower-left corner 0.5 mm outside the circle's square, up and right (amended 2026-11-23 to spec criterion 2 and UX notes P5; plan D6), all black at the line width. Canvas, SVG, PNG, hit and bounds read these lines, so the print and the screen agree. A transform maps the centre only; diameter and line are never scaled or turned (a mark always prints at its stated size). No style panel, no own style. Not a clone origin on its own; a clone of a marks group draws its marks at mapped centres.
2. **Marks object.** An ordinary group with `registration_marks = true`, created by the Document tab's Add registration marks (one step, `insert_registration_marks`, history "Add registration marks"): four marks placed by the rule of criterion 1 (page corners inset 10 mm when clear of the artwork box by 10 mm, else the artwork box grown by 10 mm, clamped 10 mm inside the page). Its Diameter and Line boxes write every mark of the group in one step; Add mark writes the lowest free number up to 4. A Delete that would leave the group with exactly 1 mark is refused ("Registration marks need at least 2 marks.", criterion 3, `ObjectEditError::MarksBelowTwo`); deleting every mark or the object deletes it whole (amended 2026-11-23, plan D2). Ungroup is not offered for it (Proposal). Merges may still leave 1, 5 or two marks of one number: nothing is repaired on read; decision 5 reports them.
3. **Never burns, always prints.** `leaf_geometry` (editor-wasm `session/output`) returns `None` for a mark, as for a dimension, on the laser page itself and through a placement (criterion 4); no operation kind sees it. An operation whose members are only marks lists as "Registration marks (not cut)" (criterion 5). SVG export writes each mark as `<g data-curvyo-kind="registration-mark" data-curvyo-mark="n">` with a `<circle>` and one `<path>` (cross and digit), stroke black, no fill; import reads plain shapes (spec). PNG export draws them like any leaf. On a laser page the renderer draws a placed sheet's marks as faint crosses in a colour no operation uses and hit testing skips them as burn items (criterion 5).
4. **Measures on the placement.** Register `x.curvyo.laser.print_and_cut` on a placement node, one atomic map: `marks` (`{"1": [x_um, y_um], ...}`, integer µm in machine work coordinates, as measured), `tolerance_um` (50 to 5000, default 500), `reset` (the 6-number matrix before the last Apply, optional). Absent = no measure (a project without it opens as before, criterion 19). A register that does not read shows "The stored measures could not be read." with empty rows and is replaced by the next measure write. Duplicate and Ctrl copy of a placement do not copy it (a measure belongs to one sheet, Proposal). Each row edit, Clear and Take head position is one step `print_and_cut_measure` (history "Measure mark"); a tolerance change is one step `print_and_cut_tolerance` (history "Print and cut tolerance", UX notes).
5. **The reading** (`curvyo-laser-core/src/print_and_cut.rs`, "the Print and cut state of a placed sheet, from its measures to the fit with its warnings"; words in `print_and_cut_text.rs`). Input: the sheet's first marks object in tree order (number to centre in source page-local mm, read by `document-core`), the register, the page's `Frame`, the current placement matrix, the page's Start. It converts each measure to laser page-local mm by `Frame::machine_point` (its own inverse), so an origin-corner flip is never mistaken for a mirrored sheet, and pairs rows with marks by number. Causes, text-free, in this order: no marks object (group absent); unreadable register; row without a mark ("Mark 3 is gone from the sheet"); a number twice on the sheet (that mark is not used); fewer than 2 pairs; too close; scale outside 98 to 102 %; mirrored; largest residual above tolerance (names the worst mark); Start is Head position ("Start is Head position: the cut will not follow the sheet", Proposal, permit); placement differs from the fit. Every warning permits Apply (criteria 13, 14).
6. **The fit** (`curvyo-geometry-core/src/point_fit.rs`, "fits a similarity map to matched point pairs by least squares"): `fit_similarity(pairs: &[PointPair], min_spread: Length) -> Result<SimilarityFit, FitError>`. Closed form (Umeyama without reflection): centroids, `d = Σ|P|²`, `a = Σ P·Q / d`, `b = Σ P×Q / d`, linear part `[a −b; b a]`, translation `q̄ − M p̄`. The matrix uses only `+ − × ÷`, so it is bit-identical on every target; `scale = hypot(a, b)` and `angle = atan2(b, a)` (via `libm`) are for display only. Result: `map: Affine`, `scale` (dimensionless ratio), `angle: Angle`, per-pair `residuals: Vec<Length>`, `largest`, `rms`, and `mirrored_rms` (the same fit with reflection, `[a' b'; b' −a']`, for 3 or more pairs). Errors: fewer than 2 pairs; no two sheet points or no two measured points at least `min_spread` apart (`TooClose`, 1 mm from `laser-core`, criterion 15). 2 pairs fit exactly; residual 0. Tolerances, all explicit constants in `print_and_cut.rs`: `MIN_SPREAD` 1 mm; `MIRROR_MARGIN` 0.01 mm (mirrored when `mirrored_rms + margin < rms`, so collinear marks never warn); `SCALE_WARN` 0.98 to 1.02; `SAME_PLACEMENT` 1 µm (the four sheet-frame corners under the current and the fitted matrix).
7. **Apply and Reset** (`curvyo-document-core/src/print_and_cut_commands.rs`). Apply writes, in one commit `apply_print_and_cut` (history "Fit sheet to marks", UX notes), the fitted matrix into `placement_link` and the previous matrix into `reset`; the drawing page is not touched (criterion 16). The existing off-page rule refuses a fit that leaves no overlap with the laser page ("The fit puts the sheet off the laser page.", Proposal). A fit with scale outside 50 to 200 % is not offered for Apply ("The fit is not usable: scale 31.00 %.", Proposal), since it is a typing error, not a printer. Reset (one commit `reset_print_and_cut`, history "Reset sheet placement") writes `reset` back and removes it. Reset is offered only while the placement equals the fit within `SAME_PLACEMENT`; after a hand move the group shows "Placement differs from the fit" with Apply again (criterion 18). No transform writer learns about print and cut.
8. **Head position** (criterion 8). The frontend's connector link keeps the last `Status`; Take head position passes its `position[0..2]` (mm) to the session's measure command, rounded to µm. `Running`, `Homing` and a jog (the connector reports it as `Idle`) show "Machine is moving"; `Door`, `Check` and `Sleep` show "Controller state: <state>"; every other state with a position is enabled (`Idle`, `Ready`, `Done`, also `Paused`, `Alarm`, `Receiving`, `AwaitingLocalStart`); no position shows "No position from the machine" (Proposal). Amended 2026-11-23 to spec criterion 8 as built: the read sends nothing and is one undo step, the head stands still in each of those states, and a position the controller no longer trusts (an unhomed `Alarm`) shows up as a residual above tolerance at 3 or more marks. No new connector message, no change to `curvyo-connector-core` or `-io`; nothing is sent (criterion 20). `0140`'s Store position reads the same helper when it is built.
9. **Format 55** (next free at merge; main is at 54). A format-54 build does not know the leaf kind or the register; the version check of ADR 0033 §7 refuses the file instead of guessing. Malformed mark registers (number outside 1 to 4, centre not two finite numbers, diameter or line out of range) refuse the file in the validator, as other leaf geometry does. Goldens in `curvyo-document-core/tests/fixtures/`: `print_and_cut_v55.curvyo` (a sheet with a 4-mark object, a laser page placing it fitted, 3 measures, tolerance 0.4 mm, a `reset`) and `print_and_cut_damaged_v55.curvyo` (two marks numbered 3, a 1-mark object, a register that is text, a measure that is not an integer; each opens as decisions 4 and 5 say and saves back unchanged). SVG golden `curvyo-svg-core/tests/fixtures/export/registration_marks.svg`; GRBL golden `print_and_cut_cut_line.gcode` as criterion 17.
10. **Later slices' seams.** *Jog, Go to mark* (slice 2): the expected machine position of mark n is `Frame::machine_point(placement × centre)`, a pure read added to `print_and_cut.rs`; the move is `0118`'s connector command. *Camera* (slice 3): the camera turns a clicked or found pixel into bed mm through `0120`'s calibration, and that is just another measure source into decision 4; the sheet fit stays a similarity, because a flat sheet on a flat bed is one. A homography belongs to the camera's pixel-to-bed map; if `0120` needs its fit, it goes beside this one as `curvyo-geometry-core/src/point_fit_projective.rs` (DLT over 4 or more pairs). *Affine fit* (Frage 331 C): `placement_link` already holds a full matrix, so it needs a second fit function and its own warnings, not a format change. *Plotter* (slice 4): its own family crate calls `fit_similarity`; no shared machine abstraction.

## Consequences

- New code: `document-core` (mark leaf model, codec, lines, commands, register codec, Apply, Reset, validator), `geometry-core` (`point_fit.rs`), `laser-core` (`print_and_cut.rs`, text), `ui-core` and `render-core` (hit, bounds, drawing of the new arm), `svg-core` (export), `editor-wasm` (session view and commands, `leaf_geometry` arm). No new crate, no new dependency, no new crate edge.
- Every exhaustive `ObjectSnapshot` match gains an arm; reviewers check that each says "never burns" or "draws its lines", never a silent `_`.
- A change of G54 or of the profile's origin corner between measuring and cutting moves the cut; the group shows "Placement differs from the fit" for the profile change only (the work offset is not visible to the app).
- **For the PO** (spec changes): criterion 8 says *work* position (as built, the `G90` frame), not `0140`'s machine position; Running *or Homing*; new warnings of decisions 5 and 7 (Start is Head position, off the page, scale outside 50 to 200 % not offered, unreadable register); "an older build shows the marks as plain shapes" becomes "an older build refuses the file"; slice 3's projective fit is the camera's, not the sheet's; Frage 331 C needs no placement change; "moved by X and Y" is the machine position of the sheet's page origin (Proposal).

## Verification

`point_fit.rs` unit tests: 2, 3, 4 pairs; known rotation, scale and offset without noise (residual below 1e-6 mm) and with fixed 0.2 mm noise (exact residuals); coincident sheet points and coincident measures (`TooClose`); a repeated measure; reversed order on 4 marks (mirrored), collinear marks (not mirrored); bit-equal matrix native and wasm32 (CI wasm build plus a pinned-bits test). `print_and_cut.rs`: each cause; origin-corner flips do not mirror. Document: Apply and Reset as one undo step each, drawing page bytes unchanged, off-page refusal, copy drops the register, goldens open and save back unchanged. Job: zero moves from marks under a Cut over everything, on the page and placed; the GRBL golden.
