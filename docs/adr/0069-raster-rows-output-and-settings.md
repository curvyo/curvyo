# ADR 0069: Raster rows in the laser job, GRBL output, preview, checks and stored settings: sweeps in the move list, seven `op_options` keys, `fill` and `dither` in the test grid recipe, format next free at merge

**Status:** Proposed 2026-11-18 (architect), `needs-customer` (document model and file format: the `op_options` keys and the test grid recipe; persistence: the material test record, Frage 237). Decisions marked **(Proposal)** are vetoable at the demo. Accepted with the customer's merge of the `0123` story PR, as ADR 0065 was. Extends [ADR 0064](0064-laser-job-builder-and-output-writers.md) §4 (images in Engrave now run), §6, §7, §8, §9, §10 and [ADR 0056](0056-laser-operation-tools-in-the-project-file.md) §6 (new keys), [ADR 0057](0057-test-grid-object-with-derived-operations.md) §1 and [ADR 0058](0058-material-records-shared-across-lasers.md) §2 (recipe keys); notes go on them on acceptance. Dot values: [ADR 0068](0068-raster-dot-values-and-dithering.md). Spec: [`0123`](../../specs/0123-raster-engraving/specification.md) criteria 1 to 3, 11 to 22, 31 to 41, 44, 45.

**In plain words (for the customer).** The job holds each raster row as a start, a direction and the burn strength of each dot, not one move per dot. The G-code writer, the Toolpath view and the time estimate all read the same rows. The image settings are seven more values in the operation's settings; the gradient test grid remembers Fill and Dither. Nothing about dots is saved in the project.

## Context

ADR 0064's move list is one `Move { to, kind, block }` per straight segment. A raster item at 0.1 mm over 200 x 200 mm has 2,000 rows and up to 2 million runs; one move per run is affordable, one per dot is not, and the 50 million dot limit (ADR 0068 §7) must fit wasm memory. Criterion 14 asks for rows of runs that every writer and the preview read through one function.

## Options

| Option | Why it won or lost |
|---|---|
| A. Each run as ordinary `Burn` moves with a power field | Simple for writers, but a worst-case dither at the 50 million limit is 25 million moves of about 40 bytes (1 GB). Lost on memory. |
| **B. A `Sweep` move per burned stretch of a row, pointing into the job's sweep table, which holds one level per dot (chosen)** | Memory is one bit per dot (one byte in Grey); writers, timeline and preview iterate runs from one function; the rest of the move list (travel, run-out, Z, passes, frame) is unchanged. |
| C. A separate raster stream beside the move list | Two orders to merge in every reader (timeline, slider, writer). Lost. |

## Decision

1. **Sweeps** (`job_raster_rows.rs`, "the sweeps of one raster item"). After ADR 0068 the levels of each row are cut into **sweeps**: from the first burned dot to the last, split where an unburned gap is longer than **5 mm** (confirmed), with leading and trailing unburned dots and empty rows dropped. `Job` gains `sweeps: Vec<Sweep>`; `MoveKind` gains `Sweep { role, node, sweep: u32 }` whose `to` is the sweep's end edge. `Sweep { start: PointUm (the first dot's leading edge), direction (unit, from the scan frame, in run order), interval_um, levels: Levels }` with `Levels::Binary(bits) | Grey(Vec<u8>)` stored **in run order** (a reversed row stores its levels reversed). One function `Sweep::runs()` yields `Run { from, to, level }` with the endpoints rounded to µm from `start + i·I·direction`; a run's burned length is `dots × I`, so criterion 40's length check holds within rounding. **Power** of a run (Grey): `lowest + (set − lowest)·level / 255` in tenths of a percent, rounded half up, with `lowest` capped at the set's power (Proposal); binary levels burn at the set's power. One function, `run_power`, used by every writer and the preview.
2. **Order and moves** (criteria 14 to 17). In a block's main role the vector hatch and lines run first in ADR 0064 §5 order, then the raster items in stacking order, bottom first, each from its first row on; then the outline role (Engrave + Outline: the image's frame or the gradient shape's outline with the Score set). This is "the dots first, then the outline" of criterion 2; criterion 14's "rows after the vector paths" is read as "after the vector hatch, before the outlines" (criterion 14 says so). Between sweeps the head travels to the next sweep's start at the maximum feed; for bidirectional rows of equal extent this is the one-Interval step of criterion 15, otherwise the straight line between the two ends. Bidirectional alternates the run direction per row; off, every row runs in the base direction. Overscanning adds the ADR 0064 §6 run-out before and after each sweep as `RunOut` moves. Crosshatch is ignored for raster items. Passes repeat the block's moves with the same sweeps (no new dither).
3. **GRBL** (criterion 19, `job_write_grbl.rs`). M4: one `G1` to each run's end with `S` from `run_power` by the ADR 0064 §3 formula, `S0` over unburned stretches inside the sweep, consecutive runs of the same `S` merged into one `G1`, `F` only where it changes. M3: per run `M3 S<n>`, `G1`, `M5`, and the unburned stretches as `G0`. Grey on M3 never reaches the writer (decision 6). The line count is runs plus sweeps plus travel; never one line per dot. `0029`'s `image_skipped.gcode` is replaced by `raster_threshold.gcode`; the other goldens of criterion 40 are added in `tests/fixtures/output/grbl/`.
4. **Time and slider** (criteria 21, 22). The timeline counts a `Sweep` as one burn move of its length at the burn feed (the head sweeps its short gaps at that feed), travel and run-out as today; "Burn" sums sweeps, "travel" the travel moves. The slider's binary search is unchanged; a head inside a sweep is interpolated along it and the drawn part ends at the head.
5. **Preview** (criterion 20; ADR 0064 §10). The job's draw cache gets, per raster item, its runs as polylines in the Engrave look and one **coverage pyramid** (burned dot counts per 4 x 4, 16 x 16, … dots, `u8` fractions, about 1/15 of the dot count in bytes), built once per job. `toolpath_look.rs` draws the runs while rows are at least one device pixel apart, else the pyramid level whose cell is at least one device pixel as quads whose alpha is the coverage (render-core stays pixel-free; pan and zoom never iterate dots, 37 e). One arrowhead per item at its first sweep. **While an Image setting is edited**, the Operations view draws the item from a preview grid of `k·I` with `k` the smallest whole number for which `k·I` is at least one device pixel, built by the same ADR 0068 functions and drawn as coverage quads, ready in 50 ms (37 d); the full grid (times, checks, Toolpath view, Export) is built in the next frame (ADR 0064 §9's one build per frame), within 37 c.
6. **Checks** (criteria 31 to 36; ADR 0064 §8). New `Cause` variants after `0029`'s, in this order: `GreyNeedsM4` (block, per operation), `TooManyDots { item, count }` (block), `CoarseImages` (warn: page pixel size above `2·I`), `OverlappingRasterItems` (warn: two frames of one operation overlap with area above 0; images by their parallelograms, gradient shapes by their outline boxes, Proposal), `ReducedImages` (warn: `is_reduced`), `UnreadableImages` (warn, skipped), `LowestPowerAbovePower` (warn: Grey with Lowest power above the set's Power; every burned dot then runs at the Power, criterion 32 b). Raster items count in `OutsideBed` by their frame. **No "has no interval" cause**: by ADR 0056 §5 an Engrave always has an Interval (absent reads 0.10, anything else is Damaged settings), so the block cannot occur and criterion 32 no longer has it. Texts in `job_gate_text.rs`.
7. **`op_options` keys** (criterion 13; amends ADR 0056 §6), allowed on `engrave` and `engrave_outline` only, each optional with its default:
   ```text
   dither: "threshold" | "ordered" | "floyd_steinberg" | "jarvis" | "atkinson" | "stucki" | "grey"   = "floyd_steinberg"
   threshold_pct: integer 0..100 = 50     brightness_pct: integer -100..100 = 0
   contrast_pct: integer -100..100 = 0    gamma: 0.10..4.00, two decimals = 1.00
   invert: bool = false                   lowest_power_pct: 0.0..100.0, one decimal = 0.0
   ```
   Names carry the unit suffix of ADR 0056 (`overscan_pct`, `scan_angle_deg`): `threshold_pct`, `brightness_pct`, `contrast_pct`, `lowest_power_pct` as criterion 13 now says (Proposal). Damage is ADR 0056 §6's (unknown value, out of range, too many decimals, on another kind); material commands never touch them.
8. **Test grid** (criteria 44, 45; amends ADR 0057 §1 and ADR 0058 §2). The one recipe type (`test_recipe.rs`, `test_recipe_json.rs`) gains `fill: "solid" | "gradient"` (absent = solid) and `dither` (only with `gradient`; the six binary modes, `grey` or a value without `gradient` is damage). With Gradient every cell is a rectangle with the ADR 0066 Gradient fill (linear (0, 0.5) to (1, 0.5), `#000000FF` to `#FFFFFFFF`) and each derived cell operation carries `op_options.dither` and Crosshatch. Neither is an axis. **The recipe is also the material test record's recipe**, so the record format goes to **3** (format 2 records read as Solid; Recreate from Materials then brings Fill and Dither back) unless the customer answers Frage 237 with B.
9. **Nothing stored, caches** (criteria 38, 39). Plans stay per operation (ADR 0064 §9); the fingerprint gains the asset hash, the frame in µm, the `PlacedGradient` bits and the Image settings. The grey pyramid is per asset (ADR 0068 §3). Saving twice with an engraved image gives byte-identical files; two exports give identical bytes.
10. **Format** (criterion 41). Project `format_version`: the **next free number at merge** after `0124`'s: a build of the previous format reads the new `op_options` keys as damage (ADR 0056 §6) and the new recipe keys as a damaged recipe, and would hatch gradient shapes and drop images, so it must refuse the file (ADR 0033 §7). **Migration: none.** Goldens `raster_engraving_v<N>.curvyo` and `raster_engraving_damaged_v<N>.curvyo` (a `dither` of `"halftone"`, `gamma` 4.01, `dither` on a Cut) in `curvyo-document-core/tests/fixtures/`; material record goldens `format3_gradient_test.json` and `format2_reads_solid.json`.
11. **Budgets** (criterion 37 b, c, e, f; thread CPU, native) **confirmed**: GRBL writer 250 ms for 2 million runs; a job with that item and 50 Cut items within (a) of ADR 0068 §8 plus ADR 0064 §9 (a); pan and zoom 16 ms; checks 16 ms after an edit that keeps every plan.

## Consequences

- Every reader of `Move` handles `Sweep`; the compiler lists them (`match` without wildcard). A second output format implements `Sweep` in its writer as part of ADR 0064 §12.
- A raster job can still be slow to build at the dot limit (ADR 0068, Consequences); the preview grid keeps editing responsive.
- Published keys: the seven `op_options` keys and their values, the recipe's `fill` and `dither`. Renaming one is a format change.
- Rejected and why: one move per run (A, memory), a separate stream (C, two orders in every reader).
