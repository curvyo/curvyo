# ADR 0073: The image adjustment pipeline and the presets: integer pixel steps in `curvyo-image-core`, presets and Strength in `curvyo-laser-core`, one adjusted image per picture and Interval

**Status:** Proposed (architect, 2026-11-19). No customer decision needed (inside spec `0135`; no new crate, no new third-party dependency, no format beyond [ADR 0072](0072-image-adjustments-on-the-image-object.md)). Decisions marked **(Proposal)** are vetoable at the demo. Spec: [`0135`](../../specs/0135-image-optimisation/specification.md) criteria 3, 5 to 7, 11 to 21, 25 to 41, 54 to 56, 60. Builds on [ADR 0068](0068-raster-dot-values-and-dithering.md) §1 to §3 and §6 (the `laser-core → image-core` edge, the grey plane, determinism), [ADR 0069](0069-raster-rows-output-and-settings.md) §5, §9, §12 (preview, caches, the resumable build) and [ADR 0017](0017-image-nodes-and-embedded-assets.md) §4 (working image, `mip_level`).

**In plain words (for the customer).** The improvements are computed fresh from the original picture whenever they are needed, with whole numbers only, so every computer gets the same dots. A preset turns your Strength and the dot size of the operation into concrete steps; a picture used in two operations is improved once per dot size.

## Context

The adjusted image replaces the working image as the input of the dot values (spec criterion 19), must be byte-identical on native and wasm (17), must not be stored (18), and must keep the empty stack equal to `0123` byte for byte (1, 54). Presets depend on the Interval of the operation being evaluated (40, 60), so the same picture can have two adjusted images.

## Options

| Option | Why it won or lost |
|---|---|
| **A. Pixel steps and the picture analysis in `curvyo-image-core` (pixel units only); stored-field typing, presets, Strength and the mm-to-pixel resolution in `curvyo-laser-core` (chosen; the spec's Proposal)** | image-core stays a pure pixel crate with no Curvyo dependency (ADR 0017 §4) and no laser concept; the preset table names Dither modes and reads the Interval, both laser-core's (ADR 0068 option A). |
| B. Everything in image-core | It would need the `Dither` enum and the Interval of a laser operation in a decoder crate; ADR 0068 rejected the same move for dithering. |
| C. A new crate `curvyo-adjust-core` | One consumer chain (laser-core, read by ui-core); `CLAUDE.md` §5. |
| Gaussian blur by a kernel table | Cost grows with the radius (Local contrast reaches hundreds of pixels); **three box passes** cost the same at every radius and are integer by construction (chosen). |
| Non-local means for Denoise (the spec's Proposal) | About 20 billion operations on 4 Mpx with a 7 x 7 patch and a 21 x 21 search: more than ten times the 800 ms budget. **A 5 x 5 bilateral filter with integer weight tables** (chosen) keeps edges and fits. |
| Cache by object revision (the spec's wording) | Two clones, two operations at one Interval and an undo would recompute; **a content key** (chosen) shares them. |

## Decision

1. **Modules** (each under 500 lines, one-sentence doc comment).
   - `curvyo-image-core`: `adjust_plane.rs` (a grey plane of the picture), `adjust_blur.rs` (three-pass box blur), `adjust_tone.rs` (Levels, Curves and Channel mix as tables), `adjust_detail.rs` (Sharpen and Contours), `adjust_clahe.rs` (Local contrast), `adjust_denoise.rs` (the bilateral filter), `adjust_geometry.rs` (Crop and Resize), `adjust_mask.rs` (stored AI results applied, ADR 0077), `adjust_analysis.rs` (histogram, percentiles, median, Otsu level, noise estimate), `adjust_run.rs` (runs a list of `PixelStep`s). Its parameters are pixels and greys only: radii as a `SubPx(u32)` newtype in 1/16 pixel. New edge `image-core → libm` (workspace pin, in the lock file, as ADR 0068) for the 256-entry tables only.
   - `curvyo-laser-core`: `image_adjust_fields.rs` (the stored fields of each step with ranges and defaults; the frontend reads them from Rust, as ADR 0061 §5), `image_preset.rs` (the seven presets as data and the Strength mapping), `image_adjust_resolve.rs` (the effective pixel steps of one picture at one Interval), and `job_raster_screen.rs` (ADR 0074).
2. **Stored fields** (ADR 0072 §1; ranges of spec criterion 5): `crop` `left_pct top_pct width_pct height_pct`; `resize` `width_px`; `channel_mix` `red_pct green_pct blue_pct`; `levels` `black_pct white_pct gamma_centi`; `curves` `in_0..in_7`, `out_0..out_7` (`_pct`, present pairs are the points); `local_contrast` `radius_um amount_pct`; `contours` `width_um sensitivity_pct`; `sharpen` `radius_um amount_pct threshold_pct`; `denoise` `strength_pct detail_pct`; `remove_background` `softness_pct threshold_pct`; `upscale` `factor` (2 or 4); `ai_denoise` `strength_pct`. Radii in mm of the page are converted with the **pixel pitch** of the step's input (page length of one pixel along the image's longer page axis, as ADR 0068 §3; it changes after Crop, Resize and Upscale), `f64 + - * /` from integers, rounded to 1/16 pixel.
3. **Order.** Three zones: **geometry** (`crop`, `resize`, `upscale`, `ai_denoise`, in this fixed order), **tone** (`channel_mix`, `denoise`, `levels`, `curves`, `local_contrast`, `contours`, `sharpen`, in the stored order, which the maker may change), **mask** (`remove_background`, last). New steps are inserted at the spec's place (criterion 3). A list read out of zone order is run in zone order, stable (ADR 0072 §1). Before the first tone step other than Denoise the picture becomes grey; with no Channel mix that is ADR 0066 §4's `over_white_grey`, so a lone neutral step reproduces `0123`'s grey exactly. Steps before it run per channel on RGBA (alpha composited over white first, criterion 6). AI steps: at most one per kind (Proposal).
4. **Arithmetic** (criterion 17; every constant below is frozen by the goldens of criterion 54).
   - **Blur:** three separable box passes whose widths come from sigma by integer square root ("boxes for Gauss"), running sums in `u32`, each pass rounded half up.
   - **Levels:** `B, W` = round(255 · pct / 100); `x` = clamp(round((g − B) · 255 / (W − B))); then `round(255 · (x/255)^(1/γ))` with `libm::pow`, **the same Gamma convention as `0123` (ADR 0068 §4): above 1 lightens**. Curves: monotone cubic (Fritsch-Carlson) in `f64 + - * /` sampled to a 256-entry table. Channel mix: `clamp(round((r·R + g·G + b·B) / 100))`.
   - **Local contrast (CLAHE):** tiles of side 2 x radius (at least 8 px) from the top-left, 256-bin `u32` histograms, clip limit = mean bin count x (1 + Amount / 25), the excess spread evenly with the remainder to the lowest bins, per pixel the bilinear blend of the four tile tables in 1/256; Amount 0 is no step.
   - **Sharpen:** unsharp mask, `d = g − blur(g)`; unchanged where `100·|d| < threshold_pct · 255`, else `clamp(g + d · amount / 100)`, rounding half away from zero.
   - **Contours** (Frage 278 A; the spec's method confirmed): difference of two blurs at sigma = width / 2 and 1.6 x that; where the pixel is darker than its surroundings, `255 − clamp(d · (16 + 15 · sensitivity / 100) / 16)`, else 255.
   - **Denoise:** 5 x 5 bilateral, a fixed integer spatial table (sigma 1.5 px), range weights `round(256 · exp(−Δ² / 2σr²))` for Δ 0 to 255 by `libm::exp` with σr = 4 + (100 − detail) · 0.6 greys, then `g + (f − g) · strength / 100`. Golden `denoise_bilateral` (the spec's `denoise_nlm`).
   - **Resize:** separable area average, source spans in 1/65536 pixel, integer weights, rounding half up. **Crop:** pixel rectangle `floor(left · w / 100)` to `ceil((left + width) · w / 100)`.
   - **Analysis** (criterion 39) on the box mip of at most 1 Mpx after the geometry zone, grey by `over_white_grey`: percentile, median, Otsu (between-class variance compared by cross-multiplication in `u128`, ties to the lower grey), **noise estimate** in tenths of a grey level = `(14826 · m + 3000) / 6000` with `m` the median absolute response of Immerkær's kernel `[1 −2 1; −2 4 −2; 1 −2 1]` (its response has six times the noise sigma, so this is 1.4826 x MAD / 6).
5. **Presets** (`image_preset.rs`; spec criteria 31 to 38 as data). The derived steps are **quantised to the stored field units before they run**, so Make editable writes exactly what ran and the dots before and after it are byte-identical (criterion 29). Clip p becomes Black at percentile p and White at 100 − p, each rounded to whole %. The median target is reached with **γ = ln(m′) / ln(t)** in the convention of decision 4 (`m′` the median after the Black/White stretch, `t` the target, both in 0..1; the spec writes the inverse ratio for the other convention, same result), `libm::log`, rounded to hundredths and clamped to 0.10 to 4.00; `m′` within one grey of 0 or 1 gives 1.00. Black and white aims at the Otsu level the same way. "k x Interval" fields use the evaluated Interval in µm (criterion 40). A preset reads no power, speed or material set.
6. **Effective steps** (`resolve(picture registers, interval, frame, stored results) -> Vec<PixelStep>`). Expert: the enabled manual steps. Another preset: the geometry and mask zones of the manual stack, plus the preset's derived tone steps in place of the manual tone steps (criterion 30). One resolution per (picture, Interval): each Image operation resolves with its own Interval (criterion 60); the Select view, the canvas look and Make editable use the first assigned Image operation in the Operations list order, else 0.10 mm. A clone resolves from its adjustment source (ADR 0072 §2) and its own operation's Interval (criterion 61). A manual stack without a preset resolves the same at every Interval, so its adjusted image is shared.
7. **Derived, cached by content** (criterion 18). Output `Adjusted::Rgba(Arc<WorkingImage>) | Adjusted::Grey(Arc<GreyPlane>)`. An empty resolution is `Rgba` of the **same `Arc`** as today, so the empty stack is byte-identical with `0123` and costs nothing. The session (`curvyo-editor-wasm`, beside the working-image cache) keys adjusted images by `AdjustKey { asset, steps: Vec<PixelStep> }` (stored results by their hash), evicting least recently used past 256 MiB of adjusted pixels (Proposal); the ADR 0068 §3 grey pyramid is keyed by `AdjustKey` instead of the asset hash, and the job fingerprint of ADR 0069 §9 gains it. Nothing derived enters the document or the history. An unreadable asset keeps the stack and the `0123` warning (criterion 21).
8. **Before and after** (criteria 11 to 13). The After pane draws the job's sweeps of that one item from the ADR 0069 §5 draw cache, so its dots are the Toolpath view's and the file's; Before draws the working image texture; both panes share one camera (render-core gains a second viewport, no new pipeline). While a field or the Strength is held, the session resolves on the mip level that fits the pane, with pixel parameters scaled by 2^−n, and dithers only the visible part on the `k·I` preview grid of ADR 0069 §5; on release the ADR 0069 §12 resumable loop builds the exact sweeps.
9. **Memory** (criterion 56, confirmed by a different means). Each step transforms one owned buffer in place, with a ring of at most its kernel height in rows (blur, bilateral) or one table per tile (CLAHE); the run holds the source `Arc` plus one buffer, so peak memory is about 2 x the working image for an RGBA chain and 1.5 x once grey. No row-band streaming across steps (the CLAHE tiles and large blur radii need whole planes). A step whose buffer cannot be allocated is skipped with the check warning of ADR 0072 §5.
10. **Budgets** (criterion 55, thread CPU, native, `curvyo-editor-wasm/tests/cpu_time/`) **confirmed** as written: on 4 Mpx each of Channel mix, Levels, Curves, Crop, Resize at most 40 ms, Sharpen 120 ms, Local contrast 400 ms, Denoise 800 ms, the whole stack 1.2 s; analysis 150 ms; a Strength drag 100 ms on the reduced view; the reduced Before and after 100 ms on a 2 Mpx mip. The PR states the wasm numbers.
11. **wasm.** image-core and laser-core stay on the wasm32 list; no new third-party crate (`libm` is locked); the job stays pixel-free (`0029` criterion 39).

## Consequences

- Every golden dot map of `0123` stays valid (empty stack); any change to a constant above changes the adjustment goldens and is reviewed as such.
- A picture in two operations at two Intervals costs two adjusted images and two grey pyramids in memory.
- The canvas shows the adjusted image of the first Image operation; on a drawing page with no operation it is the 0.10 mm resolution.
- The later trace story can take the same `Adjusted` as its input (spec Links); nothing here blocks it.
- For the PO: Levels Gamma uses the `0123` convention (criterion 32's formula is inverted to match); `denoise_nlm` becomes `denoise_bilateral`; criterion 56's row bands become decision 9's in-place rule with the same bound; the cache key is content, not revision.
