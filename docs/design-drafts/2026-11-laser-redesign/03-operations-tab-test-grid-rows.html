03 · Operations tab: a 5 × 5 test as 26 ordinary rows

The test grid resolves into ordinary operations: one Engrave for all its text (the id, axis titles and values) and one per cell, 25 for a 5 × 5 grid. They sit in the flat, dense list like every other row: same row, same Show, Output, armed trash, arrows, rename. A press on a row selects it and unfolds it directly under it; one row is unfolded at a time, so the tab never fills up. The unfolded row holds two parts: what the material's set gives (material, power, speed, passes, interval for Engrave) and, under a hairline, what belongs to the operation alone (Crosshatch, Bidirectional fill, Scan angle, Overscanning, Z offset; customer). The Add buttons are gone from this tab (screen 01); the view switch moved to the page label on the canvas (screen 01 C), so only Cuts last stays above the list.
