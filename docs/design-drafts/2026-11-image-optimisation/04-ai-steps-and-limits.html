04 · AI steps (slice 3): Run, the one-time model download, stored results and their limits

An AI step is a step row like any other, with one more thing in its body: the Run button (the Apply look) and one state line under it. Nothing runs while editing; the first Run of a model asks once, in place, before anything is downloaded. All states are words in ink or muted ink, the warn triangle of the checks, refusals in the field-invalid red. Notes: ux-notes.md section 8.
