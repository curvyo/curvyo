05 · A linked clone with its own adjustments, the Image row after Frage 283 B, the test grid's Strength axis, the checks

The clone uses the override row of 0057 (a third row, Adjustments: Original's | Own), not a new line and button; the picture's Dither is part of its adjustments. Each picture burns with its own dither (Frage 283 B, 2026-11-20): the Image operation row keeps Interval, Scan angle, Bidirectional fill and Overscanning, and shows its own nine-mode Dither strip only while it holds a gradient fill (a gradient-filled shape or a test-grid cell). The Image test grid can engrave the selected picture with a Strength axis. Notes: ux-notes.md sections 9 to 11.
