01 · The picture's own Adjustments: Preset, Dither, Strength, Make editable

A selected image shows its Adjustments in its own Style tab (the object's settings, like stroke and fill for a shape); the picture also carries its own Dither (Frage 283 B, 2026-11-20): the nine-mode strip sits right under the Preset strip, so a preset pick visibly presses its dither and nothing above Strength moves; the mode's fields (Threshold, Lowest power, Cell size or Line pitch and Angle) come after Strength. Column 256 px. Preset is a fixed two-row choice strip (no list that opens); every cell's tooltip names the dither it sets. Strength is the one slider in the app's panels, with its percent field.
