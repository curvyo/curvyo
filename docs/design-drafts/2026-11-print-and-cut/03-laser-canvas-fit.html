Scale 2 px per mm; the bed is 300 × 200 mm, the sheet a 120 × 90 mm placed Drawing page with a Cut on its outline. The marks of a placed sheet never draw as artwork on a laser page: faint crosses in --reg-mark (--guide at 60 %), number tags in the guide green, not hit, not in the job. Measured positions are --accent rings with a centre dot, tags on the other side. Nothing here is a new colour.
