03 · Air assist, the fixture material, after Export

Air assist is a machine field (Document tab, Machine block). Today its first press inserts the "Differs from profile" row above the fields, so every row below moves 36 px and the second press lands on Motorised Z axis: the switch looks inoperable. The fix keeps the state row's place. The fixture jobs take their numbers from one material picked in the Fixture jobs section of the Operations tab. Export opens the save dialog; after the write the fixture jobs are switched off in the same undo step "Export job file".
