01 · Toolpath view and the play bar

A second toggle at the page name (symbol Route, tooltip "Toolpath view", dashed outline = new) draws the job instead of the drawing: burn moves in the look of their kind (Cut solid red, Score dotted ink, Engrave scan lines in pale orange), travel as a thin dashed ink line, a numbered ring at the start of each operation (its place in the run order, the same number as in the Output tab) and a small arrowhead at the start of each burn path. Below the page a bar with Play, the one slider of the app and the readout. No new colour; nothing is told by colour alone.
