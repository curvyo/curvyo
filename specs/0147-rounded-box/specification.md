# Rounded box: a box with rounded corners or a curved top whose walls bend by laser-cut slits (living hinge), as a generator

Status: Slice 1 Done (PR #465); slices 2 and 3 Draft (revision 6, 2026-11-24; the customer answered Fragen 349 B, 355 C, 360 B, 361 A and 362 B; Fragen 348, 350, 351, 352 and 353 stand at their defaults A; ADR 0082 revision 2 is in; `ux-notes.md` revision 3 is in and its two questions are decided as Proposals (resize keeps the radius, label "Wall thickness"); **Frage 363 default A: slice 1 ships without Detach part** (parts share one operation, Object to path gives separate plates, no format change); Detach part is slice 3 with the file-format change and needs the customer's acceptance; slices 2 and 3 stay Draft)
Priority: Should
Origin: Customer (2026-11-24, with an addition the same day: inset base, curved top, lid seat, finger hollow, standalone pattern later; translated, after trying `0141`): a second box type, built as a separate generator: a box with rounded corners that bends by laser-cut slits; reference images of rounded-rectangle and oval bodies with slit-pattern walls, finger-jointed or slotted lid and base, also variants with a rolling slotted lid, a curved front and partly slotted walls. "The box generator gets real life." Everything marked **(Proposal)** is the product owner's and vetoable at the demo.

## User value

As a maker I want to type "rounded box 120 x 80 x 50 mm, corner radius 20 mm, 3 mm plywood" and get a cut-ready wall strip that bends round the corners, plus lid and base, so that I make soft-cornered boxes, pen cups and organisers without drawing hundreds of slits by hand.

**What other tools do.** Boxes.py (FlexBox family: FlexBox, FlexBox2 to 6, with slit patterns and rounded corners; web form, one-shot SVG; the slit pattern and spacing are fixed per type); MakerCase (no flexible walls, finger-jointed boxes only); Inkscape "Tabbed Box Maker" (straight finger-jointed boxes, no bending); LightBurn: no box generator (a Radial or Grid array of slits by hand). We do better: a live object (`0141`) kept in the document, with the slit width and spacing derived from the material thickness and corner radius, so a change of radius or thickness re-lays every slit, and the lid and base are generated to the same outline.

## Relation to neighbouring specs

- **`0141` parametric-generators:** the Rounded box (first called Flex box) is **another generator through the same contract** (ADR 0080: a leaf `shape = "generated"`, kind `rounded-box`, stored outline, opens as stored, proportional resize writes parameters, Object to path, sliders in the Generator block as of revision 2). It is a separate generator and a separate segment in the Generators tool, not a mode of the finger-joint Box (customer-decided 2026-11-24). It reuses the finger-joint code of `0141` for lid and base where the lid is fingered.
- **`0101` laser operations:** slits are Cut, outlines Cut; an optional Score for fold lines is out of scope.

## Slices

- **Slice 1 (Ready under the default of Frage 363 A): vertical corners with slits.** Criteria 1 to 10 and 12 to 13 (criterion 14 is later, criterion 15 is slice 3). A box with rounded vertical corners up to an exact circle; the five parts (strip, inner base, base cover, seat ring, lid), each with its own thickness; Straight, Offset and Wave slits; the box as one group-like unit whose parts share one operation (no Detach part, no format change); automatic slit values; Top shape is not in this slice. The customer can try it end to end on the laser. Needs `0141` revision 2 (corner rule, ADR 0082 §1) first, as the first commits of the same branch.
- **Slice 3 (Draft, needs the customer's acceptance): Detach part** (criterion 15): a part gets its own operation and stays part of the box. It changes the file format (ADR 0082 §10: part keys, `format_version` bump, older Curvyo versions refuse such files), so it is its own slice (Frage 363).
- **Slice 2 (Draft): curved top** (criterion 11, Frage 353 A), built from the same modules; the footprint corners are square in that mode.
- **Later** (not slices of this story): more slit patterns (the customer: "there is even more to come"; the pattern list is open for new entries, criterion 2), rolling lid, curved front, partly slotted wall (Frage 352), rounded footprint plus curved top as two strips, the standalone pattern `0148`, a box from any closed path `0149`.

## Acceptance criteria (as far as clear)

1. Given the Generators tool, then Rounded box (kind id `rounded-box`) is a third segment next to Box and Gear; pressing it places a generated object at the pointer with its defaults, selected, parameters in the Properties panel, one undo step "Add rounded box".
2. Given a rounded box, then its parameters are (all sliders, `0141` criterion 9): Inner width, Depth, Height (mm, the inside of the wall), Corner radius (mm; at least 2 times the wall thickness; the Wall thickness itself is clamped to a sixth of the smaller inside side, with a notice naming the field and the limit, so at least one tab window fits, **Proposal**, revision 6; at most half the smaller footprint side; **at the maximum the footprint is a circle (equal sides) or a stadium, no special case, Frage 360 B**: no notice, no refusal), **one thickness field per part (Frage 355 C): Wall thickness (strip), Inner base thickness, Cover thickness, Ring thickness, Lid thickness**, each defaulting to the Wall thickness and then shown muted as following it (typing overrides, as the other automatic fields), Kerf compensation (mm, default 0), Slit pattern (Straight, Offset, Wave; Frage 349 B; further patterns are later entries through the same list), Wall slits (Corners only (default), All round), Slit width, Slit spacing and Slit rows (all three **automatic by default**: a stored 0 means automatic and the block shows the used value muted; typing overrides; Frage 350 A), Base inset (mm, default 0, criterion 10), Base cover (on), Lid (on or off), Lid seat (on: ring plus loose lid; off: tabbed lid), Finger hollow (on or off, Width, Depth; criterion 13), Layout (flat; Gap). Top shape arrives in slice 2 (criterion 11). **No Finger width and no Clearance field** (derived: 2 times the wall thickness, 0; **Proposal**, they join a Joints group if the customer wants them).
3. Given the wall, then it is **one strip outline** (the developed outer boundary with its two butt ends in the middle of the longer straight (with no straight, as in a circle, at the middle of the back side between two tabs, ADR 0082 §5), the slits as holes and edge notches) whose length equals the footprint perimeter on the neutral line, `2(w - 2r) + 2(d - 2r) + pi (2r + t)`, within 0.1 mm, less the shortening of each flat tab window on a bent span (ADR 0082 §5: the window lies on the strip as its inner chord, so a circle's strip is shorter than the formula by, per window, the inner arc over the window minus its chord (the inner face follows the plate outline, the bends still add pi t); small footprints carry fewer windows than ADR 0082 §4 lists so each curved stretch keeps two slit columns; **Proposal**, revision 6); the slits are **only in the four corner arcs** by default (at the circle the whole wall is arc) and may be extended over the whole wall (Wall slits All round; **Proposal**); straight wall parts stay solid for text or a hole (reference images 31 and 32).
4. Given the slit pattern, then slits run parallel to the bend axis (vertical when the box stands) and **no band of material runs uncut across a bend**: slits run out through the top or bottom edge (alternating, the strip holds through the bridges of one wall thickness), because an uncut edge margin would crack the wood (strain thickness / (2 x bend radius), about 10 % at 3 mm and radius 15 against 1 to 2 % for wood; ADR 0082 §6; this replaces "stop one thickness from the edge"). A slit is a narrow closed slot (Frage 361 A) or an edge notch; the Wave pattern (reference image 33) is a sinusoidal slot of the same width whose ends run out through the edges alternately, with the same no-uncut-band test; the automatic width is **kerf plus 0.3 mm** (drawn 0.3 mm at kerf 0; it replaces Frage 350's "kerf plus 0.1 mm", because a closed slot is drawn at its width minus the kerf), at least kerf plus 0.2 mm, spacing at least slit width plus 1 mm (automatic: 0.22 times the corner radius); a corner radius below 2 times the wall thickness is clamped with a status notice naming the field and the limit (`0141` criterion 4). Tests: at the defaults every slot lies inside the strip, no two contours are closer than the kerf, **every line of constant height across the strip on each bent span meets at least one slot or edge notch** (no uncut band), and the strip length matches criterion 3.
5. Given the plates, then they are generated to the **footprint outline** and joined to the strip by **tab and slot**: the inner base, the seat ring and (with Lid seat off) the tabbed lid carry tabs (width 2 times the plate's own thickness, length the wall thickness, flush outside) that engage slots of the strip; on a straight never in the first or last piece (the strip owns the arcs: one owner per corner, ADR 0082 §1), and **on a footprint without a straight (exact circle) at slit-free spots of the arc, placement the architect's call, ADR 0082 amendment (Frage 360 B)**; a slot at the strip's edge is a finger notch. The mating test also runs at the exact circle and with five different part thicknesses. The strip has no joint at its seam. A test mates every plate with the strip at the given thickness and asserts the tab centres and the slot centres agree and the engaged edges lie on one line within the kerf value (as `0141` criterion 3); **on an arc the tab stands on a flat window, so the test allows the window sag l²/8r plus the kerf, not the kerf alone** (ADR 0082 §5). The base takes the inner base as in criterion 10, no separate base control.
6. Given any parameter change, then the paths regenerate within 100 ms of thread CPU time for a box of 400 slots; invalid and dependent values are clamped with a status notice, never an error dialog.
7. Given a rounded box, then it moves, rotates, aligns like any object, resizes **proportionally only** with the handle changing Inner width, Depth and Height (Corner radius, thicknesses, Kerf, slit values, Gap stay, so the slit count follows the new perimeter; a larger full-round box therefore becomes a rounded rectangle and a smaller one stays full round by the clamp (**Proposal**, UX notes question 9 A)), and operations of `0101` assign to it. Object to path turns it into the group of its parts as ordinary paths in one undo step; the parts come out as separate plates; the grouping of parts and Detach part are criterion 15 (Frage 362 B, slice 3).
8. Given save and reopen, then the kind, parameters and outline survive and the file opens exactly as stored (ADR 0080, Frage 326 A); an unknown kind opens with the stored outline, as in `0141`; `format_version` is unchanged in slices 1 and 2 (ADR 0082: the new kind is additive, slots are holes of the strip part); slice 3 (Detach part) bumps it.
9. Given the first build, then **Straight and Offset slits, the corners-only and the all-round wall, the seat lid (ring plus loose lid with finger hollow) and the tabbed lid, and the base with and without cover and inset** exist; the rolling slotted lid, the curved front and the partly slotted wall are later entries (Frage 352) and must be expressible without a new generator.
10. **(Customer addition 2026-11-24, clarified by the exploded drawings 34 and 35)** Given the base, then it is **two plates**: an **inner base** seated inside the strip (tabs into the strip's slots, criterion 5) and an **outer cover plate** under the strip with a plain rounded outline of the footprint plus the wall thickness, flush with the strip's end; each has its own thickness field (Inner base thickness, Cover thickness, Frage 355 C). **Base inset (mm, a slider, default 0; Proposal, ADR 0082)**: the inner base lies directly on the cover at 0; a larger value raises it inside the wall (the customer's inset base); a value between 0 and one wall thickness is clamped to one wall thickness with a notice (the bridge to the edge must hold). Base cover off = inner base only, flush. A test asserts the base slots lie Base inset from the strip's edge within the kerf value, the cover has no tabs and its outline is the strip's outer outline within the kerf value.
11. **(Customer addition; slice 2)** Given Top shape (Flat by default, or Curved; Frage 353 A), then a Curved top bends the strip over the top: the strip runs from the lid's free front edge over the top, round the back arc (the slit hinge, curve radius equal to Corner radius) and down the back (reference image 4, "round hinge"). The two end plates carry the profile (depth x height, back-top corner rounded) and hold the strip with tabs on the back straight; the base and the front are finger-jointed plates like the Box; the plates' top edges are the lid seat. **The footprint corners are square in this mode** (a strip cannot bend about two axes; rounded corners plus a curved top are two strips, later). The slit rules of criterion 4 apply to the curved arc.
12. **(Customer addition, clarified)** Given Lid seat on, then the lid side has a **seat ring** (Deckelauflage): the inner footprint with tabs into the strip's slots (flush with the strip's top edge) and an opening that is the footprint offset inward by the ring width (2 times Ring thickness; Ring thickness default the wall thickness, Frage 355 C); the loose lid rests on the ring. A test asserts the ring's top is flush with the strip's top edge and its opening equals the footprint offset by the ring width, both within the kerf value, and its tabs engage as in criterion 5.
13. **(Customer addition, clarified)** Given the lid with Lid seat on, then it is a plain plate of the outer outline lying on the strip's top edge and the ring, with a Finger hollow (on or off, Width and Depth sliders, default on **(Proposal)**, as the drawings show it): a U notch centred in the front straight's edge so the lid can be lifted; a test asserts the hollow lies inside the lid outline and leaves the lid in one piece. With Lid seat off the lid is a tabbed plate as criterion 5; with Lid off no lid part exists.
14. **(Customer addition, LATER)** The bending pattern also as a standalone tool for **any outline** (pick a path, get a slit pattern along it) is explicitly later: it is the draft idea `0148` `slit-bend-pattern` in the README (cf. `0071` tiling), not part of this story. A whole box from any closed path is the draft `0149` `path-box`, which builds on this story and on `0148`.
15. **(Customer, Frage 362 B, 2026-11-24; slice 3, not in slice 1 under the default of Frage 363 A)** Given a rounded box, then it is **a group-like unit**: its parts (strip, inner base, cover, ring, lid) are shown and selected as one object and **by default share one operation** (`0101`), set on the box (slice 1 ships this half: one operation for all parts; Object to path gives separate plates and no format change). A part can be **detached** (Detach part, selected inside the box, one undo step "Detach part"): it gets its own operation (and so its own sheet, speed and power) but **stays a part of the box**: it still regenerates with the parameters, moves with the box and keeps its place in the layout; "the single parts still belong together". Attach part undoes it. A test asserts: a new box has one operation for all parts; after Detach part on the lid the lid carries its own operation, a thickness change still regenerates it, and moving the box moves it; save and reopen keeps both. How the one-object model (ADR 0080, leaf `shape = "generated"`) carries per-part operations is the architect's call (ADR 0082 §10: part keys, `member.<node>#<part>`; it **bumps `format_version`**, so older Curvyo versions refuse a file holding a detached part; **Proposal** for the exact wording of the UI: Detach part in the part's context menu and a row in the Generator block; which operation Detach part writes, a copy of the box's or a picked one, is set in the UX notes revision 3).


## Out of scope

- The standalone slit pattern for any outline (criterion 14).
- A rolling (tambour) lid, a sloping front, a partly slotted wall with a free choice of which sides, hinged or sliding lids with their rails: later entries through the same contract (Frage 352).
- Dividers, compartments, handles, thumb holes, text on the wall, assembly drawing or 3D preview, bend simulation.
- Per-part operations beyond Detach part (grouping several detached parts, per-part layers), and a separate object per material.
- Material databases (recommended slit pitch per material) and automatic kerf from the laser.
- Rounding the corners of the finger-joint Box of `0141` (customer-decided: separate generators).

## Open questions for the customer (all with defaults)

- **Frage 348 (FLEX-RADIUS):** the corner radius default and range. A: default 15 mm, minimum 2 times the thickness, maximum half the smaller footprint side (stadium or circle at the maximum). B: radius as a fraction of the shorter side (30 %). C: free numeric only. **Recommendation and default: A.**
- **Frage 349 (FLEX-PATTERN):** slit pattern kinds at the first build. A: Straight and Offset (two rows in a brick pattern, the usual look of images 29 to 31). B: A plus Wave (sinusoidal slits, image 33). C: Straight only. **ANSWERED B (customer, 2026-11-24, translated): Straight, Offset and Wave; "there is even more to come"** (more patterns are later entries through the same list).
- **Frage 350 (FLEX-SLITS):** slit width and spacing. A: derived: width = kerf plus 0.3 mm (drawn 0.3 mm; revised from 0.1 mm by ADR 0082), spacing 0.22 times the corner radius, both overridable (automatic marker). B: fixed typed values only. C: a material list. **Recommendation and default: A.**
- **Frage 351 (FLEX-LID):** lid types at the first build. A: the seat lid (ring plus loose lid with finger hollow) and the tabbed lid (tabs into the strip), plus None (open box); revised by ADR 0082 from Fingered/Slotted. B: A plus a lid that sits on top without joint. C: tabbed lid only. **Recommendation and default: A.**
- **Frage 353 (ROUND-TOP):** the curved top. A: a Top shape choice, Flat or Curved, with the curve radius equal to the corner radius (one radius field). B: a separate Top radius field. C: later entry. **Recommendation and default: A.**
- **Frage 354 (ROUND-SEAT): superseded 2026-11-24** by the frame ring of criterion 12 (a ring with tabs, not a ledge); no longer to be asked.
- **Frage 352 (FLEX-PLATES):** which plates the first build outputs. A: the five parts: strip, base inner plate, base cover plate, frame ring, lid plate (laid out flat with a Gap: strip in the first row, the four others below it in the order of the drawings). B: A plus the options for the reference variants (rolling lid, curved front, partly slotted wall) in the first slice. C: strip only. **Recommendation and default: A.**
- **Frage 355 (ROUND-THICK):** thickness per part. A: the inner plates and the ring have their own thickness fields, default equal to the wall thickness; the cover and lid take the Material thickness. B: all parts use one Material thickness. C: every part its own field. **ANSWERED C (customer, 2026-11-24): every part gets its own thickness field** (criterion 2).

- **Frage 360 (ROUND-CIRCLE):** a fully round box. A: the corner radius stops where the longer side still holds tabs (a stadium is fine, an exact circle is not; a notice says so). B: also tabs in curved parts at slit-free spots (a later slice). **ANSWERED B (customer, 2026-11-24, translated): an exact circle must be possible, a square with its corners rounded completely (radius up to half the side), no special case.** Tabs on arcs at slit-free spots follow (criterion 5), no longer a later slice.
- **Frage 361 (ROUND-SLOT):** slits as narrow slots or single lines. A: each slit is a narrow closed slot (0.3 mm drawn), works in every build and file, but the laser cuts round each slit (about twice the cutting time of a line). B: single lines, faster, but the file format changes and older Curvyo versions cannot open such a box. **ANSWERED A (customer, 2026-11-24): narrow closed slots.**
- **Frage 362 (ROUND-SHEETS):** parts of different materials. A: one object, parts laid out in rows by thickness; to cut each on its own sheet use Object to path and move the rows. B: one object per material with its own laser settings (a contract change, later). **ANSWERED B, reshaped (customer, 2026-11-24, translated): parts can have their own operations, but by default all together; \"for single parts the grouping has to be dissolved; the single parts still belong together\"** (criterion 15: the box is a group-like unit, a part can be detached to its own operation).
- **Frage 363 (ROUND-DETACH):** when a part gets its own operation (criterion 15; a detached part stays a part of the box: it regenerates, moves with it and keeps its layout place). A (recommended, default): **slice 1 ships without Detach part**: all parts share one operation set on the box; for separate sheets, speed or power use Object to path (separate plates, no format change); Detach part follows as its own later slice (slice 3), with the file-format change (a detached part is stored under a part key and `format_version` goes up; older Curvyo versions then cannot open such a file) and your acceptance of it. B: Detach part in slice 1, with the format change now (a file with a detached part does not open in older Curvyo versions). C: Object to path only, Detach part is never built (no format change, ever). **Recommendation and default: A** (the customer can try the box on the laser without waiting for the format decision).
- **Frage 356 to 359:** unused (left free for this spec by ADR 0082).

## Decisions

Customer 2026-11-24: a separate generator from the finger-joint Box; the additions of criteria 10 to 14. Product owner (all **(Proposal)**): the slit width rule (Frage 350 A, kerf plus 0.3 mm), Base inset slider, no Finger width and Clearance fields. Revision 2 (2026-11-24, from the architect's eight spec issues in `adrs.md`): criterion 4 slits run out through the edges (no uncut band); criteria 5 and 9 are tab and slot, not fingered or slotted lid; Base inset added; slit width kerf plus 0.3 mm; an exact circle is not built (stadium yes, Frage 360 A); curved top has square footprint corners and is slice 2; Slit rows is an automatic count; Fragen 360 to 362 added. Revision 6 (2026-11-24, lead, **Proposal**): criterion 3 counts the flat tab windows of ADR 0082 §5 in the strip length; criterion 2 clamps the Wall thickness to a sixth of the smaller inside side. Revision 5 (2026-11-24, lead decisions, all **Proposal**, vetoable at the demo): a resize keeps the Corner radius even at full round (UX question 9 A, criterion 7); the strip's thickness field is labelled "Wall thickness" everywhere (UX question 10, criterion 2); status aligned with `ux-notes.md` revision 3. Revision 3 (customer answers 2026-11-24): Wave in slice 1 (349 B); one thickness field per part (355 C); an exact circle is built, no notice, tabs on arcs (360 B); slits stay closed slots (361 A); the box is a group-like unit with Detach part instead of one object per material (362 B, criterion 15, Frage 363); defaults of 348, 350, 351, 352 and 353 stand.
