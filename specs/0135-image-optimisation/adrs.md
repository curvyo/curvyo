# ADRs for image optimisation

Status: Proposed (architect, 2026-11-19). Six new ADRs, 0072 to 0077. Slices 1 and 2 need [ADR 0072](../../docs/adr/0072-image-adjustments-on-the-image-object.md) (`needs-customer`: document model, file format; accepted with the slice 1 PR), [ADR 0073](../../docs/adr/0073-image-adjustment-pipeline-and-presets.md) and [ADR 0074](../../docs/adr/0074-halftone-dots-and-line-screen-dither-modes.md) (architect). Slice 3 is not Ready before [ADR 0075](../../docs/adr/0075-on-device-image-models-runtime-and-crate.md), [ADR 0076](../../docs/adr/0076-model-download-host-and-the-one-network-use.md) and [ADR 0077](../../docs/adr/0077-derived-image-assets-in-the-project-file.md) are accepted (all `needs-customer`) and its step 0 (ADR 0075 §7) has measured the models. Decisions marked **(Proposal)** are the architect's and vetoable at the demo. Two customer questions: Fragen 280 and 281 (below). Fragen 277 to 279 are taken at their default A.

- [ADR 0072](../../docs/adr/0072-image-adjustments-on-the-image-object.md): registers `image_preset`, `image_strength`, `image_adjustments` (one atomic list, integer fields with units in the key) on the image node; `own_adjustments` marker plus the same registers on a clone (ADR 0016 pattern, adjustment source = nearest own on the chain); crop is a step and the drawn frame is the placement's visible part (placement and trace mask unchanged); merge, damage, history; dither values `halftone`, `line_screen` with `screen_size_mm`, `screen_angle_deg`; test-grid recipe `picture` and the `strength` axis, material record format 4; one `format_version` per slice (51 for slice 1 if nothing merges first).
- [ADR 0073](../../docs/adr/0073-image-adjustment-pipeline-and-presets.md): integer pixel steps and the analysis in `curvyo-image-core` (new edge `→ libm`); stored fields, presets, Strength and the mm-to-pixel resolution in `curvyo-laser-core`; three-zone pipeline order; three-box blur, CLAHE, bilateral denoise, DoG contours; presets quantised to stored units (Make editable is byte-exact); one adjusted image per picture and Interval, cached by content; Before and after from the job's sweeps; in-place memory rule; budgets of criterion 55 confirmed.
- [ADR 0074](../../docs/adr/0074-halftone-dots-and-line-screen-dither-modes.md): Halftone dots and Line screen as threshold tables of a rotated cell grid anchored at the item, independent of the scan angle; integer cos/sin table.
- [ADR 0075](../../docs/adr/0075-on-device-image-models-runtime-and-crate.md): new crate `curvyo-imageai-core` on `rten` (already locked and in the editor wasm via `vectrace`), never a dependency of laser-core or image-core; U²-Netp (Apache-2.0, 4.7 MB), Real-ESRGAN general x4v3 (BSD-3-Clause, 4.9 MB), SCUNet real PSNR (Apache-2.0 / MIT, 72 MB; FFDNet MIT 3.4 MB as fallback); our ONNX conversion with pinned hashes in `models.toml`; dedicated worker; security; step 0 measurements.
- [ADR 0076](../../docs/adr/0076-model-download-host-and-the-one-network-use.md): the frontend's `fetch` in both builds, a fixed URL with no credentials, referrer, query or body; verify before store; desktop data folder or IndexedDB; consent per model per device; models never synced; host choice Frage 280 (default bundling).
- [ADR 0077](../../docs/adr/0077-derived-image-assets-in-the-project-file.md): `assets/derived/<sha256>.png` as a second asset class; the step's `result` record with model, version and input hash; integer application of the stored result; Out of date keeps burning, missing does not burn; pruning at save to what the tree or this session refers to; 64 MiB and 512 MiB confirmed, separate from the 512 MiB of pictures and fonts.
- Extended, not replaced: [ADR 0016](../../docs/adr/0016-clone-overrides.md) §1 to §5 (third override), [ADR 0017](../../docs/adr/0017-image-nodes-and-embedded-assets.md) §1, §2, §4 (visible frame, derived class, working limit), [ADR 0045](../../docs/adr/0045-clones-of-groups-texts-images-and-movable-placed-pages.md) §1 (image roots), [ADR 0057](../../docs/adr/0057-test-grid-object-with-derived-operations.md) §1 and [ADR 0058](../../docs/adr/0058-material-records-shared-across-lasers.md) §2 (recipe, record format 4), [ADR 0068](../../docs/adr/0068-raster-dot-values-and-dithering.md) §3, §5 (grey pyramid keyed by the adjusted image, two modes), [ADR 0069](../../docs/adr/0069-raster-rows-output-and-settings.md) §5, §7 to §9, §12, [ADR 0028](../../docs/adr/0028-ocr-text-in-trace.md) §4 and [ADR 0011](../../docs/adr/0011-workspace-and-crate-layout.md) §2, §6 (new crate, the `rayon` check), [ADR 0061](../../docs/adr/0061-image-trace-mask-in-the-project-file.md) §1 (the mask stays in the whole picture's square).

## Feature-local decisions

- 2026-11-19: **Linked clones of images already exist** (ADR 0045, spec `0100`). The spec's "amends `0049`, which allows only paths as origins" is done; only the third override is new. **For the PO:** criterion 9 and "Changes to accepted specs".
- 2026-11-19: **Own adjustments are a marker plus the ordinary registers on the clone** (ADR 0072 option A), not one nested `own_adjustments` value. All or nothing still holds (absent registers read as defaults). **For the PO:** "File format notes".
- 2026-11-19: **Crop keeps `image_placement`** (ADR 0072 §3): the stored frame stays the whole picture, the drawn frame is its cropped part. The maker sees what criterion 5 says (the visible part keeps its size and place, the object's box shrinks). Nothing for the PO.
- 2026-11-19: **One format number per slice** (ADR 0072 §8), not one for the story: each slice is its own PR and a build of the previous number would burn other dots. **For the PO:** criterion 8 and "File format notes". **For the lead:** three rows in the `format_version` table of `specs/README.md` (slice 1 next free after 50; slices 2 and 3 next free at their merge).
- 2026-11-19: **Material record format 4** for the picture source and the Strength axis in the test recipe (ADR 0072 §6): the recipe is also the material test record's (ADR 0069 §8). The Image set itself stores nothing new (criterion 52 holds). Place on page of a picture test from Materials needs a selected image, else it places the Ramp without the Strength axis (Proposal). **For the PO:** criterion 51.
- 2026-11-19: **The runtime sits in a `-core` crate** (ADR 0075, crate option A), against criterion 50's "a `*-core` crate never contains the runtime". Inference is pure compute with bytes in, as ADR 0028 decided for OCR; what the criterion protects (the job never needs a model) is held by the rule that laser-core and image-core never depend on `curvyo-imageai-core`, checked in CI. **For the PO:** criterion 50.
- 2026-11-19: **Levels Gamma uses the `0123` convention** (above 1 lightens), so criterion 32's `ln(target) / ln(median)` becomes `ln(median) / ln(target)` with the same result (ADR 0073 §5). **For the PO:** criterion 32.
- 2026-11-19: **Denoise is a bilateral filter** (ADR 0073 §4); non-local means misses the 800 ms budget more than tenfold. Golden `denoise_bilateral` instead of `denoise_nlm`. **For the PO:** criteria 5 and 54.
- 2026-11-19: **Memory** (criterion 56): in-place steps with the source plus one buffer, about 2 x the working image, instead of row bands (ADR 0073 §9). **For the PO:** criterion 56.
- 2026-11-19: **A missing stored result does not burn the picture** (ADR 0077 §4, Proposal); Out of date still burns the stored result. **For the PO:** criterion 66's "not burnable until Run" read as "the item is skipped with a warning".
- 2026-11-19: **Upscale x2** is our integer 2 x 2 box of the x4 model's tiles (one model, ADR 0075); the stored result is that x2 picture.
- 2026-11-19: **Screen fields are shared** (`screen_size_mm`, `screen_angle_deg`) by Halftone dots and Line screen; switching mode keeps them (ADR 0072 §6). The `0123` damaged golden's `dither: "halftone"` case moves to `"halftone_x"`.
- 2026-11-19: **Cache by content, not revision** (ADR 0073 §7). Two clones, two operations at one Interval and an undo share one adjusted image. **For the PO:** criterion 18 wording.
- 2026-11-19: **Debt entries for the lead** (this agent edits only ADR files and this file): the whole asset store in wasm memory with up to 1 GiB of assets (ADR 0077 §6, trigger: a measured out-of-memory); the model host as an operating duty if Frage 280 is A.

## Frage 280 (IMGOPT-MODEL-HOST): Where the AI models are downloaded from

The three models (about 82 MB together) are not shipped with the app (Frage 274 B). Curvyo needs a place to download them from. The file is checked against a fingerprint built into the app, so the place cannot slip in a wrong file; it only sees that someone downloaded it.
- **A** Our own address, e.g. `models.<your domain>`: a storage bucket behind your domain (for example Cloudflare R2, free downloads). You need a domain and upload the files once per model version.
- **B** A Hugging Face organisation "curvyo": free, nothing to run, but the downloads come from a third party's servers.
- **C** No download: the models come with the app (installer and web app about 82 MB larger, or 13 MB with the smaller denoise model). No network use at all on the desktop.

Recommendation: **A** (what you asked for in Frage 274). Default if unanswered: **C** until a host exists; switching later changes no project file.

## Frage 281 (IMGOPT-MODEL-DATA): Accept models trained on research datasets

The three models' code and weights are under permissive licences (Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, MIT; checked 2026-11-19). As for the text reader in Trace (Frage 112), their makers trained them on public research picture collections whose own terms are "research use" or not itemised.
- **A** Accept, as for Frage 112; recorded in the model list.
- **B** Only models whose training pictures are themselves freely licensed: no suitable models exist today, so slice 3 waits.
- **C** Drop the AI tools (slice 3) for now.

Recommendation: **A**. Default if unanswered: **A** (nothing is shipped before ADR 0075 is accepted).
