# ADRs for image optimisation

Status: Proposed (architect, 2026-11-19). Six new ADRs, 0072 to 0077. Slices 1 and 2 need [ADR 0072](../../docs/adr/0072-image-adjustments-on-the-image-object.md) (`needs-customer`: document model, file format; accepted with the slice 1 PR), [ADR 0073](../../docs/adr/0073-image-adjustment-pipeline-and-presets.md) and [ADR 0074](../../docs/adr/0074-halftone-dots-and-line-screen-dither-modes.md) (architect). Slice 3 is not Ready before [ADR 0075](../../docs/adr/0075-on-device-image-models-runtime-and-crate.md), [ADR 0076](../../docs/adr/0076-model-download-host-and-the-one-network-use.md) and [ADR 0077](../../docs/adr/0077-derived-image-assets-in-the-project-file.md) are accepted (all `needs-customer`) and its step 0 (ADR 0075 §7) has measured the models. Decisions marked **(Proposal)** are the architect's and vetoable at the demo. Fragen 277 to 279 are taken at their default A; Fragen 280 A and 281 A were answered by the customer on 2026-11-19 (below). One new customer question: Frage 282 (below), needed before slice 3 ships, not before it is built. Frage 283 answered B on 2026-11-20 (the Dither on the picture, below); its follow-ups Frage 284 (answered B: Lowest power stays on the operation) and Frage 300 (answered A: Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Invert stay on the operation) on 2026-11-20 (below). No question is open for the Dither rework.

- [ADR 0072](../../docs/adr/0072-image-adjustments-on-the-image-object.md): registers `image_preset`, `image_strength`, `image_adjustments` (one atomic list, integer fields with units in the key) on the image node; `own_adjustments` marker plus the same registers on a clone (ADR 0016 pattern, adjustment source = nearest own on the chain); crop is a step and the drawn frame is the placement's visible part (placement and trace mask unchanged); merge, damage, history; dither values `halftone`, `line_screen` with `screen_size_mm`, `screen_angle_deg`; test-grid recipe `picture` and the `strength` axis, material record format 4; one `format_version` per slice (51 for slice 1 if nothing merges first).
- [ADR 0073](../../docs/adr/0073-image-adjustment-pipeline-and-presets.md): integer pixel steps and the analysis in `curvyo-image-core` (new edge `→ libm`); stored fields, presets, Strength and the mm-to-pixel resolution in `curvyo-laser-core`; three-zone pipeline order; three-box blur, CLAHE, bilateral denoise, DoG contours; presets quantised to stored units (Make editable is byte-exact); one adjusted image per picture and Interval, cached by content; Before and after from the job's sweeps; in-place memory rule; budgets of criterion 55 confirmed.
- [ADR 0074](../../docs/adr/0074-halftone-dots-and-line-screen-dither-modes.md): Halftone dots and Line screen as threshold tables of a rotated cell grid anchored at the item, independent of the scan angle; integer cos/sin table.
- [ADR 0075](../../docs/adr/0075-on-device-image-models-runtime-and-crate.md): new crate `curvyo-imageai-core` on `rten` (already locked and in the editor wasm via `vectrace`), never a dependency of laser-core or image-core; U²-Netp (Apache-2.0, 4.7 MB), Real-ESRGAN general x4v3 (BSD-3-Clause, 4.9 MB), SCUNet real PSNR (Apache-2.0 / MIT, 72 MB; FFDNet MIT 3.4 MB as fallback); our ONNX conversion with pinned hashes in `models.toml`; dedicated worker; security; step 0 measurements; training data accepted (Frage 281 A) and named in the About licence list.
- [ADR 0076](../../docs/adr/0076-model-download-host-and-the-one-network-use.md): on demand only, like digiKam (Frage 280 A): never bundled, nothing at app start; `https://models.curvyo.org/<name>/<version>/<sha256>.onnx`, an origin bucket behind a CDN, immutable paths; the manifest (name, version, size, SHA-256, licence, attribution, one URL) compiled into the app, no remote manifest; the frontend's `fetch` with no credentials, referrer, query or body; `Range` resume into a partial file, verify before commit; IndexedDB chunks in the browser with `persist()` and a quota check, the data folder on the desktop; states incl. paused, withdrawn and storage; About licence list; takedown; CORS and cache headers; CDN provider Frage 282.
- [ADR 0077](../../docs/adr/0077-derived-image-assets-in-the-project-file.md): `assets/derived/<sha256>.png` as a second asset class; the step's `result` record with model, version and input hash; integer application of the stored result; Out of date keeps burning, missing does not burn; pruning at save to what the tree or this session refers to; 64 MiB and 512 MiB confirmed, separate from the 512 MiB of pictures and fonts.
- Extended, not replaced: [ADR 0016](../../docs/adr/0016-clone-overrides.md) §1 to §5 (third override), [ADR 0017](../../docs/adr/0017-image-nodes-and-embedded-assets.md) §1, §2, §4 (visible frame, derived class, working limit), [ADR 0045](../../docs/adr/0045-clones-of-groups-texts-images-and-movable-placed-pages.md) §1 (image roots), [ADR 0057](../../docs/adr/0057-test-grid-object-with-derived-operations.md) §1 and [ADR 0058](../../docs/adr/0058-material-records-shared-across-lasers.md) §2 (recipe, record format 4), [ADR 0068](../../docs/adr/0068-raster-dot-values-and-dithering.md) §3, §5 (grey pyramid keyed by the adjusted image, two modes), [ADR 0069](../../docs/adr/0069-raster-rows-output-and-settings.md) §5, §7 to §9, §12, [ADR 0028](../../docs/adr/0028-ocr-text-in-trace.md) §4 and [ADR 0011](../../docs/adr/0011-workspace-and-crate-layout.md) §2, §6 (new crate, the `rayon` check), [ADR 0061](../../docs/adr/0061-image-trace-mask-in-the-project-file.md) §1 (the mask stays in the whole picture's square).

## Frage 284 (IMGOPT-LOWEST-POWER): answered B (customer, 2026-11-20)

Lowest power stays on the operation, next to Power (0123's `lowest_power_pct`); recorded in ADR 0072 A10. The question as asked, for the record:


Lowest power is the power of the palest dot in Grey mode, a percentage of the laser like Power. With Frage 283 B it can move to the picture with the dither, or stay on the Image operation beside Power and Speed.
- **A** On the picture, with the dither (as criterion 67 now says): two photos in one operation can have different lowest powers; the same photo on another material keeps its percentage; each raster row then carries its own lowest power to the G-code writer.
- **B** On the operation: the picture stores the Dither mode, Threshold and the screen fields; Lowest power stays a machine number of the operation, next to Power (criterion 22: no machine settings in the Adjustments group); nothing changes in the writer.

Recommendation: **B** (a power belongs to the material and the operation; a picture in two operations on two materials would otherwise burn its pale dots with one percentage on both). Default if unanswered was **A**.

## Frage 282 (IMGOPT-CDN-PROVIDER): Which CDN serves `models.curvyo.org`

The three models (about 82 MB per device for the full set) need a storage bucket and a CDN in front, on an account you own and pay for. The app checks every file itself, so the provider only has to deliver it; it sees each downloader's IP address and the time.
- **A** Cloudflare R2 with the custom domain `models.curvyo.org`: downloads are not charged, storage costs cents a month. Needs `curvyo.org`'s DNS on Cloudflare (moving the DNS, the domain stays where it is registered).
- **B** Bunny.net (Storage plus Pull Zone): a CNAME at your current DNS provider, a small charge per GB downloaded, EU company.
- **C** Amazon S3 with CloudFront: the widest-known setup, the most settings, downloads charged per GB.

Recommendation: **A** (no traffic cost, so hotlinking or many installs cost nothing). Default if unanswered: **A**, set up by you before slice 3 is released; building and testing slice 3 use a local static server and need no provider.
