## User value

As a maker engraving a picture on wood, slate or anodised metal I want to pick what kind of picture it is (photo, graphics, line art, newspaper look) and say with one Strength slider how strongly to improve it, and see the dots that will burn next to the original, so that the first burn is recognisable without a photo editor and trial burns; and I want to open the result as ordinary steps when I want to fine-tune it.

**What other tools do.** LightBurn has one "Adjust image" window (brightness, contrast, gamma, enhance edge, sharpen radius and amount, denoise, a few dither modes) that edits the image object destructively-in-place, previews only in a separate window and has no automatic mode (LightBurn has no preset that picks dither and adjustments together); its background removal does not exist (people use remove.bg or Photoshop). xTool Creative Space and Glowforge have one-button "enhance" and a cloud or local background remover, but the result is a black box: no steps to edit, no reproducible settings, and the picture goes to a server. Inkscape has nothing for pixels. **We do better:** a preset is a named recipe of dither mode and steps with its numbers visible (not a black box), one Strength slider scales it, the resolved steps can be turned into an ordinary editable stack at any time ("Make editable"), every step is undoable; the before/after shows the real dots at the operation's Interval; no picture ever leaves the machine (the only network use is the one-time model download after a consent line, criterion 63); the original pixels are never touched and "Restore original" is one click (criterion 59); the job file stays reproducible (derived pixels are recomputed; the results of the AI steps are stored as derived PNG assets, criterion 48).

## Words (shown in the UI)

The one-line meanings below are the spec's (the customer's rule: the meaning of a new tool before it is built). The UI shows **none** of them as a sentence or tooltip (the no-tips rule of `docs/design-system.md`): a control names itself; tooltips are the control's name only (`ux-notes.md` §16, E2, E3).

- **Adjustments:** the ordered stack of steps applied to a picture before the dots are made (criterion 1). Steps can be switched off, reordered, edited and removed.
- **Step:** one entry of the stack with its own fields. One-line meaning of each (customer rule: the meaning of a new tool before it is built):
  - **Crop:** "Keeps a rectangle of the picture and drops the rest."
  - **Resize:** "Sets how many pixels the picture has, with a clean resampling." (Reduces only; the only enlargement is Upscale, criteria 14 and 47.)
  - **Channel mix (grey):** "Chooses how colour becomes grey: how much red, green and blue count, so a red lip does not turn the same grey as a green leaf."
  - **Levels:** "Sets which grey counts as black and which as white, so the picture uses the whole range."
  - **Curves:** "Bends the tones: lifts the shadows or darkens the highlights without touching the rest."
  - **Contours:** Line width and Sensitivity (criterion 45).
- **Local contrast:** "Brings out detail inside small areas (faces, wood grain) without changing the picture as a whole."
  - **Sharpen:** "Makes edges crisper so they survive being turned into dots."
  - **Denoise:** "Smooths grain and speckle that would turn into stray dots."
  - **Remove background (mask):** "Hides everything except the subject, so only the subject burns."
- **Preset:** "Picks the dither and a recipe of steps that suits this kind of picture, scaled to your dot size." Seven are offered (criterion 25); the last, Expert, means you set everything yourself. Preset and Strength belong to the picture, not to the operation (Frage 272 A).
  - **Restore original:** "Takes every improvement off this picture; the original picture is kept and never was changed."
  - **Own adjustments (clone):** the clone has its own improvements; the original picture's stay as they are (the override row Adjustments, Original's | Own; criterion 9).
  - **Remove background, Upscale, AI denoise (run on this computer):** Remove background as above; **Upscale:** "Makes the picture 2 or 4 times larger in pixels and invents plausible detail, so a small picture can be engraved with fine dots."; **AI denoise:** "Removes grain and compression blocks by learning what a clean picture looks like; gentler on detail than Denoise."
  - **Strength:** "How hard the preset works on the picture: Gentle stays close to the original, Strong turns contrast and sharpening up."
  - **Make editable:** "Turns the preset's steps into ordinary steps you can change."
  - **Contours:** "Turns the picture into dark lines where it changes sharply from light to dark and white elsewhere, for line drawings and handwriting."
- **Before and after:** "Shows the original next to the dots that will burn, at the real dot size."
- **Working image, dot grid, dither mode, Interval:** as in `0058` and `0123`.
- **Halftone dots, Line screen (new dither modes):** "Burns the picture as round dots of changing size (Halftone dots) or as lines of changing thickness (Line screen) on a regular, rotatable grid, like a printed newspaper."
- **Adjusted image:** the working image after the preset's steps and the stack; derived, never stored (criterion 18), except the raw results of the AI steps (criterion 48).

### The stack (manual)

1. Given a selected image, then the Style tab of the image (the "Properties panel" of this spec is the Style tab; it is no longer dimmed for an image selection; `ux-notes.md` §2) offers an Adjustments group showing, from the top, the header with the symbol button **Restore original** (criterion 59), the Preset strip, below it the picture's **Dither strip** of nine modes with the fields of the chosen mode (criterion 67; UX places it), and Strength (criteria 25 and 26), the picture's stack, one row per step: switch (on/off), name, its fields, a remove button, and below the stack the **add toolbar** of step glyphs (an always-visible toolbar, no Add button and no list that opens; `ux-notes.md` §3.5). The group shows for exactly one image or image clone selected and does not switch tabs. A new picture has Preset Expert and an empty stack, and an empty stack changes nothing: the dots equal those of `0123` byte for byte (golden regression, criterion 54).
2. Given steps, then each can be switched off (the picture and dots are as if it were absent, the fields kept), removed, edited in place and moved up or down (drag handle; Proposal: keyboard Alt+Up and Alt+Down); every change is one undo step "Edit adjustments" (`0020`), also a preset pick or Make editable (criteria 27 and 29).
3. Given the stack, then the steps run top to bottom on the working image (RGBA8, `0058`) in the order of the rows; Proposal: a new step is inserted **after the last row whose kind comes earlier in the pipeline** (Crop, Resize, Upscale, AI denoise, then Channel mix, Denoise, Levels, Curves, Local contrast, Contours, Sharpen, then Remove background last); a second step of a kind is allowed; the maker may reorder only within what keeps the result defined (the mask last; Crop, Resize, Upscale, AI denoise first). The mask is computed from the picture after AI denoise and applied last. Test: adding Sharpen then Levels in either order leaves them in the pipeline order.
4. Given where the adjustments live (Frage 272 A, answered): **everything is on the image object**: the stack of criteria 1 to 5, the Preset and the Strength, and the results of the AI steps. Every Image operation the picture is assigned to, the Select view and every non-overriding clone read the same registers. The **Dither belongs to the picture** (Frage 283 B, answered 2026-11-20; criterion 67): the Dither mode and the fields of that mode (Threshold %, Cell size or Line pitch, Angle) are registers of the same image object, so each picture burns with its own dither even when several pictures share one Image operation. The Image operation keeps what `0123` gave it apart from that (Interval, Scan angle, Bidirectional fill, Overscanning, power, speed, **Lowest power** of the Grey mode (Frage 284 B, answered 2026-11-20), the tone fields Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Invert of `0123` as built (Frage 300 A, answered 2026-11-20), all for every item of the operation, pictures included); it holds no Preset and no stack, and its own Dither is only the dither of its non-picture leaves (criterion 67). The one input a preset needs from the operation is the **Interval**, read at evaluation time: see criterion 60 for a picture assigned to two operations. Criteria 5 to 24 hold for the steps as they stand.
5. Given each step, then its fields are plain number fields with units, never sliders (customer rule; the one exception is the Strength slider of criterion 26; the Levels and Curves graphs are readouts and direct-manipulation handles with the same values in fields), values rounded to the field's step, out-of-range refused with the range in the message (as `0123` criterion 12):
   - **Crop:** left, top, width, height in % of the picture (whole numbers, default the whole picture; width and height at least 1 %). The crop is also dragged on the canvas by a frame with handles. The crop makes the image object's frame the cropped rectangle at the same scale (the visible part keeps its size and place on the page; the object's width and height shrink to match; Proposal) and keeps the removed pixels in the asset, so un-cropping is lossless.
   - **Channel mix (grey):** Red, Green, Blue each -200 to 200 % (whole numbers, default 21.26, 71.52, 7.22 shown as 21, 72, 7; the default is the luminance of `0123` criterion 6); the sum may differ from 100 %; the result is clamped to 0 to 255. Presets as a strip (Luminance, Red filter, Green filter, Blue filter; text segments) set the three fields.
   - **Levels:** Black point 0 to 98 %, White point 2 to 100 % (black below white by at least 2), Gamma 0.10 to 4.00 (two decimals), all on grey; a histogram of the picture's greys (256 bins, drawn as hairline bars in `--panel-muted-fg`; no new colour) with the two points as handles; a button "Stretch" (symbol, tooltip "Stretch to the picture's range") sets Black and White to the 0.5 % and 99.5 % percentiles of the histogram.
   - **Curves:** at most 8 points, each Input % and Output % (0 to 100, whole numbers), the end points at 0 and 100 are fixed in input; the curve is monotone cubic through the points (no overshoot); handles on the graph; a reset button (symbol, tooltip "Straight curve").
   - **Local contrast:** Radius in mm of the page (0.2 to 20.0, one decimal, default 3.0) and Amount 0 to 200 % (default 50); an adaptive histogram method (CLAHE, clip limit from Amount) on tiles of the stated radius; the mm are converted to pixels with the image's pixel pitch on the page.
   - **Sharpen:** Radius in mm (0.05 to 5.00, two decimals, default the Interval) and Amount 0 to 500 % (default 100), threshold 0 to 50 % (default 0): an unsharp mask; it runs on the adjusted image at its pixel pitch (the mm are converted with the pitch; the radius 1 x Interval of the presets is then one dot of the engraving).
   - **Denoise:** Strength 0 to 100 % (default 30) and Detail kept 0 to 100 % (default 60): a **bilateral filter** (ADR 0073; non-local means misses the budget of criterion 55 more than tenfold); mm are not involved.
   - **Resize:** Width in pixels (the height follows; a lock keeps the ratio), 16 to the working image's width; only reduces (criterion 14).
6. Given a step that needs a colour image and the picture is already grey, or a step on a picture with alpha, then alpha is blended over white as in `0123` criterion 6 before any grey step, and Channel mix on a grey picture is accepted and changes nothing (no error).
7. Given the first step that produces grey, then later steps run on grey; before it a colour step (Denoise, Resize, Crop) runs per channel. Test: Denoise before and after Channel mix are both valid and both listed.
8. Given the registers of criterion 4, then they are stored on the image object as `preset` (absent = Expert), `strength` (absent = 50), the picture's dither as **one register `image_dither`** (criterion 67; a map `{mode, threshold_pct, screen_size_um, screen_angle_deg}`, all four written on every write, the three fields whole numbers; absent = Floyd-Steinberg at the `0123` defaults 50, 800, 45; no Lowest power, which stays the operation's, Frage 284 B; ADR 0072 amendment A1) and `adjustments`, an ordered list of `{kind, enabled, fields}`; absent or empty is no stack; unknown kinds from a later version are kept and not run, with a check warning (damage rule of ADR 0056). Full list under "File format notes". **One `format_version` per slice** (ADR 0072 §8), not one for the story: slice 1 takes 51 if nothing else merges first, slices 2 and 3 the next free number at their merge (rows in the `format_version` table of `specs/README.md`); the project file is unreleased, no migration.
9. Given a **linked clone of an image** (linked clones of images exist since ADR 0045 and spec `0100`, so nothing in `0049` is amended; the clone shares the asset bytes; only the third override is new), then it shows the **original's adjustments** (Preset, Strength, dither, stack, stored results) until the maker edits any adjustment on the clone, **a change of the clone's Dither strip included** (criterion 67): that first edit creates the clone's **own adjustments**, a full copy of the original's four registers (`image_preset`, `image_strength`, `image_dither`, `image_adjustments`) at that moment (all or nothing, like own style of `0057`: Preset, Strength, the picture's dither (criterion 67), stack and stored results are overridden together, never mixed), which is then edited; from then on the clone is independent in both directions (later edits of the original's adjustments do not reach it, edits of the clone never reach the original). The original is unchanged by everything done on the clone (test: the original's registers and rendered dots are byte-identical before and after). The clone's Style tab shows a third override row **Adjustments** with the pill (Original's | Own) beside Placement and Style (`0057`); the first edit flips it to Own, and pressing **Original's** is Follow original (one undo step; there is no line "Own adjustments" and no Follow original button); the Layers tooltip and canvas tag of `0057` criteria 28 and 29 gain "own adjustments". Given a duplicate image object (Ctrl+D), then it has its own copy of the stack (not a clone); a duplicate of a clone with own adjustments has a copy of them. Given Unlink clone, then the new image object gets the effective adjustments it showed. Given an original that is deleted, the repair of `0049` criterion 35 applies unchanged (the clone is turned into its stored outline, or removed when it has none).
10. Given Copy and Paste of an image to another document or another page, then its Preset, Strength, Dither, stack and stored results travel with it. Given a "Copy adjustments" and "Paste adjustments" pair (two symbol buttons in the group header; Proposal), then the adjustments of one picture can be applied to another; pasting replaces the target's Preset, Strength, stack and Dither (Frage 283 B; the clone rule of criterion 9 applies when the target is a clone) in one undo step.

### Before and after

11. Given a selected image that has an Image operation (or a test-grid cell), then a Before and after view can be opened with a symbol button (tooltip "Before and after") in the Adjustments group: a pane in the canvas area (the split of the font preview `0077`, taking 50 % of the canvas area, closing the font preview when open, switchable to full view) shows the original, and the engraved result as the dots, at the operation's Interval and Scan angle and the picture's own dither, side by side, with a Split switch (a draggable divider instead of two panes; Split is the default view, Side by side one press away; Proposal) and zoom and pan locked together. Given a picture assigned to more than one Image operation, then a text-segment pick above the view names the operation whose Interval and Scan angle are shown (default the first, criterion 60).
12. Given the engraved result in that view, then it is the same dots as the Toolpath view and the file (`0123` criteria 14 and 20) at the zoom shown: at 100 % one dot is Interval mm wide on screen at the document's scale; below one device pixel per dot it is the shaded dot area of `0123` criterion 20. Test: the dot count of a 10 x 10 mm region read from the view equals that of the job.
13. Given an edit of a step's field, then the view updates in the same frame at a reduced level while the pointer or the key is held (the working image's mip level that fits the pane, the dither on the dot grid of the visible part only) and settles to the exact dots within 500 ms of release for the working set of criterion 55; the exact dots are the ones the job will burn.
14. Given Resize, then it can only reduce the working image (there is no enlargement; an enlargement is the AI Upscale of criterion 47 or nothing); Resize never goes above the working limit of `0058` criterion 18. A smaller picture than the dot grid needs is the "coarser than the Interval" warning of `0123` criterion 35, which stays.
15. Given a picture whose adjustments are not empty, then the canvas shows the adjusted image (greyscale or as the colour stands after the steps, as the Select view shows an image, at the Interval of criterion 60) Nothing else about the object's look changes: **no symbol on the canvas object**, also not for steps that are off (E10).
16. Given the histogram of Levels, then it is of the picture after the steps above it, live, from the working image at the mip level of the pane.

### Determinism, derived data, caching

17. Given the same image bytes, stack and Interval, then the adjusted image and the dots are identical on every platform (native and wasm): every step except Remove background (criterion 47) uses integer or fixed-point arithmetic only, as `0123` criterion 8 (Proposal: the Gaussian and bilateral kernels are integer tables; the CLAHE uses integer histograms; the curve is a 256-entry integer table). Test: golden outputs byte-identical native and wasm.
18. Given the work, then the adjusted image is derived: computed from the asset bytes of `0058` and the registers, cached in memory **by content** (the picture's bytes, the resolved steps and the Interval; ADR 0073 §7), so two clones, two operations at one Interval and an undo share one adjusted image, never written into the project (the project does not grow by adjusting). The one exception is the raw result of an AI step (criterion 48). The original asset bytes are never modified by any step, preset or restore (test: the SHA-256 of the asset is identical after every edit, including Crop).
19. Given the job (`0029`), then the laser-core job builder takes the adjusted image as the "working image" of `0123` criterion 7; the stack steps live in a `*-core` crate (Proposal: the pixel steps in `curvyo-image-core`, the stack and dot-scale integration in `curvyo-laser-core`; the architect decides in `adrs.md`); the job itself stays pixel-free and format-neutral (`0029` criterion 39), the G-code is as built.
20. Given two exports of the same project, then they are byte-identical (`0029` criterion 31).
21. Given an image whose bytes cannot be read, then the stack is kept and the checks warn as `0123` criterion 36; the steps run again when the bytes return.
22. Given an unfolded Image operation row and an image with adjustments, then the row is unchanged by this story apart from its Dither strip, which is shown only while the operation holds a non-picture leaf (criterion 67) and has the two new modes and their fields (criteria 42 to 44); the Preset, Strength, stack and the picture's dither are not shown there. The row keeps **Lowest power** (`0123`), shown while any item of the operation burns Grey (a picture with Grey or its gradient fills' Grey); a Grey picture burns with the Lowest power of the operation being evaluated, so a picture in two operations burns with each one's (Frage 284 B, ADR 0072 A10). The row's numbers line names a dither (" · Jarvis", " · Halftone 0.80 mm") **only when every item of the operation burns with the same dither** (each picture its own, each gradient-filled leaf the operation's); otherwise the line shows the text "several dithers" in its place, and an empty operation shows none (Frage 283 B, UX notes E17). There are no machine settings in the Adjustments group (no Lowest power field there; Frage 284 B).
23. Given the checks (`0029`), then adjustments add no block and at most two warn lines: "1 step in the Adjustments of <image name> could not run." / "<n> steps ..." (unknown kind, damaged fields; a button selects the image), the `0123` Grey lines (Grey needs laser mode M4; Lowest power above Power), which **stay the operation's lines** (one per Image operation, naming and selecting it, raised when any of its items burns Grey, a picture included; Frage 284 B), and "<n> stored AI results of <image name> are out of date." (criterion 48).
24. Given Undo (`0020`) after any edit of the adjustments (including a preset pick, criterion 27, Make editable, criterion 29, Restore original, criterion 59, a clone's own adjustments, criterion 9, and an AI run, criterion 48), then the registers are exactly as before, the Before and after view follows, and no pixel is stored in the history beyond the registers' fields (and, for an AI step, the asset reference).

### Presets and Strength (slice 1, the centre of the story)

25. Given a selected image, then the Adjustments group (criterion 1) opens with a **Preset** pick: a fixed **two-row choice strip** (not a drop-down; short cell "Dots" for Dot raster) with these entries in this order: **Photo (detailed)**, **Graphics (flat areas)**, **Black and white (hard edges)**, **Line art (outlines and handwriting)**, **Dot raster (round dots)**, **Newspaper (retro screen)**, **Expert** (manual: no preset). **No one-line description is shown** (the no-tips rule). The tooltip of each cell is "<full name> · Dither: <mode>", e.g. "Photo (detailed) · Dither: Jarvis"; the tooltip of Expert is **"Expert · Dither unchanged"** (Expert writes no dither, criterion 27); the chosen cell is pressed. A new picture has Preset **Expert**, so nothing changes against `0123` until the maker picks one (Proposal). The strip is keyboard operable (arrows move and select) and one selection is one undo step "Edit adjustments".
26. Given a Preset other than Expert, then below the pick a **Strength** control shows the one slider this story allows (the customer asked for it explicitly; `docs/design-system.md` gets this as its single exception to "no sliders", criterion 57): a slider with a percent field next to it (0 to 100 %, whole numbers, default 50, out-of-range refused with the range in the message as `0123` criterion 12), the left end labelled **Gentle** ("stays close to the original") and the right end **Strong** ("hard contrast and sharpening"). Keyboard: Left and Down minus 1, Right and Up plus 1, Page Up and Page Down plus and minus 10, Home 0, End 100; the field and the slider show the same value. While dragging, the dots (Before and after view, Toolpath view) update at the reduced level of criterion 13; one drag from press to release is one undo step "Edit adjustments". With Expert the Strength control is hidden (not disabled), and its value is kept.
27. Given a pick of a preset, then one undo step "Edit adjustments" writes the Preset on the **picture**, and the picture's own Dither mode and the fields of that mode (Threshold, Cell size or Line pitch, Angle) as the tables of criteria 32 to 37 say, the mm fields resolved as **k x the Interval of the first Image operation the picture is assigned to** (Cell size and Line pitch; criterion 60; 0.10 mm when it is assigned to none), rounded to 10 micrometres and kept within 0.20 to 5.00 mm, and then ordinary fields (Proposal); a field the preset does not name keeps its value; a picture in no Image operation gets its Preset and Dither all the same; it **never writes to an operation**, so two pictures with different presets in one Image operation keep their own dither and nothing is overwritten (Frage 283 B; no notice line exists). It leaves everything else as it was (Strength, Interval, power, Lowest power, speed, Invert, Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Scan angle); a preset that picked Grey would write no Lowest power either (no preset picks Grey, criterion 40; Frage 284 B). The dither stays an ordinary picture field afterwards: the maker may change it with the Dither strip (criterion 67), which keeps the Preset and Strength (the derived steps of criterion 28 do not read the Dither mode). **Expert writes only the Preset** (the dither stays as it is).
28. Given a Preset other than Expert (Frage 279; default A), then the stored state is **Preset and Strength** (two small registers on the image object, the `format_version` bump of criterion 8) and the picture steps are **derived** when the job is built, from Preset, Strength, the picture analysis (criterion 39) and the Interval of the operation being evaluated (criterion 60); nothing derived is stored (criterion 18). The Adjustments group then shows the derived steps as read-only rows under the line "From preset <name>, Strength <n> %" with the resolved numbers, and a button **Make editable** (criterion 29). Changing the Interval, Strength or the picture recomputes the steps (the preset is live, not a one-time fill). With Frage 279 B the pick fills an ordinary stack once and nothing is derived.
29. Given **Make editable** (text button, no tooltip sentence), then in one undo step the derived steps with their numbers resolved at that moment (at the Interval of the operation shown in the Before and after view, criterion 60; a notice names it when the picture has more than one operation) become the ordinary manual stack of criteria 1 to 5, Preset becomes Expert, Strength is kept; the dots of that operation before and after the click are identical byte for byte (test); another operation with a different Interval now uses the fixed mm numbers (stated in the notice). Manual tone and detail steps that were listed as "Preset in use" (criterion 30) are **replaced** by the resolved steps. Nothing else changes: the picture's Dither mode and its fields stay.
30. Given a manual stack and a Preset other than Expert on the same picture, then the preset's derived steps run instead of the picture's manual tone and detail steps (Channel mix, Denoise, Levels, Curves, Local contrast, Contours, Sharpen; those are listed switched off with the line "Preset in use", kept, and run again with Expert); Crop, Resize, Upscale, AI denoise and Remove background always run.
31. Given the **Strength mapping**, then every field marked "Gentle to Strong" in criteria 32 to 37 is v(S) = v0 + round-half-up((v100 - v0) x S / 100) in integer arithmetic on the field's step (for example Amount in whole %, Gamma target in whole %, clip in tenths of %), then clamped to the field's range of criterion 5; v0 is the value at Strength 0 (Gentle), v100 at 100 (Strong). Fields without two values are fixed; fields given as "k x Interval" use the Interval of the operation in mm, rounded to the field's step and clamped to its range (criterion 40).
32. Preset **Photo (detailed)** (for photos on wood, slate, anodised metal): Dither **Jarvis**. Steps in pipeline order: **Denoise** only when the noise estimate (criterion 39) is above 3.0: Strength 10 to 40 %, Detail kept 70 to 50 %; **Levels**: clip 0.2 to 2.0 % at each end of the histogram (Black and White point at these percentiles), Gamma chosen so the median grey lands at 50 to 42 % (Gamma = ln(median) / ln(target), clamped 0.10 to 4.00, two decimals; the `0123` convention, above 1 lightens, so the result is the same as ln(target) / ln(median) in the inverted convention, ADR 0073 §5); **Local contrast**: Radius 100 x Interval, Amount 10 to 90 %; **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 40 to 200 %, Threshold 0 %.
33. Preset **Graphics (flat areas)** (logos, clip art, scans of flat drawings): Dither **Ordered**, so flat areas of different greys stay different patterns and edges stay clean. Steps: **Denoise** (always): Strength 20 to 60 %, Detail kept 90 to 70 % (flattens JPEG speckle inside flat areas, keeps edges); **Levels**: clip 0.0 to 1.0 %, Gamma 1.00 (no median target); **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 0 to 100 %, Threshold 5 to 10 % (the threshold keeps flat areas flat). No Local contrast.
34. Preset **Black and white (hard edges)** (signs, stamps, scanned documents, photos to be burnt as a two-tone picture): Dither **Threshold** with Threshold 50 %. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 0 to 30 %, Detail kept 80 to 60 %; **Levels**: clip 0.5 to 3.0 %, Gamma chosen so the Otsu level (criterion 39) lands at 50 %; ~~**Local contrast**: Radius 30 x Interval, Amount 0 to 60 % (evens uneven lighting);~~ **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 50 to 200 %, Threshold 0 %. (Amended 2026-11-20, UX review U7: no Local contrast; on a soft-shaded photo it turned the paper's darker shading into a solid black area at Strength 100. Test `curvyo-laser-core/tests/preset_quality.rs`.)
35. Preset **Line art (outlines and handwriting)** (Frage 278; default A): Dither **Threshold** with Threshold 50 %. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 20 to 40 %, Detail kept 80 to 60 %; **Levels**: clip 0.5 to 2.0 %, Gamma 1.00; **Local contrast** (flattens paper and background): Radius 300 x Interval, Amount ~~50 to 100 %~~ 0 to 30 %; **Contours** (criterion 45): Line width ~~3 x~~ 5 x Interval, Sensitivity ~~30 to 80 %~~ 60 to 100 %; (amended 2026-11-20, UX review U7: at 3 x Interval Contours traced both edges of a wider pen line, Gentle burned too little and Strong speckled the paper; the new numbers burn at least 90 % of the line pixels and no paper at every Strength on a synthetic pen drawing, `curvyo-laser-core/tests/preset_quality.rs`; the preset tables stay Proposals) **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 0 to 100 %, Threshold 0 %. With Frage 278 B the Contours step is dropped from this preset and Local contrast carries it.
36. Preset **Dot raster (round dots)** (Frage 277; default A): Dither **Halftone dots** (criterion 42) with Cell size 8 x Interval and Angle 45 degrees. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 20 to 40 %, Detail kept 60 %; **Levels**: clip 0.5 to 2.0 %, Gamma median target 50 to 45 %; **Local contrast**: Radius 100 x Interval, Amount 0 to 50 %; **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 0 to 80 %, Threshold 0 %.
37. Preset **Newspaper (retro screen)** (Frage 277; default A): Dither **Line screen** (criterion 43) with Line pitch 12 x Interval and Angle 45 degrees. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 30 to 60 %, Detail kept 50 %; **Levels**: clip 2.0 to 6.0 %, Gamma median target 45 to 38 %. No Local contrast, no Sharpen (the coarse screen is the look).
38. Preset **Expert** derives nothing: the manual stack (criteria 1 to 5) and the Image settings of `0123` are as the maker set them.
39. Given the **picture analysis** every preset reads, then it is computed on the working image after Crop, Resize, Upscale and AI denoise (criterion 3), on a grey image (`0123` criterion 6) at the box-averaged power-of-two mip of at most 1 megapixel, in integer arithmetic only (criterion 17): the 256-bin histogram; the **percentile** p is the smallest grey whose cumulative share is at least p %; the **median** is the 50 % percentile; the **Otsu level** is the grey that maximises the between-class variance (ties to the lower grey); the **noise estimate** is 1.4826 x the median of the absolute 3 x 3 Laplacian response in grey levels (Proposal; the architect fixes the kernel). ## Open questions

**Frage 284 (IMGOPT-LOWEST-POWER) (ANSWERED B by the customer, 2026-11-20: Lowest power stays on the operation next to Power, `0123`'s `lowest_power_pct`; criteria 4, 8, 22, 23, 27, 67): Does Lowest power go with the picture's dither or stay on the operation?** Lowest power is the power of the palest dot in Grey mode, a percentage of the laser like Power.
- **A** On the picture, in `image_dither`, as criterion 67 says: two photos in one operation can have different lowest powers; each raster row carries its own lowest power to the G-code writer.
- **B** On the operation next to Power: the picture stores mode, Threshold and the screen fields only; a power belongs to the material and the operation (criterion 22: no machine settings in the Adjustments group); a picture in two operations on two materials keeps one percentage per operation; the writer is unchanged.

Recommendation (architect): **B**. Default if unanswered was **A**.

**Frage 300 (IMGOPT-TONE-OP) (ANSWERED A by the customer, 2026-11-20: they stay on the Image operation and apply to every item in it, pictures included): Where do Brightness, Contrast, Gamma and Invert belong now?**
- **A** They stay on the Image operation for all its items, pictures included (as built in `0123`; criterion 67 as written).
- **B** They move to the picture with the dither (one more place on the image object, more own-adjustments data, and the operation keeps them for gradient fills only).

Recommendation: **A** (they are the same tone settings for every item the operation burns, and nothing is built or stored twice). Default if unanswered was **A**.

**Frage 277 (IMGOPT-HALFTONE) (ANSWERED A by the customer, 2026-11-19): Are the two halftone Dither modes built in this story?** Dot raster (round dots) and Newspaper (retro screen) need two Dither modes that `0123` does not have: **Halftone dots** (round dots that grow with the darkness on a rotated grid, fields Cell size and Angle) and **Line screen** (lines that thicken with the darkness, fields Line pitch and Angle).
A: Yes, both in slice 1 (nine modes; all seven presets are in the demo).
B: Not now; slice 1 offers five presets plus Expert, the two halftone presets and modes follow as their own story.
C: Only Halftone dots now; Newspaper then uses Halftone dots at a coarse cell until Line screen follows.
Recommendation: A (the presets are the heart of the story and both modes are small, integer and golden-testable). Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 278 (IMGOPT-LINEART) (ANSWERED A by the customer, 2026-11-19): Is Line art an edge-extraction step in the stack?** The preset Line art (outlines and handwriting) can either find the lines itself or only clean a picture that already is lines.
A: Yes, a new step **Contours** (dark lines where the picture changes sharply from light to dark, width and sensitivity as fields) in the stack, used by Line art; a photo can become a line drawing.
B: No new step; Line art = flatten the background (Local contrast), Levels, Threshold: right for pen drawings and handwriting on paper, no line drawing from a photo; Contours follows later if wanted.
C: Contours as a step the maker can add by hand, but not used by the Line art preset.
Recommendation: A. Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 279 (IMGOPT-PRESET-STORE) (ANSWERED A by the customer, 2026-11-19): Is a preset live or a one-time fill?**
A: Live: the operation stores only Preset and Strength; the steps are derived whenever the picture, the Interval or the Strength changes (a new Interval re-scales the preset by itself); "Make editable" turns the current steps into an ordinary stack and sets Preset to Expert.
B: One-time fill: picking a preset (and moving Strength) writes an ordinary stack; the stack is the stored state; nothing follows a later Interval change; no Make editable needed, but the preset name and Strength are lost as soon as the maker edits a step.
Recommendation: A (the maker keeps the one-click look and the slider, and fine-tuning is one click away). Default if unanswered: A.

## Out of scope

- A Cut-out that follows the contour of a subject, mask or transparent background (later idea, from the same review).
- Colour management, colour editing, retouching, clone stamp, perspective or lens correction, rotate or skew (an image rotates and skews as an object).
- Depth or relief maps and "make engravable" enhancement (later stories). Upscaling and AI denoise are in scope (slice 3, Frage 273 C).
- An AI or automatic guess of the best preset for a picture, and a preset that chooses power or speed (the maker chooses the preset; the Interval is the only machine value a preset reads).
- Batch processing of many pictures, the maker's own presets saved to a library (a later idea; Make editable and Copy and Paste adjustments are the first steps).
- A cloud service, accounts, paid content and telemetry (the customer chose the on-device way, Frage 274 B; the only network use is the model download).
- Models bundled with the app, loaded at start, or served from any other host (Frage 280: on demand from the one Curvyo origin behind a CDN only, criterion 63).
- Changes to `0123`'s dithers, dot grid, material sets, G-code and the checks, except where this story says so.
- Mask painting (Keep and Erase brush): slice 4, Could, after Frage 273.

## File format notes

One `format_version` per slice (criterion 8; ADR 0072 §8): slice 1 takes 51 if nothing else merges first, slices 2 and 3 the next free number at their merge; **material record format 4** for the grid's picture source and Strength axis (criterion 51). On the **image object** (ADR 0072 §1 to §4): the registers `image_preset` (absent = Expert), `image_strength` (0 to 100; absent = 50) and `image_adjustments` (one atomic ordered list of `{kind, enabled, fields}`, integer fields with the unit in the key; kinds crop, resize, channel_mix, levels, curves, local_contrast, contours, sharpen, denoise, and the AI kinds remove_background, upscale, ai_denoise; an AI step also holds `result: {asset, model, model_version, input_hash, size}`), and on a clone an `own_adjustments` marker plus the same registers (absent registers read as defaults; all or nothing; the adjustment source is the nearest own on the chain, ADR 0016 pattern). Crop is a step and keeps `image_placement` (the stored frame stays the whole picture, the drawn frame is its cropped part; ADR 0072 §3). Frage 283 B: the **picture registers also carry the dither** (criterion 67): the fourth register **`image_dither`**, one atomic map `{mode, threshold_pct, screen_size_um (200 to 5000, a multiple of 10), screen_angle_deg (0 to 89)}`, absent = Floyd-Steinberg at 50, 800, 45 (nine modes, `halftone` and `line_screen` share the screen fields, criteria 42 and 43), copied, followed and removed with the other three registers on a clone's `own_adjustments`; a damaged map reads as the defaults, is kept, and counts in criterion 23 (ADR 0072 amendment A1, A5; no Lowest power in the map: it stays the operation's `lowest_power_pct`, Frage 284 B). Slice 1 stays **format 51**, unreleased: changed in place, no migration. The Image operation gains no register; its existing Dither keys (`dither`, `threshold_pct`, `screen_size_mm`, `screen_angle_deg`) stay and serve only its non-picture leaves (a Gradient fill, the Ramp cells); `lowest_power_pct` and the tone keys serve all its items. `assets/derived/<hash>.png` holds the raw results of the AI steps (8-bit PNG), unreferenced ones are dropped at save. The test-grid recipe keeps the Picture source and, per cell, the Strength (criterion 51). Model files are not part of the project and are never embedded. Unknown kinds and damaged fields follow the damage rule of ADR 0056 (criterion 23). A golden project file pins the format (own adjustments, one stored result per AI kind).

## Changes to accepted specs

- `0049` linked clone: **nothing to amend**; clones of images exist since ADR 0045 and spec `0100` (criterion 9).
- `0057` clone overrides: a third, all-or-nothing override **own adjustments** beside own placement and own style, a third override row Adjustments (Original's | Own) whose Original's is the way back; its Layers tooltip and canvas tag gain the words (criterion 9). The rules of `0057` criterion 37 (format) are met by the bump of slice 1.
- `0058` bitmap-import: the working image gains Upscale as its only enlargement; derived assets in `assets/derived/` are a new asset class with garbage collection at save (criteria 14, 48, 59).
- `0123` raster-engraving: nine Dither modes instead of seven (criteria 42 to 44); the picture's registers sit on the image object, and since Frage 283 B (2026-11-20) the **Dither of a picture** (mode and its fields, `0123` criterion 11) moves from the operation to the picture: the operation's Dither strip stays only for non-picture leaves (criteria 22 and 67); the Image operation is otherwise unchanged; the Image test grid's recipe (criterion 51, also `0123` criterion 44).
- `0112` test grids: the Image grid gets a Picture pick and the Strength axis (criterion 51); a material's Image set stores no adjustment (criterion 52).
- `0029` criterion 35 (no network, no telemetry): the one-time model download after a consent line is the only network use of the application, carries no picture and no identifier (criterion 63).
- `docs/design-system.md` gets the Strength slider as its one exception to "no sliders" (criterion 57).
