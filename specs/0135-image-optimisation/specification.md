# Image optimisation: presets with one Strength slider, a stack to edit by hand, and (by decision) local AI tools

Status: Draft (2026-11-19; waiting for Fragen 272 to 279; builds on `0123` raster-engraving, Done)
Priority: Should
Origin: Customer (review of PR #394, 2026-11-19: the engraved result of a test photo is hard to recognise; wants image optimisation "automatic, AI-assisted and manual"). Customer feedback on the draft (2026-11-19): the most important thing is an **automatic mode that optimises the picture according to a preset** (a preset list as in another program, tooltip showing the dither used) plus **one slider for how strongly to improve**, from Gentle (stays close to the original) to Strong (hard contrast and so on); the questions are to be asked now. Everything marked **(Proposal)** is the product owner's and vetoable at the demo. Platform, runtime, model and data-leaving decisions are the customer's (`CLAUDE.md` §3) and are asked in Fragen 273 to 275; until answered, slice 3 is not Ready.

## User value

As a maker engraving a picture on wood, slate or anodised metal I want to pick what kind of picture it is (photo, graphics, line art, newspaper look) and say with one Strength slider how strongly to improve it, and see the dots that will burn next to the original, so that the first burn is recognisable without a photo editor and trial burns; and I want to open the result as ordinary steps when I want to fine-tune it.

**What other tools do.** LightBurn has one "Adjust image" window (brightness, contrast, gamma, enhance edge, sharpen radius and amount, denoise, a few dither modes) that edits the image object destructively-in-place, previews only in a separate window and has no automatic mode (LightBurn has no preset that picks dither and adjustments together); its background removal does not exist (people use remove.bg or Photoshop). xTool Creative Space and Glowforge have one-button "enhance" and a cloud or local background remover, but the result is a black box: no steps to edit, no reproducible settings, and the picture goes to a server. Inkscape has nothing for pixels. **We do better:** a preset is a named recipe of dither mode and steps with its numbers visible (not a black box), one Strength slider scales it, the resolved steps can be turned into an ordinary editable stack at any time ("Make editable"), every step is undoable; the before/after shows the real dots at the operation's Interval; nothing leaves the machine by default; the job file stays reproducible (derived pixels are recomputed, the one stored exception is decided in Frage 275).

## Words (shown in the UI)

- **Adjustments:** the ordered stack of steps applied to a picture before the dots are made (criterion 1). Steps can be switched off, reordered, edited and removed.
- **Step:** one entry of the stack with its own fields. One-line meaning of each (customer rule: the meaning of a new tool before it is built):
  - **Crop:** "Keeps a rectangle of the picture and drops the rest."
  - **Resize:** "Sets how many pixels the picture has, with a clean resampling." (Reduces only; see criterion 14.)
  - **Channel mix (grey):** "Chooses how colour becomes grey: how much red, green and blue count, so a red lip does not turn the same grey as a green leaf."
  - **Levels:** "Sets which grey counts as black and which as white, so the picture uses the whole range."
  - **Curves:** "Bends the tones: lifts the shadows or darkens the highlights without touching the rest."
  - **Contours:** Line width and Sensitivity (criterion 45).
- **Local contrast:** "Brings out detail inside small areas (faces, wood grain) without changing the picture as a whole."
  - **Sharpen:** "Makes edges crisper so they survive being turned into dots."
  - **Denoise:** "Smooths grain and speckle that would turn into stray dots."
  - **Remove background (mask):** "Hides everything except the subject, so only the subject burns."
- **Preset:** "Picks the dither and a recipe of steps that suits this kind of picture, scaled to your dot size." Seven are offered (criterion 25); the last, Expert, means you set everything yourself.
  - **Strength:** "How hard the preset works on the picture: Gentle stays close to the original, Strong turns contrast and sharpening up."
  - **Make editable:** "Turns the preset's steps into ordinary steps you can change."
  - **Contours:** "Turns the picture into dark lines where it changes sharply from light to dark and white elsewhere, for line drawings and handwriting."
- **Before and after:** "Shows the original next to the dots that will burn, at the real dot size."
- **Working image, dot grid, dither mode, Interval:** as in `0058` and `0123`.
- **Halftone dots, Line screen (new dither modes):** "Burns the picture as round dots of changing size (Halftone dots) or as lines of changing thickness (Line screen) on a regular, rotatable grid, like a printed newspaper."
- **Adjusted image:** the working image after the preset's steps and the stack; derived, never stored (criterion 24), except an AI mask (Frage 275).

### The stack (manual)

1. Given a selected image, then the Properties panel offers an Adjustments group (a symbol-only add button with tooltip "Add adjustment" opening the step list; Proposal, as the tool-specific bar rule of the customer: UX notes decide the exact place) showing the picture's stack, one row per step: switch (on/off), name, its fields, a remove button. A new picture has an empty stack, and an empty stack changes nothing: the dots equal those of `0123` byte for byte (golden regression, criterion 54).
2. Given steps, then each can be switched off (the picture and dots are as if it were absent, the fields kept), removed, edited in place and moved up or down (drag handle; Proposal: keyboard Alt+Up and Alt+Down); every change is one undo step "Edit adjustments" (`0020`), and one undo of a preset pick or Make editable (criteria 27 and 29).
3. Given the stack, then the steps run top to bottom on the working image (RGBA8, `0058`) in the order of the rows; Proposal: a new step is inserted at the sensible place of the fixed pipeline (Crop, Resize, Channel mix, Denoise, Levels, Curves, Local contrast, Contours, Sharpen, Mask), and the maker may reorder only within what keeps the result defined (the mask last; Crop and Resize first). Test: adding Sharpen then Levels in either order leaves them in the pipeline order.
4. Given where the stack lives (Frage 272; default C): **A** everything on the image object, **B** everything on the Image operation, **C** split: the geometry and subject steps (Crop, Resize, Remove background) on the image object, so every operation, the Select view and a clone see the same cut-out picture; the tone and detail steps (Channel mix, Denoise, Levels, Curves, Local contrast, Contours, Sharpen), the Preset and Strength and the tone fields Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Invert, Threshold of `0123` (as built) on the Image operation, because they depend on the Interval and the material. Criteria 5 to 24 hold for the steps wherever they live; the rows of the Adjustments group then show the object steps and, below a hairline, a read-only line "Preset, tone and detail: set in <operation name>" that selects the operation.
5. Given each step, then its fields are plain number fields with units, never sliders (customer rule; the one exception is the Strength slider of criterion 26; the Levels and Curves graphs are readouts and direct-manipulation handles with the same values in fields), values rounded to the field's step, out-of-range refused with the range in the message (as `0123` criterion 12):
   - **Crop:** left, top, width, height in % of the picture (whole numbers, default the whole picture; width and height at least 1 %). The crop is also dragged on the canvas by a frame with handles. The crop makes the image object's frame the cropped rectangle at the same scale (the visible part keeps its size and place on the page; the object's width and height shrink to match; Proposal) and keeps the removed pixels in the asset, so un-cropping is lossless.
   - **Channel mix (grey):** Red, Green, Blue each -200 to 200 % (whole numbers, default 21.26, 71.52, 7.22 shown as 21, 72, 7; the default is the luminance of `0123` criterion 6); the sum may differ from 100 %; the result is clamped to 0 to 255. Presets as a strip (Luminance, Red filter, Green filter, Blue filter; text segments) set the three fields.
   - **Levels:** Black point 0 to 98 %, White point 2 to 100 % (black below white by at least 2), Gamma 0.10 to 4.00 (two decimals), all on grey; a histogram of the picture's greys (256 bins, drawn as hairline bars in `--panel-muted-fg`; no new colour) with the two points as handles; a button "Stretch" (symbol, tooltip "Stretch to the picture's range") sets Black and White to the 0.5 % and 99.5 % percentiles of the histogram.
   - **Curves:** at most 8 points, each Input % and Output % (0 to 100, whole numbers), the end points at 0 and 100 are fixed in input; the curve is monotone cubic through the points (no overshoot); handles on the graph; a reset button (symbol, tooltip "Straight curve").
   - **Local contrast:** Radius in mm of the page (0.2 to 20.0, one decimal, default 3.0) and Amount 0 to 200 % (default 50); an adaptive histogram method (CLAHE, clip limit from Amount) on tiles of the stated radius; the mm are converted to pixels with the image's pixel pitch on the page.
   - **Sharpen:** Radius in mm (0.05 to 5.00, two decimals, default the Interval) and Amount 0 to 500 % (default 100), threshold 0 to 50 % (default 0): an unsharp mask; with Frage 272 C it runs on the dot grid's grey values after the resample of `0123` criterion 6 (so the radius is in dots of the real engraving), with A or B on the working image at the pixel pitch.
   - **Denoise:** Strength 0 to 100 % (default 30) and Detail kept 0 to 100 % (default 60): an edge-preserving filter (Proposal: non-local-means-style bilateral, architect chooses); mm are not involved.
   - **Resize:** Width in pixels (the height follows; a lock keeps the ratio), 16 to the working image's width; only reduces (criterion 14).
6. Given a step that needs a colour image and the picture is already grey, or a step on a picture with alpha, then alpha is blended over white as in `0123` criterion 6 before any grey step, and Channel mix on a grey picture is accepted and changes nothing (no error).
7. Given the first step that produces grey, then later steps run on grey; before it a colour step (Denoise, Resize, Crop) runs per channel. Test: Denoise before and after Channel mix are both valid and both listed.
8. Given a stack of steps, then it is stored on the object (or operation, criterion 4) in a new register `adjustments` as an ordered list of `{kind, enabled, fields}`; absent or empty is no stack; unknown kinds from a later version are kept and not run, with a check warning (damage rule of ADR 0056). The format bump is the architect's (Proposal: one bump of `format_version`; the project file is unreleased, no migration; Curvyo is not deployed).
9. Given Edit steps in a clone (`0049`) or a placed page (`0106`), then they edit the original's stack; the clone shows the same result. Given a duplicate image object (Ctrl+D), then it has its own copy of the stack.
10. Given Copy and Paste of an image to another document or another page, then its stack travels with it. Given a "Copy adjustments" and "Paste adjustments" pair (two symbol buttons in the group header; Proposal), then the stack of one picture can be applied to another; pasting replaces the target's stack in one undo step.

### Before and after

11. Given a selected image that has an Image operation (or a test-grid cell), then a Before and after view can be opened with a symbol button (tooltip "Before and after") in the Adjustments group: a pane in the canvas area (the split of the font preview `0077`, switchable to full view) shows the original, and the engraved result as the dots, at the operation's Interval, Scan angle and dither, side by side, with a Split switch (a draggable divider instead of two panes; Proposal) and zoom and pan locked together.
12. Given the engraved result in that view, then it is the same dots as the Toolpath view and the file (`0123` criteria 14 and 20) at the zoom shown: at 100 % one dot is Interval mm wide on screen at the document's scale; below one device pixel per dot it is the shaded dot area of `0123` criterion 20. Test: the dot count of a 10 x 10 mm region read from the view equals that of the job.
13. Given an edit of a step's field, then the view updates in the same frame at a reduced level while the pointer or the key is held (the working image's mip level that fits the pane, the dither on the dot grid of the visible part only) and settles to the exact dots within 500 ms of release for the working set of criterion 55; the exact dots are the ones the job will burn.
14. Given Resize, then it can only reduce the working image (there is no enlargement; an enlargement is the AI Upscale of criterion 49 or nothing); Resize never goes above the working limit of `0058` criterion 18. A smaller picture than the dot grid needs is the "coarser than the Interval" warning of `0123` criterion 35, which stays.
15. Given a picture whose stack is non-empty, then the canvas shows the adjusted image (greyscale or as the colour stands after the steps, as the Select view shows an image) with a small symbol on the object when the stack is non-empty and some steps are off. Nothing else about the object's look changes. (Proposal; UX decides.)
16. Given the histogram of Levels, then it is of the picture after the steps above it, live, from the working image at the mip level of the pane.

### Determinism, derived data, caching

17. Given the same image bytes, stack and Interval, then the adjusted image and the dots are identical on every platform (native and wasm): every step except Remove background (criterion 47) uses integer or fixed-point arithmetic only, as `0123` criterion 8 (Proposal: the Gaussian and bilateral kernels are integer tables; the CLAHE uses integer histograms; the curve is a 256-entry integer table). Test: golden outputs byte-identical native and wasm.
18. Given the work, then the adjusted image is derived: computed from the asset bytes of `0058` and the stack, cached in memory by the object's revision and dropped when it changes, never written into the project (the project does not grow by adjusting). The one possible exception is an AI mask (Frage 275).
19. Given the job (`0029`), then the laser-core job builder takes the adjusted image as the "working image" of `0123` criterion 7; the stack steps live in a `*-core` crate (Proposal: the pixel steps in `curvyo-image-core`, the stack and dot-scale integration in `curvyo-laser-core`; the architect decides in `adrs.md`); the job itself stays pixel-free and format-neutral (`0029` criterion 39), the G-code is as built.
20. Given two exports of the same project, then they are byte-identical (`0029` criterion 31).
21. Given an image whose bytes cannot be read, then the stack is kept and the checks warn as `0123` criterion 36; the steps run again when the bytes return.
22. Given an unfolded Image operation row and an image with a stack, then the row is unchanged by this story apart from the line of criterion 4 (Frage 272 C), the Preset and Strength of criteria 25 and 26 and the Image settings fields of `0123` as built. There are no machine settings in the Adjustments group.
23. Given the checks (`0029`), then a stack adds no block and at most one warn line: "1 step in the Adjustments of <image name> could not run." / "<n> steps ..." (unknown kind, damaged fields; a button selects the image).
24. Given Undo (`0020`) after any edit of the stack (including a preset pick, criterion 27, Make editable, criterion 29, and a mask, criterion 48), then the stack is exactly as before, the Before and after view follows, and no pixel is stored in the history beyond the stack's fields (and, for an AI mask, the asset reference).

### Presets and Strength (slice 1, the centre of the story)

25. Given a selected image with an Image operation, then the Image settings group of `0123` criterion 11 gains, **above** the Dither mode, a **Preset** pick: a list (a drop-down listbox, because seven entries with a description do not fit the always-visible segmented rule of the Dither pick; Proposal, UX decides) with these entries in this order, each with its one-line meaning as the second line of the row: **Photo (detailed)**, **Graphics (flat areas)**, **Black and white (hard edges)**, **Line art (outlines and handwriting)**, **Dot raster (round dots)**, **Newspaper (retro screen)**, **Expert** (manual: no preset). The tooltip of each entry names the Dither mode it sets, e.g. "Dither: Jarvis" on Photo; the closed pick shows the chosen name. A new Image operation has Preset **Expert**, so nothing changes against `0123` until the maker picks one (Proposal). The pick is keyboard operable (arrows move, Enter selects, Escape closes) and one selection is one undo step "Edit settings".
26. Given a Preset other than Expert, then below the pick a **Strength** control shows the one slider this story allows (the customer asked for it explicitly; `docs/design-system.md` gets this as its single exception to "no sliders", criterion 57): a slider with a percent field next to it (0 to 100 %, whole numbers, default 50, out-of-range refused with the range in the message as `0123` criterion 12), the left end labelled **Gentle** ("stays close to the original") and the right end **Strong** ("hard contrast and sharpening"). Keyboard: Left and Down minus 1, Right and Up plus 1, Page Up and Page Down plus and minus 10, Home 0, End 100; the field and the slider show the same value. While dragging, the dots (Before and after view, Toolpath view) update at the reduced level of criterion 13; one drag from press to release is one undo step "Edit settings". With Expert the Strength control is hidden (not disabled), and its value is kept.
27. Given a pick of a preset, then one undo step "Edit settings" writes into the Image operation: the Preset, the Dither mode and the fields of that mode (Threshold, Cell size, Angle) as the tables of criteria 32 to 37 say, and leaves everything else as it was (Strength, Interval, power, speed, Invert, Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Scan angle). The Dither mode and its fields stay ordinary fields afterwards: the maker may change them, which keeps the Preset and Strength (the derived steps of criterion 28 do not read the Dither mode). Expert writes nothing but the Preset.
28. Given a Preset other than Expert (Frage 279; default A), then the stored state is **Preset and Strength** (two small registers on the Image operation, `format_version` bump of criterion 8) and the picture steps are **derived** when the job is built, from Preset, Strength, the picture analysis (criterion 39) and the Interval; nothing derived is stored (criterion 18). The Adjustments group then shows the derived steps as read-only rows under the line "From preset <name>, Strength <n> %" with the resolved numbers, and a button **Make editable** (criterion 29). Changing the Interval, Strength or the picture recomputes the steps (the preset is live, not a one-time fill). With Frage 279 B the pick fills an ordinary stack once and nothing is derived.
29. Given **Make editable** (text button, tooltip "Turn the preset into steps you can edit"), then in one undo step the derived steps with their numbers resolved at that moment become the ordinary manual stack of criteria 1 to 5, Preset becomes Expert, Strength is kept; the dots before and after the click are identical byte for byte (test). Nothing else changes: the Dither mode and its fields stay.
30. Given a manual stack and a Preset other than Expert on the same picture, then the preset's derived steps run instead of the operation's manual tone and detail steps (those are listed switched off with the line "Preset in use", kept, and run again with Expert); the geometry steps on the image object (Crop, Resize, Remove background, criterion 4) always run first.
31. Given the **Strength mapping**, then every field marked "Gentle to Strong" in criteria 32 to 37 is v(S) = v0 + round-half-up((v100 - v0) x S / 100) in integer arithmetic on the field's step (for example Amount in whole %, Gamma target in whole %, clip in tenths of %), then clamped to the field's range of criterion 5; v0 is the value at Strength 0 (Gentle), v100 at 100 (Strong). Fields without two values are fixed; fields given as "k x Interval" use the Interval of the operation in mm, rounded to the field's step and clamped to its range (criterion 40).
32. Preset **Photo (detailed)** (for photos on wood, slate, anodised metal): Dither **Jarvis**. Steps in pipeline order: **Denoise** only when the noise estimate (criterion 39) is above 3.0: Strength 10 to 40 %, Detail kept 70 to 50 %; **Levels**: clip 0.2 to 2.0 % at each end of the histogram (Black and White point at these percentiles), Gamma chosen so the median grey lands at 50 to 42 % (Gamma = ln(target) / ln(median), clamped 0.10 to 4.00, two decimals); **Local contrast**: Radius 100 x Interval, Amount 10 to 90 %; **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 40 to 200 %, Threshold 0 %.
33. Preset **Graphics (flat areas)** (logos, clip art, scans of flat drawings): Dither **Ordered**, so flat areas of different greys stay different patterns and edges stay clean. Steps: **Denoise** (always): Strength 20 to 60 %, Detail kept 90 to 70 % (flattens JPEG speckle inside flat areas, keeps edges); **Levels**: clip 0.0 to 1.0 %, Gamma 1.00 (no median target); **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 0 to 100 %, Threshold 5 to 10 % (the threshold keeps flat areas flat). No Local contrast.
34. Preset **Black and white (hard edges)** (signs, stamps, scanned documents, photos to be burnt as a two-tone picture): Dither **Threshold** with Threshold 50 %. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 0 to 30 %, Detail kept 80 to 60 %; **Levels**: clip 0.5 to 3.0 %, Gamma chosen so the Otsu level (criterion 39) lands at 50 %; **Local contrast**: Radius 30 x Interval, Amount 0 to 60 % (evens uneven lighting); **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 50 to 200 %, Threshold 0 %.
35. Preset **Line art (outlines and handwriting)** (Frage 278; default A): Dither **Threshold** with Threshold 50 %. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 20 to 40 %, Detail kept 80 to 60 %; **Levels**: clip 0.5 to 2.0 %, Gamma 1.00; **Local contrast** (flattens paper and background): Radius 300 x Interval, Amount 50 to 100 %; **Contours** (criterion 45): Line width 3 x Interval, Sensitivity 30 to 80 %; **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 0 to 100 %, Threshold 0 %. With Frage 278 B the Contours step is dropped from this preset and Local contrast carries it.
36. Preset **Dot raster (round dots)** (Frage 277; default A): Dither **Halftone dots** (criterion 42) with Cell size 8 x Interval and Angle 45 degrees. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 20 to 40 %, Detail kept 60 %; **Levels**: clip 0.5 to 2.0 %, Gamma median target 50 to 45 %; **Local contrast**: Radius 100 x Interval, Amount 0 to 50 %; **Sharpen**: Radius 1 x Interval, Amount 0 to 80 %, Threshold 0 %.
37. Preset **Newspaper (retro screen)** (Frage 277; default A): Dither **Line screen** (criterion 43) with Line pitch 12 x Interval and Angle 45 degrees. Steps: **Denoise**: Strength 30 to 60 %, Detail kept 50 %; **Levels**: clip 2.0 to 6.0 %, Gamma median target 45 to 38 %. No Local contrast, no Sharpen (the coarse screen is the look).
38. Preset **Expert** derives nothing: the manual stack (criteria 1 to 5) and the Image settings of `0123` are as the maker set them.
39. Given the **picture analysis** every preset reads, then it is computed on the working image after the geometry steps (criterion 4), on a grey image (`0123` criterion 6) at the box-averaged power-of-two mip of at most 1 megapixel, in integer arithmetic only (criterion 17): the 256-bin histogram; the **percentile** p is the smallest grey whose cumulative share is at least p %; the **median** is the 50 % percentile; the **Otsu level** is the grey that maximises the between-class variance (ties to the lower grey); the **noise estimate** is 1.4826 x the median of the absolute 3 x 3 Laplacian response in grey levels (Proposal; the architect fixes the kernel). Test: a noise-free ramp gives a noise estimate below 1.0, the same ramp with uniform noise of plus and minus 8 gives above 3.0; a picture of only two greys gives the Otsu level between them.
40. Given the preset adapts to the **material and dot size**, then every radius, width, cell and pitch is a multiple of the operation's Interval (the "k x Interval" fields), so an Interval of 0.2 gives twice the mm of an Interval of 0.1 and the same number of dots; the preset reads no power or speed (the Grey mode, whose levels depend on the power range, is not used by any preset; Proposal). Test: the derived stack at Interval 0.1 and 0.2 differs exactly in the mm fields.
41. Given any preset, then it never crops, resizes, removes a background, changes the Invert, Brightness, Contrast and Gamma fields of `0123`, or touches a transparent area (alpha blends over white, `0123` criterion 6); it never changes the material's Image set (criterion 52).
42. Given Frage 277 (default A), then a new Dither mode **Halftone dots** (the dither picker of `0123` criterion 11 gains the segment "Halftone"; the mode is the **eighth** of nine) burns, per cell, one round dot that grows with the darkness of the cell: fields **Cell size** 0.20 to 5.00 mm (two decimals; in whole dots max(4, round(Cell size / Interval))) and **Angle** 0 to 89 degrees (whole numbers, default 45); the cell grid is rotated by the Angle around the item's origin; the dot threshold is a fixed integer spot-function table (a clustered round dot) over the cell; the fields appear in the group only for this mode; integer arithmetic only, deterministic on every platform (`0123` criterion 8). Golden dot map `halftone_dots_ramp` in `curvyo-laser-core/tests/fixtures/raster/`. With Frage 277 B the preset Dot raster is not offered in this story.
43. Given Frage 277 (default A), then a new Dither mode **Line screen** (the segment "Lines"; the **ninth** mode) burns, per cell row, one line of dots whose thickness grows with the darkness: fields **Line pitch** 0.20 to 5.00 mm (whole dots max(4, round(Pitch / Interval))) and **Angle** 0 to 89 degrees (default 45), the same rules as criterion 42. Golden dot map `line_screen_ramp`. With Frage 277 B the preset Newspaper is not offered in this story.
44. Given the nine Dither modes, then the always-visible segmented picker of `0123` criterion 11 keeps its rules (radiogroup, no drop-down, arrow keys move and select, "Floyd" tooltip Floyd-Steinberg) and gets a third row Halftone | Lines (UX decides the exact rows); the order of the group is Preset, Strength, Dither mode, the mode's fields, Brightness, Contrast, Gamma, Invert and the rest as built.
45. Given Frage 278 (default A), then a stack step **Contours** (one-line meaning: "Turns the picture into dark lines where it changes sharply from light to dark and white elsewhere, for line drawings and handwriting") has the fields **Line width** 0.05 to 2.00 mm (two decimals, default 3 x Interval) and **Sensitivity** 0 to 100 % (default 50); Proposal for the method (the architect confirms): a difference of two Gaussian blurs at sigma = Line width / 2 and 1.6 x that, keeping only the pixels darker than their surroundings, scaled by Sensitivity to grey 0 to 255 with white elsewhere, in integer tables (criterion 17); it sits after Local contrast and before Sharpen in the pipeline order (criterion 3). Golden `contours_dog` in criterion 54. With Frage 278 B this step is not built here.
46. Given a test-grid cell (`0112`), a duplicated or copied Image operation and a clone, then they carry the operation's Preset and Strength with the other Image settings (a grid cell uses them with its own Interval, so the preset rescales; Frage 276), and the material's Image set stores none (criterion 52).

### AI tools (slice 3; depends on Fragen 273, 274, 275; defaults below)

47. Given the default of Frage 273 (B: Remove background only) and Frage 274 (A: local, model file ships with the app), then a Remove background step (symbol, tooltip "Remove background") runs a small salient-object model on the device, nothing about the picture leaves the machine, no account, no telemetry (`0029` criterion 35); the result is a soft mask (8 bit) of the subject; masked-out pixels count as white (burn nothing). The step has Edge softness 0 to 20 % (whole numbers, default 5) and Threshold 0 to 100 % (default 50) so the maker can tighten or grow the subject; a brush to Keep or Erase parts by hand is an own follow-up (Could, slice 4, Frage 273).
48. Given the mask, then it is stored with the object as a derived asset in `assets/` (an 8-bit PNG, at most the working image's size, referenced by the step) together with the model name and version, so the job is reproducible without the model and a second device needs no AI to burn the same dots (Frage 275 A; default). With Frage 275 B the mask is recomputed from the model, and the step stores only the model version and a warning states that output may differ between runtimes. Test: a project with the mask burns byte-identical G-code on a machine where the model is not installed (A).
49. ## Open questions

**Frage 272 (IMGOPT-STACK): Where does the adjustment stack live?**
A: On the image object only; every operation and clone sees the same picture; the Preset and its steps cannot know the Interval or the material.
B: On the Image operation only; the same picture can be adjusted differently per material and Interval; the Select view shows the unadjusted picture; two operations on the same image keep two stacks.
C: Split: geometry and subject steps (Crop, Resize, Remove background) on the image object; everything that depends on the dot size or material (Preset, Strength, tone and detail steps, Sharpen, the tone fields of `0123`) on the Image operation.
Recommendation: C. Default if unanswered: C.

**Frage 273 (IMGOPT-AI-SCOPE): Which AI-assisted tools do you want?**
A: None in this story; Presets and the manual stack only, AI as a later story.
B: Background removal (subject mask) only, run on the device; the first model in the ranking is small (under 50 MB) and permissively licensed; later tools follow if the result is good.
C: B plus Upscale (more pixels for a small picture) and AI denoise.
(Depth or relief and "make engravable" enhancement stay later ideas under every option; say if you want one first.)
Recommendation: B (the biggest visible gain for photos on wood and the smallest model). Default if unanswered: B, slice 3 is built last.

**Frage 274 (IMGOPT-AI-PLACE): Where do AI tools run?**
A: On the device only; the model file ships with the app as a lazily loaded static asset from the app's own host (loaded on the first use, not at start); no picture, no thumbnail and no usage data ever leaves the machine; works offline after the first load.
B: On the device, with the model downloaded once from a separate Curvyo model host on the first use (the file comes in; nothing goes out; a network request exists).
C: In a cloud service when the maker presses the button (needs an account or an API key, the picture leaves the machine; opt-in per press with a notice; Curvyo pays or the maker brings a key; the result is not reproducible).
Recommendation: A. Default if unanswered: A (no network use for the picture; C is not built).

**Frage 275 (IMGOPT-AI-STORE): How does an AI result stay reproducible in the project file?**
A: The mask is stored with the project as a derived 8-bit asset (about 100 KB to 2 MB, PNG) next to the picture, with the model name and version; the job is identical on every device even without the model.
B: Only the model version is stored and the mask is recomputed on open (small file; the dots can differ between runtimes and model versions, so a job could change after an update).
Recommendation: A. Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 276 (IMGOPT-GRID): Should the Image test grid get an axis for the adjustment?**
A: No; the axes stay Power, Speed, Passes, Interval, and the dither choices as built; each cell uses the operation's Preset and Strength (criterion 46).
B: Yes, one more axis "Strength" (5 values: 0, 25, 50, 75, 100 %) so a maker can find the Strength that suits a wood.
C: Yes, one more axis "Preset" (the presets that fit the picture).
Recommendation: A for now (revisit after the demo). Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 277 (IMGOPT-HALFTONE): Are the two halftone Dither modes built in this story?** Dot raster (round dots) and Newspaper (retro screen) need two Dither modes that `0123` does not have: **Halftone dots** (round dots that grow with the darkness on a rotated grid, fields Cell size and Angle) and **Line screen** (lines that thicken with the darkness, fields Line pitch and Angle).
A: Yes, both in slice 1 (nine modes; all seven presets are in the demo).
B: Not now; slice 1 offers five presets plus Expert, the two halftone presets and modes follow as their own story.
C: Only Halftone dots now; Newspaper then uses Halftone dots at a coarse cell until Line screen follows.
Recommendation: A (the presets are the heart of the story and both modes are small, integer and golden-testable). Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 278 (IMGOPT-LINEART): Is Line art an edge-extraction step in the stack?** The preset Line art (outlines and handwriting) can either find the lines itself or only clean a picture that already is lines.
A: Yes, a new step **Contours** (dark lines where the picture changes sharply from light to dark, width and sensitivity as fields) in the stack, used by Line art; a photo can become a line drawing.
B: No new step; Line art = flatten the background (Local contrast), Levels, Threshold: right for pen drawings and handwriting on paper, no line drawing from a photo; Contours follows later if wanted.
C: Contours as a step the maker can add by hand, but not used by the Line art preset.
Recommendation: A. Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 279 (IMGOPT-PRESET-STORE): Is a preset live or a one-time fill?**
A: Live: the operation stores only Preset and Strength; the steps are derived whenever the picture, the Interval or the Strength changes (a new Interval re-scales the preset by itself); "Make editable" turns the current steps into an ordinary stack and sets Preset to Expert.
B: One-time fill: picking a preset (and moving Strength) writes an ordinary stack; the stack is the stored state; nothing follows a later Interval change; no Make editable needed, but the preset name and Strength are lost as soon as the maker edits a step.
Recommendation: A (the maker keeps the one-click look and the slider, and fine-tuning is one click away). Default if unanswered: A.

## Out of scope

- A Cut-out that follows the contour of a subject, mask or transparent background (later idea, from the same review).
- Colour management, colour editing, retouching, clone stamp, perspective or lens correction, rotate or skew (an image rotates and skews as an object).
- Upscaling, depth or relief maps, "make engravable" enhancement (unless Frage 273 C or a later story).
- An AI or automatic guess of the best preset for a picture, and a preset that chooses power or speed (the maker chooses the preset; the Interval is the only machine value a preset reads).
- Batch processing of many pictures, the maker's own presets saved to a library (a later idea; Make editable and Copy and Paste adjustments are the first steps).
- A cloud service, accounts, paid content and telemetry (not built under any default; Frage 274 C only if the customer asks).
- Changes to `0123`'s dithers, dot grid, material sets, G-code and the checks, except where this story says so.
- Mask painting (Keep and Erase brush): slice 4, Could, after Frage 273.
