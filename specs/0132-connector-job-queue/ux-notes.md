# 0132 Job list on the connector: UX notes

ux-engineer, 2026-11-23. Covers criteria 1 to 8, 11 and 14 of `specification.md` (criterion 12, Restart from line, is a later slice: these notes reserve no control for it). Checked against `specs/0118-machine-control/ux-notes.md` (footer, toolbar, one state line), `specs/0064-machine-connector/ux-notes.md` sections 7 and 8 (state words, numbers, name cut), `specs/0128-connector-api-and-ctl/ux-notes.md` section 8 (owner and member, Start slot), `specs/0134-send-and-start/ux-notes.md` (armed press, P13, P17), the built `MachineControls.tsx`, `sendArm.ts`, and `docs/design-system.md` (Machine controls row, Inline delete confirm, Armed action, List row, Hidden not disabled, Layers drag and keys). Customer rules: no popups, no dialog, no hint or tip labels (labels name a control or a state), no notice after a success, symbol buttons with tooltip and accessible name. Choices marked (Proposal) are vetoable at the demo; section 9 lists them as J1 to J12. Examples: owner app `Laptop`, member `Phone`, owner label `Marc`, jobs `sign.gcode`, `box.gcode`, `tags.gcode`.

## 1. Place
- In the Machine controls footer, between the job block (the current job, unchanged) and the toolbar: settings warning, job block, its failure line, **job list**, toolbar. The toolbar still never moves; the list grows the footer upward. The current job is the job block, so "current first" holds; the list holds the waiting jobs and, after a connector restart, the interrupted one.
- **Empty list:** the list and its header are absent; the footer is exactly as `0118`. No "No jobs" line.
- **Height:** at most 4 rows visible (164px with the header), then the rows scroll inside the list (J1). 244px content width; rows never scroll sideways.
- The job block's counts line adds the current job's sender after the name: "sign.gcode, Phone" (`--panel-muted-fg`; the name is cut in the middle first, then the sender at its end); "this app" for its own (J2).

## 2. Header and rows
- **Header row** 28px: "Job list" (12px, `--panel-muted-fg`, the list's name) left; owner only: **Move up** `ArrowUp` and **Move down** `ArrowDown`, 28px symbol buttons, a `role="toolbar"` named "Job order", acting on the selected row (J3). Greyed at the ends (`aria-disabled`, two-line tooltip with the cause "First in the list" / "Last in the list"; on the interrupted row both "Interrupted"). A member has no header buttons (hidden, not disabled).
- **Waiting row** 40px (44 coarse), the List row look (hover `--editor-accent-hover`; selected `--value-fill` plus the 3px `--accent` bar; focus ring inside). Left to right: grip `GripVertical` 16px muted (owner only), then two 12px lines: the name (`--toolbar-icon`, cut in the middle, full name in `title`) and "412 lines, 4.2 KiB, Phone" (`--panel-muted-fg`, `0064` number forms, sender cut at its end, "this app" for its own); at the right **Run next automatically** and **Remove** (section 3). The name column gets 160px on an owner row.
- **Own row while sending:** this app's send appears at once as the last row, second line "Sending" with `LoaderCircle` 12px; no actions until `JobAccepted`, then the normal row. A refusal removes it (section 5).
- **Interrupted row** (after a connector restart, criterion 6): first row, above the waiting ones, not in the order (no grip, no flag, never a drop target); second line "Interrupted at line 1,234, Phone" with `CircleAlert` and the line text in `--destructive`; owner: Remove only. No restart control in this slice. Start never starts it.
- **Selection** is local to this app, never shared: the first waiting row by default; a pointer press on a row (not on its buttons) or the arrow keys select; it follows its job through moves and pushes; when its job leaves, the row now at its place is selected (the last row at the end).

## 3. Row actions and rights (Frage 257 A, criteria 5, 11)
| Control | Owner | Member, own row | Member, other row |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grip, Move up, Move down | yes | absent | absent |
| Run next automatically `SkipForward`, toggle, `aria-pressed` | yes | yes | absent; when set, the glyph as a static mark (`--toolbar-icon`, not a button) |
| Remove `Trash2` (Inline delete confirm) | yes | yes | absent |
- Nothing a viewer may not do is drawn as a control: no greyed grip, no greyed trash. The flag's state is shown to everyone; only who may change it sees a button.
- **Run next automatically:** 28px toggle, name and tooltip "Run next automatically", accessible name "Run box.gcode next automatically"; on: `--toolbar-icon-active-bg` ground, `--toolbar-icon-active-fg` glyph; off: `--toolbar-icon` glyph. One press, not armed (setting the flag starts nothing; the machine still needs `--require-local-start` where set). `SkipForward` because the toolbar already speaks media (Play, Pause, Square); `Link2` is the linked clone's glyph (J4).
- **Remove:** the Inline delete confirm (`0081`): always visible on the rows that have it, not a Tab stop of its own (grid cell, section 6), first press arms (`--destructive-soft` row, filled `--destructive` square, white glyph), name "Remove box.gcode", armed name and tooltip "Press again to remove box.gcode", 400 ms guard, disarms after 4 s, on Escape, a press elsewhere, focus leaving the row, or when the armed job's place or presence changes. A removed waiting job cannot be undone, so it asks; the current job is not in the list and ends with Stop as built.

## 4. Order: drag, buttons, keys (criterion 3)
- **Drag** (owner, pointer events, not HTML5 drag and drop, as Layers): from the grip only, after 4px (coarse: a 400 ms hold); the row stays in place in the selected look, a 2px `--accent` insertion line shows where it lands, between waiting rows only (never above the interrupted row); the list scrolls when the pointer rests within 16px of its top or bottom. Release sends one move; Escape or a release outside the list cancels. No ghost, no animation.
- **Buttons and keys:** Move up and Move down move the selected job one place; `Alt+Up`, `Alt+Down` one place, `Alt+Home`, `Alt+End` to first or last (the Layers keys). The selection, and the focus, stay on the moved job, so a second press moves the same job again.
- **No optimistic order:** the row moves when the connector's list arrives (every client at once); presses in the list are ignored until that answer.
- **Stale position** (`Error { WrongState }`, also for Remove of a job that left meanwhile): the app reads the list again and shows it as the connector holds it; under the header one 12px line "The list changed" (`CircleAlert`, `--destructive`, alert, it answers a press) until the next press in the list or the next list push (J5).

## 5. Start, the chain, sending while busy (criteria 1, 4, 11)
- **Start on the first waiting job:** the toolbar's Start slot (no fifth button, no Start on a row, J6). With no current job or the current one Done and a waiting job in the list, Start is on; its name and tooltip read "Start box.gcode" (name cut at 40 characters), `aria-describedby` the first waiting row. A held Ready job keeps "Start" as built. One press, as `0064` Q14 and `0134` section 2 (Frage 301). A member's press waits for an owner (`0128` 8.2, 8.3); an owner's Confirm start is unchanged. When the first waiting job starts, its row leaves the list and the job block shows it.
- **Chain stopped:** when the chain is cancelled (criterion 11), one line under the header, Notice look (`TriangleAlert`, `--toolbar-icon`; the job block already shows the failure in `--destructive`), polite: "Chain stopped: Failed", "Chain stopped: Stopped", "Chain stopped: Alarm", "Chain stopped: Lost the controller", and when the job ended well but the controller is not ready for the next one "Chain stopped: Door", "Chain stopped: Check", "Chain stopped: Sleep" (the controller word) or "Chain stopped: Not ready" (laser mode off). It stays until the next Start or until no waiting job is left; the cleared flags show off.
- **Send while a job is held** (`0064` P13, `0125` S8 amended): Send to machine keeps the name "Send to machine" and never arms (`0134` "Idle, send only"); Send job... is on in Receiving (another app's), Ready, AwaitingLocalStart, Running, Paused and Done (Alarm, NoController as built). The result is the new row (section 2), never a visible notice; the sr-only polite region says "Added to the job list: sign.gcode, 3 of 3" (position among the waiting jobs). An `0134` P13 confirm that found the start gone lands here too: no "Not started" line. "Connector busy" no longer answers a send.
- **List full:** when the status shows 20 waiting jobs, Send to machine and Send job... are greyed with the cause "List full" (two-line tooltip, sr-only description). A refusal by the 256 MiB total reads "List full" in the failure line under the job block's line (`0134` P17 place), alert, once.

## 6. Keyboard and focus (J7)
- **Tab order of the footer:** Unlock (when shown), Job order toolbar (owner), the list (one Tab stop), the Machine toolbar.
- **The list is a `role="grid"`** named "Job list", one row per job (`aria-rowindex`; the interrupted row first), roving focus: Up, Down, Home, End select and focus rows; Left, Right move between the row and its buttons (Run next automatically, Remove); Enter or Space press the focused button; `Delete` on a row arms Remove, `Delete` or `Enter` on the armed row removes (Inline delete keys); Alt keys of section 4. A key that the viewer may not use does nothing. No global shortcut; Escape keeps its panel meanings after a disarm.
- After a removal the focus stays at the same row index (the next job, or the new last row); on an empty list it goes to the toolbar's current button. A row that leaves by a push takes the focus to the row now at its place.

## 7. Accessibility
- Row accessible text: "box.gcode, 412 lines, 4.2 KiB, from Phone, 2 of 3" plus ", runs next automatically" when flagged, ", interrupted at line 1,234" on that row; selected via `aria-selected`.
- Not live: pushes that reorder or add rows by other apps (a busy list would flood a screen reader). Live: this app's own add (polite, section 5), "Chain stopped" (polite), "The list changed" and "List full" (alert), a move by this app ("box.gcode, 1 of 3", polite).
- Not colour alone: interrupted and failure lines carry `CircleAlert`, the flag carries `aria-pressed` and words. Contrast: existing tokens only (`--toolbar-icon` 8.2:1, `--panel-muted-fg` 5.0:1, `--destructive` 4.8:1 on `--panel-bg`). Targets 28px, 36px coarse (rows 44px).

## 9. Decisions for veto (Proposal)
J1 4 visible rows, then an inner scroll. J2 the sender in the counts line. J3 Move up/down in the header, acting on the selection, not per row (per-row buttons put the neighbour's button under the pointer after a move, so a double press swaps back). J4 `SkipForward` for the flag. J5 "The list changed". J6 Start in the toolbar slot names the job. J7 grid with roving focus. J8 the interrupted row on top, outside the order. J9 the sending row at the end. J10 greyed Send with "List full" at 20 jobs. J11 no visible notice for an added job. J12 "Chain stopped" in the Notice look.

## 10. Question for the customer

B: an armed press in `--armed-accent` (`sendArm.ts` rules: 400 ms guard, 4 s, fresh status) only when Start promotes a waiting job; a held Ready job stays one press. C: Start armed always.
