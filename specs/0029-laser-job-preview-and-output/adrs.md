# ADRs for laser job preview and output

Status: Proposed (architect, 2026-11-16), amended 2026-11-17 after the customer's try of PR #371 (`ux-notes.md` §15 M9 to M16). Builds after `0113` (Done, PR #365). Two new ADRs: [ADR 0064](../../docs/adr/0064-laser-job-builder-and-output-writers.md) (job builder, gate, output writers with GRBL first; architect) and [ADR 0065](../../docs/adr/0065-laser-output-settings-and-air-assist.md) (stored values, `needs-customer`, accepted with the story PR). Decisions marked **(Proposal)** are the architect's and vetoable at the demo.

- [ADR 0064](../../docs/adr/0064-laser-job-builder-and-output-writers.md): `pub mod job` in `curvyo-laser-core` (no new crate, no dependency, `-io` untouched); one format-neutral integer-micrometre move list feeds every writer, Toolpath view, summary and slider; one entry point `write(format, &job)` over one writer module per format (`job_write_grbl.rs` only), no trait before a second format; flattening 0.0095 mm plus µm rounding (≤ 0.01 mm, criterion 17); inner first then nearest, brute-force defined, ties by stacking order; hatch, run-out at 1,000 mm/s²; gate type with one ordered, text-free `Cause` list shared by the operation rows and the checks (2026-11-17), its words in `job_gate_text.rs`; per-operation plan cache and the confirmed budgets; preview draw cache and time-based slider; export step, Lasered as a reserved non-step commit that undo keeps.
- [ADR 0065](../../docs/adr/0065-laser-output-settings-and-air-assist.md): `x.curvyo.laser.fixture_material` and `x.curvyo.laser.start` (`machine_origin` default and when absent, `head_position`, `home_then_origin`; replaces the first build's `.start_from` and `.home_first`, 2026-11-17) on the manufacturing node; a damaged `start` blocks Export; `air_assist` and `output_format` (closed enum, `grbl` only; a new value is a format change) in `ProfileValues`, the page copy and profile record format 6; project `format_version` 48, no migration.
- [ADR 0054](../../docs/adr/0054-laser-settings-and-test-grids-in-the-project-file.md) §2, §3: effective numbers are `resolve(..).run()`; the output refresh is `refresh(.., guard: false)` in the export step.
- [ADR 0056](../../docs/adr/0056-laser-operation-tools-in-the-project-file.md) §5 to §7: `op_options` drive the hatch; Z offset only with `motorised_z` from the page copy.
- [ADR 0057](../../docs/adr/0057-test-grid-object-with-derived-operations.md) §4, §5: the export writes `test_grid_lasered`; the hidden hook keeps working through the same reserved commit.
- [ADR 0058](../../docs/adr/0058-material-records-shared-across-lasers.md) §3, §7: the test record's `lasered`; a material's set per laser and kind feeds fixture jobs.
- [ADR 0046](../../docs/adr/0046-laser-operations-record.md) §3, §7 and [ADR 0052](../../docs/adr/0052-laser-fixture-jobs-in-the-project-file.md) / [ADR 0059](../../docs/adr/0059-fixture-sets-job-fixtures-and-pins-in-the-project-file.md): run order (`run_sequence`, `fixture_block`), the nearest rule, fixture job parts and pins' role jobs; no change.
- [ADR 0038](../../docs/adr/0038-machine-profile-records.md) §2, [ADR 0039](../../docs/adr/0039-machine-profile-copy-in-the-project-file.md) §1, §5, [ADR 0060](../../docs/adr/0060-fixture-sets-in-the-machine-profile-record.md) §3: amended by ADR 0065 §3 (`air_assist`, `output_format`).
- [ADR 0062](../../docs/adr/0062-synced-per-user-libraries.md): synced profile records of format 6 are skipped as newer on older builds; no change.
- [ADR 0035](../../docs/adr/0035-machine-connector-protocol.md): the exported GRBL file is the job `0064` will stream; nothing of it is built here, and a connector for another format is that connector's ADR.

## Feature-local decisions

- 2026-11-16: **Format.** Project `format_version` 48, profile record format 6, numbers taken at merge; the job itself has no format (criterion 32 confirmed).
- 2026-11-16: **Start from and Home first are page registers** (since 2026-11-17 the one register `start`), as criterion 38 says ("stored on the page"), not machine profile fields; only Air assist is in the profile.
- 2026-11-16: **Dependencies.** None added. Budget tests (criterion 36) live in `curvyo-editor-wasm/tests/cpu_time/` (it already has the thread clock); `curvyo-laser-core` gets no dev-dependency. `curvyo-geometry-core` exports `flatten_outline` and `direction_of_degrees` (architect review at build).
- 2026-11-16: **Slider maps time** (Proposal, ADR 0064 §10). Criterion 37's test ("slider at half the total length reads half the total time within 0.5 s") holds only where burn and travel run at one feed; the tester reads it as: at half the total time the drawn lengths up to the head equal the file's up to that move within 0.01 mm. For the product owner to align the test text.
- 2026-11-16: **Budget 36 (d)** is confirmed for edits that keep every plan valid (numbers, material, Output, switches); a geometry edit is bounded by 36 (a) (ADR 0064 §9). For the product owner to align the criterion text.
- 2026-11-16: **Head start for ordering** is the machine origin's page point in every Start value, so the plan never depends on Start and Machine origin equals the criterion 26 file.
- 2026-11-16: **Undo labels**: `export_job` "Export job file" (step); `set_fixture_material`, `set_start_from`, `set_home_first_on` / `_off` (steps); `mark_test_grid_lasered` becomes reserved and non-step (no history row); "Mark lasered" leaves `history_names.rs`.
- 2026-11-16: **Run-out and Z lines**: in the move list run-out is a laser-off move at the burn feed and Z a laser-off `Z` move; GRBL writes them as `G1 … S0` in both laser modes and `G1 Z<offset> F<n>` (GRBL refuses a `G1` with no feed set); neither is in the time estimate beyond its length at that feed (Proposal).
- 2026-11-16: **New module rule**: each new module stays under 500 lines and its doc names one responsibility; `job_plan.rs` is the likeliest to grow, split by kind (`job_plan_lines.rs`, `job_plan_hatch.rs`) before it passes the limit.
- 2026-11-16: **Output formats** (customer review of PR #370: "GRBL is only one solution, there can be other formats; it must be easy to extend"). The builder, move list, gate, preview and timeline are format-neutral; the move list carries positions in µm, burn, travel, run-out and Z moves, per block power in tenths of a percent, feed and passes, the start mode, air assist and the machine frame, nothing of a dialect. GRBL's S range (`max_power`), M3 or M4 (`laser_mode`), M8 and M9, `$H`, G90 or G91 and the comment header live in `job_write_grbl.rs` and its profile fields. Adding a format = one `OutputFormat` value in `machine_profile.rs` (profile record and project format bump), one `job_write_<format>.rs` with its arms in `job_write.rs`, goldens in `curvyo-laser-core/tests/fixtures/output/<format>/`, its `mode_line` and any `Cause` it needs in `job_gate.rs`, and the Machine block pick (ADR 0064 §12). No trait or option before the second format (`CLAUDE.md` §5).
- 2026-11-16: **Words.** User-facing text says "job file" where every format is meant ("Export job file" button, step and accessible name) and "GRBL" or "G-code" where the GRBL format is meant (the Machine line, the save dialog's "GRBL G-code" file type, the `.gcode` extension).

### Amendments after the customer's try of PR #371 (2026-11-17)

- **One Start register** (M14; ADR 0065 §1, §2). `x.curvyo.laser.start` with `machine_origin` (default, also when absent), `head_position`, `home_then_origin`; one closed enum `Start` in `output_settings.rs`; format 48 stays (unreleased), no migration; `start_from` and `home_first` are neither read nor written (a PR #371 file keeps them as unknown process registers). Steps `set_start_machine_origin`, `_head_position`, `_home_then_origin` ("Start: <option>"); the `set_start_from_*` and `set_home_first_*` labels go from document-core, `history/rows.rs`, `step_scope.rs` and `history_names.rs`.
- **Unknown Start value** (Proposal, vetoable): reads as Machine origin for the job and the preview (criterion 38's test), shows as damaged in the output group (no checked option) and blocks Export with the check `StartDamaged` ("Start is damaged. Pick one above.", button "Start" opens the pick); any pick clears it. Same reason as `output_format`: no file from a guessed start. **For the PO:** criterion 4 lists the blocks (a) to (d); this is a fifth, (e), and its text goes into UX §10.
- **Home, then machine origin** writes `$H` as the first line after the header, then exactly the Machine origin file (G90 from the machine origin, no work offset); the moves are those of Machine origin. Goldens `home_first.gcode` and `start_head_position.gcode` keep their names and bytes; only their `job_input` fixtures spell the start as one value. Document-core goldens `laser_output_v48.curvyo` and `laser_output_damaged_v48.curvyo` are rewritten for the one register.
- **No Head position warn** (M14): `Cause::HeadPosition` and its text go. In Head position the bed check measures from the job's own box: the work area is placed with its origin corner on the matching corner of the burn moves' box, and leaves outside it block as `OutsideBed` (Proposal; criterion 38 "measured from the job's own box").
- **One text-free gate list** (ADR 0064 §8 amendment). `Gate { machine, runs, issues }`; causes carry ids, kinds and numbers, never names or sentences; rows (ring, time, "–" for No settings) and checks read the same list. `job_gate_text.rs` is the only place that builds a sentence: a check as `before / button / after` with a names lookup from the session (no substring search), the numbers line, the machine line. `ModeLine` and `job_write::mode_line` are removed (no reader).
- **Machine line** (P30): "<profile> · <format>", " · air assist" when on; "Machine profile damaged" for a damaged copy. Work area, origin and laser mode stay in the Machine block.
- **Rings** (P26): `Block` carries `Ring::Operation(digit) | Ring::Fixture`, the digit passed by the session from the Operations list; the core counts no run numbers. Rows that do not run have no ring.
- **View JSON ownership** (M9, M10, M12): `session/output/` owns the job cache and is the only reader of `Gate`; it answers `row_runs()` (operations view: ring and time per operation id), `fixture_runs()` (Fixture jobs section: the pick and one run line per kind, header time) and `output_group_json` (machine line, Start, checks, total, Export). Line 3 (numbers, "typed") is built by the operations view through `job_numbers` and `job_gate_rows::numbers_lines`, the functions the job uses, so it shows on every row, Output on or off, without a job. The Output tab view, `PanelTab::Output` and `PanelContent::Output` are removed.
- **Laziness** (ADR 0064 §9): the job is built while a laser page's Operations tab is shown or its Toolpath view is pressed; budget 36 e reads "the Operations tab with the output group opens in 100 ms" (M12, for the PO).
- **`0101` summary** (M11, P28): its count lines are the gate's `NotAssigned`, `HiddenRuns`, `OutputOff` checks from `GateFacts` (same operations index); the operations view JSON drops its `summary` counts; "Runs N operations, M items." is the total's second line, from the same `summarize`. A change of accepted `0101` behaviour that the customer asked for (one compact tab); no new question.
- **Play readout** (M13): time only; the session's play view drops the lengths, the timeline keeps them for the total and the tests.
- **No format or dependency change** beyond ADR 0065's: no crate, no dependency, format 48 and profile record format 6 unchanged.
