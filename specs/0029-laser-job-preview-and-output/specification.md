# Laser job preview and output: a toolpath and time preview, a machine and material gate, and job file export (GRBL G-code first) from a laser page

Status: Done (PR #371)
Priority: Must
Origin: Customer (R-MFG-003, R-MFG-LASER-001, R-SYS-008; MVP slice 16, the capstone of epic `0063`, "Settings and output"). Everything marked **(Proposal)** is the product owner's and vetoable at the demo. Fragen 219 to 226 were answered by the customer on 2026-11-16 (see "Answered customer questions"); the customer added a homing request to Frage 224. The customer's review of PR #370 (2026-11-16) made the output format a field of the machine profile with GRBL as its first value and the job format-neutral (criterion 39).

## User value

As a maker with a laser (a GRBL controller first) I want to see on my laser page exactly what will run, in which order and for how long, to be told which machine and which material numbers every step will use before anything is written, and to export one job file in my machine's format that cuts, scores and engraves my design, so that a design drawn in Curvyo reaches my laser without another program in between.

**What other tools do.** LightBurn previews in a separate window with a play slider and estimates time; its machine and layer settings are checked by the maker's memory, not by a gate; a wrong device or a layer with the wrong power goes into the file without a word. Inkscape's laser plug-ins write G-code from colours and ask for power in a dialog; they have no preview of order or time. xTool and Glowforge are tied to their own machines. **We do better:** the preview is the page itself (no second window), every operation's row shows its run number, its time and its numbers (and says "typed" where the maker overrode one), the checks next to the Export button name the machine and state what blocks and what only warns, and the numbers are the ones the maker already set in operations and materials, so the export dialog has no settings at all.

## Words (shown in the UI)

- **Output group:** the last group of the Operations tab of a laser page, after the Fixture jobs section (`0103`): the machine line, the Start pick, the checks, the total and the Export button. There is no Output tab (customer, 2026-11-17: one compact tab). Pages of other kinds have none.
- **Job:** everything that runs when the page is output: the running fixture jobs, then the work operations, as `0103` criterion 14 and `0101` criterion 7 define. Nothing else is in a job.
- **Toolpath view:** a toggle at the page name on the canvas (next to the Operations view toggle of `0111`), symbol `Route`, tooltip and accessible name "Toolpath view". Pressed: the page shows the job's moves instead of the drawing's style.
- **Burn move:** a move with the laser on. **Travel move:** a move with the laser off. In the Toolpath view a burn move is drawn by the look of its operation's kind (`0101` criterion 18: Cut red, Score dotted ink, Engrave pale orange), a travel move as a thin dashed ink line, the start of each operation with a numbered ring (ink, the operation's place in the run order) and a small arrowhead at the start of each burn path. Nothing is told by colour alone.
- **Checks:** the list of findings in the output group: a line per block or warn, with a symbol and a text (the name of this list was "Gate" until 2026-11-17). Each operation's numbers are not a check: they stand in its row (criterion 2).
- **Block / Warn:** a **block** is a condition that disables the Export button until the maker removes its cause; a **warn** is shown as a check line with a symbol and a text and never disables Export.
- **Effective numbers:** an operation's power, speed, passes (and Interval) after the rule of ADR 0054 §2 and `0028` "Where settings live" 5: the live material's numbers, or the stored ones when the material is not found, with the operation's typed overrides applied over them.
- **Export:** writing the job as one job file in the output format of the page's machine (GRBL G-code in this slice); it also records that the job was output (criterion 28).
- **Output format:** the file format the page's machine reads, a field of the machine profile (criterion 39); GRBL is its only value in this slice. **Job file:** the exported file, in that format.

## Acceptance criteria

"Laser page", "item", "leaf", "Not assigned", "nearest rule", "Cuts last" are as in `0101`; "fixture job" and "Runs first" as in `0103`; "operation-only settings" and "Motorised Z axis" as in `0111`; "test grid", "Lasered" as in `0112`; "set", "pin", "Centre mark" as in `0113`. The page's machine is its profile copy (`0039`). All numbers in the criteria are mm unless a unit is named.

### The Output tab and the gate

1. Given a laser page whose extension is on, then its Operations tab holds, after the list and the Fixture jobs section (`0103`), the output group in this order: the machine line, the Start pick (criterion 38), the checks, the total and the Export button (symbol `Download`, tooltip and accessible name "Export job file"). There is no Output tab and no `FileOutput` entry (customer, 2026-11-17). A page of another kind, and a "Laser (off)" page, have no output group. The group has no number field of the job; every number comes from operations, materials and the machine profile; its only controls are the fixture material pick (Frage 221 B, criterion 2), the Start pick (criterion 38) and the Toolpath scrub bar's own slider on the canvas (criterion 37) (Proposal, "no machine settings clutter").
2. Given the Operations tab of a laser page, then every operation that runs (Output on, a readable kind, at least one item) is a numbered row, in run order, with three lines: line 1 as built (glyph, name, Show, Output and the row buttons); line 2 the run ring with the row's digit (the number its ring in the Toolpath view shows), the kind and item count, and at the right that run's time (criterion 10; "-" with No settings); line 3 the effective numbers "<power> %, <speed> mm/min, <passes> x" plus, for Engrave and Engrave + Outline, "<interval> mm", each overridden number followed by the word "typed", or "No settings". Lines 2 and 3 show on every row, folded or unfolded; the material name, "Own numbers" and the live or stored state show only in the unfolded row. A row that does not run keeps its plain digit and has no time. The machine line of the output group reads "<profile name> · <output format>" ("· GRBL", criterion 39) and ends with " · air assist" when the profile's Air assist is on (criterion 25); the work area, the origin corner and the laser mode with its S range stay in the Machine block of the Document tab. At the top of the Fixture jobs section sit the single pick "Fixture jobs run with" (a material of the library, stored on the page, Frage 221 B) and, for a running fixture job, one run line per kind it runs (run ring with the fixture glyph, the kind, its time, and the numbers of that material's set for the page's laser); the fixture runs take the first run numbers (one per kind). Without a pick, or when the material has no set for this laser and that kind, a running fixture job blocks Export (criterion 4 a).
3. Given the Operations tab, then every row's numbers and time, the machine line, the checks and the total are recomputed in the same frame as any change of an operation, a material, the machine, the Output switches or the Start pick, and the numbers are the ones the operation's unfolded row shows (`0111` criterion 18). Test: change Cut 1's typed power, line 3 of its row changes at once.
4. Given a condition that blocks (Frage 220 A: **(a)** an operation or fixture job that runs and has no power or no speed ("No settings"; a running fixture job with no material pick or no set for its kind counts, Frage 221 B); **(b)** burn geometry outside the work area, by the box of the geometry that would run, with a tolerance of 0.01 mm; **(c)** a machine profile copy that is damaged or unreadable (an output format this build does not know counts, criterion 39); **(d)** nothing at all runs; **(e)** a Start value this build does not know, "Start is damaged. Pick one above.", its button opens the Start pick (ADR 0065; read as Machine origin, never guessed into a file)), then the checks show one line per cause with the symbol `OctagonX` and a text that names it ("Cut 2 has no power and speed.", "3 items lie outside the 400 x 300 mm bed."), the Export button is disabled with its tooltip naming the first cause, and the line's count or name is a button that selects the offending items, or, for an operation, selects and unfolds its row in the same tab. Geometry that would not run (Not assigned, Output off) never blocks.
5. Given a condition that warns (Frage 220 A: Generic laser as the machine; a material not found, using stored numbers; a material with no set for this laser and kind; a typed override of a material number; a speed above the machine's maximum feed or a Z offset with no motorised Z axis (clamped or ignored, criterion 21); items Not assigned; items that run while hidden; operations with Output off; a Cut-out of an item with no closed contour), then the checks show one line per cause with the symbol `TriangleAlert` and a text, and Export stays enabled. Each count is a button that selects the items; the counts of items Not assigned, items that run while hidden and operations with Output off are these checks (the count lines of the `0101` summary are merged into them, criterion 10). The lines are in a fixed order: blocks first, then warns, each group in the order of this criterion.
6. Given no machine profile is selected on the page (the profile copy is absent), then the page cannot be a laser page (`0098`), so this case is the block (c); given the built-in Generic laser, then it is a profile and warns only (criterion 5; R-SYS-008: the machine line states the machine and the export refuses with none).
7. Given the Operations tab with nothing to run, then the checks read "Nothing runs. Assign items to an operation." (the text of `0101` criterion 17, a block, 4 d), the total is absent and Export is disabled.

### Preview

8. Given a laser page, then the Toolpath view toggle sits at the page name next to the Operations view toggle; the state is per laser page, session state, not saved, not a history step. Pressed, the page draws the job as in Words (Toolpath view) and nothing else of the drawing (the items' own style is hidden, fixtures and the grid stay as built). While pressed the page is read as in `0111` (select, move and the edit tools still work); a change of the drawing or an operation updates the view live (Frage 219 C); the play slider of criterion 37 sits with it.
9. Given the Toolpath view and a job, then the burn moves are exactly the moves the export writes (one function builds both, so the view and the file cannot differ), drawn in run order with the numbered ring at the start of each operation, an arrowhead at the start of each burn path and the travel moves between them. Test: the sum of the burn and travel lengths read from the view equals the lengths read from the exported file within 0.01 mm.
10. Given the total in the output group, then it reads "About <h> h <m> min" ("About 12 min 40 s" below an hour, "About 1 h 5 min" above) and under it "Runs <N> operations, <M> items." (the words of `0101` criterion 17; M counts the output-page leaves that hold an assignment and run, each once; "<M> items" is a text button that selects those items, as the `0101` count buttons; shown only when a work operation runs), then the fixture line of `0103` criterion 17, "1 fixture job runs." or "<F> fixture jobs run." (shown only when a fixture job runs; the count is a text button that selects the fixtures of those jobs), and "Burn <length> · travel <length>" (m with two decimals above 1 m, else mm, whole numbers); it is absent while nothing runs. Each run's own time stands in its row's line 2 (criterion 2), so the total is not a second list of operations. The estimate is the burn length divided by the programmed feed, times passes, plus the travel length divided by the machine's maximum feed rate; no acceleration is modelled (Proposal: it runs short, the text says "About"; an acceleration field in the profile is later). The numbers are recomputed in the same frame as the checks. Test: a 100 mm Cut line at 600 mm/min, 2 passes, travel 100 mm at 6,000 mm/min reads "About 21 s" (200 mm / 600 mm/min = 20 s, plus 1 s). A page where only one fixture job runs reads the time, "1 fixture job runs." and the lengths, with no "Runs 0 operations" line.
11. Given a hidden item that runs (`0101` criterion 13), then the Toolpath view draws it anyway (what is output is what is shown); given Show off on an operation (`0101` criterion 15), then the Toolpath view still draws its moves (Show is view of the drawing, the preview shows the job).
12. Given the Toolpath view pressed on a page with a job of 1,000 items, then every pointer move (pan, zoom) takes within 16 ms; rebuilding the job after a change is within the budget of criterion 36.

### What each kind writes

13. Given a leaf in a Cut operation, then every outline runs once per pass as burn moves along the flattened curve (criterion 17), a closed outline returning to its first point, an open path along its own direction, as `0101` criterion 19 says; Score the same, in the Marking section; an image in Cut or Score writes its frame as a line (`0101` criterion 19, 20). Fill, stroke, colour and dash never count (`0101` criterion 24).
14. Given a leaf in an Engrave operation, then the closed areas run as parallel burn lines Interval apart (the Interval of the operation, `0111` criterion 19) at the Scan angle (0 degrees: lines parallel to the bed's X axis, positive angles turn the lines counter-clockwise as seen on the page, `0111` criterion 20), clipped to the area by the leaf's fill rule (`0101` criterion 21); an area with holes leaves its holes empty; open outlines of a leaf with a closed outline are closed by a straight line (`0101` criterion 21); a leaf whose outlines are all open runs as lines (140 A). The first line sits half an Interval inside the area's box edge (Proposal).
15. Given Bidirectional fill on (default), then the burn lines alternate direction line by line and the travel between two lines of one area is the shortest connection; off, every line runs in the same direction with a laser-off travel back. Given Crosshatch on, then the area is hatched a second time at the Scan angle plus 90 degrees after the first hatch, in the same operation and pass. Given Overscanning, then each burn line is extended on both ends by the stated percentage of the run-out the speed needs (`0111` Words), with the laser off over the extension, and the laser switches on and off exactly at the area's edge (Proposal for the run-out length: the distance to reach the feed at 1,000 mm/s2, stated in `adrs.md`, because the profile holds no acceleration).
16. Given Engrave + Outline, then the Engrave hatch of criterion 14 runs, then every closed outline as a line (criterion 13), the hatch with the Engrave set and the outline with the Score set (`0028` criterion 25); given Cut-out, then the outer contour of each item runs once per pass as one closed line (`0101` criterion 23, with its margin).
17. Given a curve, then it is written as straight segments with a deviation of at most 0.01 mm from the exact curve (Proposal; no arcs in any format, in GRBL no G2 or G3, so every controller runs the file the same way); a placed page, a clone and an item with a transform write its geometry as it stands on the laser page.
18. Given an image in an Engrave or Engrave + Outline operation, then (Frage 226 A, customer 2026-11-16: silently ignored) the image is skipped completely: it writes no pixels, no frame and no move in the Toolpath view, the gate shows no warn line for it, and an Engrave + Outline operation writes only the outlines of its other items. A later feature handles raster engraving. Images in Cut and Score still write their frame (criterion 13). *Amended by `0123` (2026-11-19; Frage 271 A replaces Frage 226 A): no longer true. An image in Cut, Score, Cut-out, Engrave and Engrave + Outline is the rectangle of its frame and runs as a rectangle would (Cut, Score and Cut-out the closed line, Engrave the hatch, Engrave + Outline hatch and line), still without pixels and without a warn line; only an image in the new kind Image engraves as rows of dots (`0123` criteria 1, 46, 47; ADR 0069).*
19. Given the operation-only settings of `0111` and a Z offset: a Z offset on an operation (only with the motorised Z axis, `0111` criterion 21) is a laser-off move to Z <offset> at the operation's start and back to Z 0 after its last pass (GRBL: absolute `G1 Z<offset>` and `G1 Z0`); with the capability off nothing is written and the gate warns once when an offset is stored (criterion 5). The file never changes Z beyond these two moves.

### Order and passes

20. Given the job, then the order is: the running fixture jobs first (Frage 176 A of `0103`, "Runs first"; inside that block Marking before Cutting while Cuts last is on, list order while off), then the work operations in list order (`0101` criterion 5, 7); an item in several operations runs in each (`0101` criterion 7). Test: Engrave 1, Cut 1 and a fixture job's Score produce, in the file, the Score first, then Engrave 1, then Cut 1.
21. Given the passes of an operation (1 to 20, `0028` criterion 24), then the operation's whole set of paths is written once per pass in the same order (Frage 223 A: pass 1 of all paths, then pass 2); with a speed above the machine's maximum feed rate the written feed is the maximum and the gate warns.
22. Given the order of the paths inside one operation (Frage 225 A), then a path that lies inside the outline of another path of the same operation runs before the path that surrounds it (inner shapes, holes and islands before their outlines, to any depth), and among paths of equal depth the next path is the one whose nearest start point is closest to the head's last position (nearest neighbour; a closed path may start at any of its nodes, an open path at either end); the choice is deterministic: ties go to the lower stacking order. Test: a rectangle with a circular hole and a text inside the hole cuts the text, then the hole, then the rectangle.
23. Given a Cut-out and a Cut in one job with Cuts last on, then the cutting operations run after every marking operation, so no cut loosens a part before it is marked (`0063` criterion 5).

### The file

24. Given a job file of any output format, then it holds no date, no time, no path of the maker, no user name, no application version and no random number, so the same project and settings always write byte-identical bytes. Given the GRBL format, then the file holds in this order: a comment header (job name, machine name, the operations in run order with their numbers), when Start is Home, then machine origin (criterion 38), `$H` as the first line after the header, then `G21` (mm), `G90` (absolute) (`G91` relative moves with Start Head position, criterion 38), `G17`, the laser-off and S0 start (`M5` then `M4 S0` or `M3 S0` by criterion 25), then the moves, then `M5` and the end (criterion 25). Lines end with LF; coordinates have 3 decimals, S whole numbers, F whole numbers.
25. Given the GRBL format and the S value, then S = round(power % x S-max / 100) with the S-max of the machine profile (`0026`); a burn move carries F and S only where they change (`G1 X.. Y.. F.. S..`), a travel move is `G0` with the laser off. Given laser mode on (M4), then S0 may be written in a burn line's end; given M3, travel moves are preceded by `M5` and burn moves by `M3 S<n>`. Air assist (Frage 222 B, customer 2026-11-16): the machine profile has a field "Air assist", off by default; on, the job switches the air on before the first burn move and off at the end, for every job on that machine (GRBL: `M8` and `M9`); off writes neither. The end of the job: laser off, air off when air assist is on, and a rapid back to the job's origin (Proposal: the head returns to where the job started, laser off); GRBL writes `M5`, `M9` and `G0 X0 Y0` in absolute mode, or the inverse of the sum of moves in relative mode.
26. Given the origin corner of the machine profile (`0026`), then file coordinates are the page's coordinates measured from that corner with axes pointing into the bed (bottom-left: Y up, as the page; top-left: Y down; right corners: X from the right), so every coordinate of the work area is zero or positive and the file's origin is the corner the machine homes to (Frage 224 B). With Start on Machine origin, no homing and no work offset are written (GRBL: no `G10`, `G92` or `G54`); Head position and Home, then machine origin are criterion 38. Test: the same page with the four corners gives four files whose coordinates are mirrored, each starting inside the bed.
27. Given Export, then it works as the SVG export of `0024` criterion 4: the OS save dialog (a download in the browser build) opens with the default name "<project file name without extension> - <page name>.<extension of the output format>" ("Untitled - Laser 1.gcode" for GRBL; the dialog's file type is the format's, "GRBL G-code"; Proposal; characters not allowed in file names are replaced by "-"), one notice "Exported <name>." (browser: "Downloaded <name>.") shows for 3 s; when fixture jobs were switched off (criterion 28 a) the same notice has a second line "Output turned off on N fixture jobs." and shows for 5 s, so one export never shows two notices, and a write failure shows the existing error dialog with the system's reason and writes nothing. Cancelling the dialog does nothing and writes no history step (criterion 28).

### After the output

28. Given a successful export, then in one undo step "Export job file": (a) every fixture job whose Output was on is switched off and shows "Output off" (`0103` criterion 6, Frage 178 B), with the second notice line of criterion 27 when N is above 0 (the fixture pick and run lines leave the Fixture jobs section; the operation rings keep their numbers, a ring is its row's digit); the Centre mark and Diameters jobs of pins and screws (`0113` criterion 29) are such jobs; (b) the stored numbers beside every operation's material link are refreshed, never the overrides (`0028` "Where settings live" 5); (c) every test grid that has at least one operation in the file gets the state **Lasered** in its material (`0112` criterion 11), so a later change of the grid or of its operations by hand gives it a new test id. Undo restores (a) and (b); **Lasered stays** (Proposal: it records that a file was written, not a project state, as `0112` keeps tests outside the project). The Lasered state is set by this export only; the internal test hook of `0112` stays for tests.
29. Given an export that wrote no operation of a grid (its operations had Output off), then that grid is not Lasered and its id stays.
30. Given a project with test grids exported, then the next export of an unchanged grid keeps its id (same grid, nothing changed) and a changed one has the new id (`0112` criterion 11); the GRBL file's comment header names the test id in the label operation's name.

### Scrub, start and homing

37. Given the Toolpath view and a job (Frage 219 C, customer 2026-11-16), then a bar below the page holds a play control (one button, symbol `Play`, becomes `Pause` while running, symbol-only, tooltip and accessible name "Play" and "Pause"), one plain single slider over the whole run, and next to it a readout "<elapsed> of <total>" (time only, as in criterion 10; no length, customer 2026-11-17). Moving the slider draws the moves up to that point at full weight and the rest thin and pale (never told by colour alone, with a ring at the head's position); Play animates the head along the run from the slider's position, the whole job in 10 s whatever its length (Proposal), stops at the end and returns to the start on the next press; the arrow keys step the slider by 1 % of the run. The slider is the one slider allowed here (other fields stay plain, units inside); the position is session state, not saved, not a history step. The slider runs over the job's time (ADR 0064 §10), so slow moves take more of it. Test: slider at half reads half the total time within 0.5 s, and the drawn burn and travel lengths up to the head, read from the view and not from the readout, equal the file's up to that move within 0.01 mm.
38. Given the output group (Frage 224 B and the customer's homing request, customer 2026-11-16; one pick, customer 2026-11-17; the details are a Proposal, vetoable), then it holds ONE pick "Start" stored on the page, with three values: **Machine origin** (default, also when the register is absent), **Head position** and **Home, then machine origin**. Head position: the file is written in relative moves (GRBL: `G91`), the job's first point is placed where the head stands when the file starts, and the end returns to that point; the origin corner and the work area check of criterion 4 b are measured from the job's own box; there is no warning (the text "Starts where the head stands; the bed is not checked." is removed). ### Output formats

39. Given a machine profile (the library record and the page copy), then it holds the field **Output format**, a closed list with one value, GRBL, in this slice (the customer's review of PR #370, 2026-11-16; ADR 0065 §3). The Machine block of the Document tab shows it as a read-only line "Output: GRBL" while the list has one value, and as a pick with one undo step once it has more (Proposal). The job, the order, the Toolpath view, the total, the time, the play slider and the check rules of criteria 4 to 7 are the same for every format; the format gives only the file's bytes, its extension and file type (criterion 27), its name in the machine line (criterion 2) and any check of its own. Adding a format is one writer module, one value of the list, its golden files and its check hooks, nothing else (ADR 0064 §12). Test: the goldens of criterion 31 are the bytes of `write(GRBL, job)`; the tests of criteria 9, 10 and 37 use no format; a page copy with an unknown output format blocks (4 c) and writes nothing.

### The Machine block

40. ## Out of scope

- Sending the job to a machine, streaming, status, pause and stop, framing on a connected laser (`0064`); a frame file or a frame preview on the machine.
- A second output format (Marlin, Ruida, LinuxCNC and others) and the Output format pick that comes with it; the field itself, with GRBL as its one value, and the one place each format plugs in are in scope (criterion 39).
- A focus test, auto-focus, rotary and any second axis; the camera; a user origin set on the page (Frage 224 C; not chosen).
- A live Home button on the page that moves the connected machine (customer request 2026-11-16: needs the device connector `0064`; later list). The file's `$H` line (Home first, criterion 38) is in scope, the button is not.
- Image engraving and the warning for images in Engrave (criterion 18; silently ignored now, a later feature handles raster engraving).
- Image engraving of pixels, dithering, a power ramp and Offset fill (Frage 226; noted for later in `0063`); Dot mode.
- A full simulation (R-MFG-009) with machine dynamics, a 3D view or a drawn head model: the scrub and play bar of criterion 37 is in scope; an acceleration model and a more exact time estimate; a 3D view.
- Tabs and bridges for Cut-out, lead-in and lead-out of cuts, kerf compensation, cut order by the maker's drag in the preview.
- Material or operation settings in the export (there is no export dialog); a job file editor; importing G-code or other job files.
- Other machine kinds (cutting plotter, CNC, embroidery); a machine abstraction before the second family (`CLAUDE.md` §6).
- Library or sync of the exported file; remembering the last export folder across machines.
