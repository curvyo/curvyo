07 · Pin and Screw: grids and their jobs

New kind Pin: one round dowel, a diameter (default 6 mm, Proposal). Pins and screws are single or gridded: Columns and Rows, each with its spacing, evenly spaced from the first one; each axis is one unit (count and spacing in one row, wrapping as a whole), and Spacing is hidden while its count is 1. The add bars carry symbols only, as the Select bar's kind buttons; the tooltip and the accessible name are the kind's word. Jobs (pin and screw): two toggles with symbol and word. Centre mark (Frage 209 A: a small cross in a circle at every centre, Size settable, default 4 mm, shown only while the toggle is on) is engraved so you can laser on it and drill or tap; Diameters engraves the pin's circle, or the screw's head and thread circles.
