06 · Fixtures tab: machine sets and job fixtures

Fixtures leave the Document tab and get their own tab on a laser page (strip: after Materials, Holder symbol, dashed = new). Two sections. Machine sets: the fixture sets of the page's machine (Frage 207 B: a set is a named group of fixtures, a page turns on any number of them). A set row is folded by default: chevron, name, line 2 with its kinds and count; at the right "On this page" (check box), armed trash, arrows; the row drags to reorder; double-click or F2 renames in place. Unfolded, a set holds its add bar (symbols only) and its fixture rows as 0102 has them. Job fixtures: fixtures of this page only, not of the machine (pins, a screw, a holder for this job); each row has Lock (08). "Apply fixtures from page…" copies another laser page's job fixtures here (09). The page's active sets are the checked rows (heading count "2 of 3 on this page") and, on the canvas, the only sets drawn.
