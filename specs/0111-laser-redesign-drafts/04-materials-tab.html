04 · Materials tab

Materials leave the Operations tab and get their own tab (strip: after Operations, dashed = new). A material is one record shared by every laser; under it each laser has its own sets, one per kind (Cut, Score, Engrave), each typed by hand or taken from a test. A press on a material row selects it and unfolds it in place, like an operation row. Rename: double-click or F2. Delete: the armed trash.
