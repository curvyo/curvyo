# ADRs for operation always has numbers

Status: Proposed (architect, 2026-11-24). Built with the defaults of Fragen 337 to 340 (all A). No new ADR: the decisions below amend accepted ADRs by notes (the pattern of ADR 0056 and 0065), because they add two fields to an existing map and record and change the meaning of an absent register; no crate, dependency, trait or published API shape is new. `needs-customer` (file format, persistence): accepted with the story PR, on the customer's Frage 336 A and the defaults of 338 and 340. Decisions marked **(Proposal)** are the architect's and vetoable at the demo.

- [ADR 0038](../../docs/adr/0038-machine-profile-records.md) §2: the profile record gains the default pair, record format 7 (decision 2). Note added.
- [ADR 0039](../../docs/adr/0039-machine-profile-copy-in-the-project-file.md) §1, §3, §5: `mfg_profile` gains the default pair, read per field, never rewritten on read, compared by "Differs from profile" (decision 1). Note added.
- [ADR 0054](../../docs/adr/0054-laser-settings-and-test-grids-in-the-project-file.md) §1 to §3: an absent `op_settings` no longer means No settings; it reads as own numbers from the copy's pair, and three writes store it (decisions 3 to 5). Note added.
- [ADR 0064](../../docs/adr/0064-laser-job-builder-and-output-writers.md) §3, §8: `NoSettings` no longer blocks; the cause goes (decision 6). Note added.
- [ADR 0033](../../docs/adr/0033-manufacturing-data-in-the-project-file.md) §3, §4, §7: plain values in document-core, nothing rewritten on read, the bump rule. Reused unchanged.
- [ADR 0057](../../docs/adr/0057-test-grid-object-with-derived-operations.md): derived grid operations are created with `op_settings`; unchanged. Legacy format-43 `op_grid` rows (ADR 0054 §4) keep their meaning (decision 3).
- [ADR 0014](../../docs/adr/0014-history-undo-and-branches.md): one commit is one undo step; the reserved non-step commit of ADR 0042 §9.

## Feature-local decisions (2026-11-24)

1. **The default pair in the profile values.** `curvyo-laser-core/src/machine_profile.rs` gains `DefaultNumbers { power: PowerPercent, speed: FeedRate }` (plain struct, no trait) and `ProfileValues.defaults: DefaultNumbers`. Built-in: **10.0 %, 1,000 mm/min** (Frage 337 A); Generic laser (code only, ADR 0038) has exactly these. Invariant: `defaults.speed <= max_feed`, held by every path that builds values: a read takes `min(read or built-in, max_feed)`; an edit of Max feed below the Default speed lowers it **in the same step** `edit_max_feed` (criterion 2). `DefaultNumbers::settings()` gives the set with passes 1. "Differs from profile" needs no code: it compares `ProfileValues`.
   **Copy (`mfg_profile`)**: two keys `default_power_pct` (number, 1 to 100, at most one decimal, as `op_settings.power_pct`) and `default_speed_mm_min` (integer 1 to 100,000), always written by `write_copy`. Read per field (ADR 0039 §3): a missing or unreadable field reads the built-in value, then the speed is limited to the copy's max feed; no damage flag (as max feed and max power); nothing is rewritten on read.
   **Damaged copy (UX point, changed) (Proposal):** there is no whole-copy fallback. A new operation takes the copy's pair as read; only a damaged or missing field of the pair reads the built-in value. A damaged name, Air assist or output format leaves a readable pair in use; the page's block "The machine profile of this page is damaged." stands as built.
2. **Profile record format 7** (`machine_profile_file.rs`): `default_power_pct` and `default_speed_mm_min` after `output_format`, as `#[serde(default, skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none")] Option<_>` fields checked per version in one function beside `output_values` (the ADR 0065 §3 pattern, not plain serde defaults, because the record is strict). Format 5 and 6 read the built-in pair (speed limited to max feed) and are not rewritten on read; the next change of the record writes format 7. Damaged: the keys in a format 5 or 6 record, a format 7 record without either, power outside 1 to 100 or with two decimals, speed outside 1 to 100,000 or above the record's max feed. Newer than 7: skipped as today.
3. **Read rule for an operation without `op_settings`** (Frage 338 A). `read_settings(value, kind, defaults: DefaultNumbers) -> SettingsRead`; an absent register reads `Stored(StoredSettings::own(kind, defaults.settings(), None))`: own numbers, passes 1, Engrave + Outline with `outline = main`, no `interval_mm` (the kind's default interval, criterion 5), no link. `SettingsRead::None` is removed. `defaults` are those of the operation's own manufacturing copy. A damaged register stays Damaged (criteria 17, 19); an Unknown kind is never read (its block stays Damaged in `block_numbers`; `output/input.rs` gives its placeholder the derived read, as it gives `OperationOptions::default()`); a legacy `op_grid` row is never filled and runs its cells and labels as before. Nothing is committed on read, so `Document::version()` is unchanged and `is_dirty()` stays false: no step, no modified mark, no notice (criterion 15).
4. **Pinning the numbers shown at open (W12) is in the document, not the session.** The derived numbers are written exactly where they could otherwise change:
   - **(a) Creation:** `NewOperation` gains `settings: Option<StoredParams>` (plain values, document-core decides nothing), written in the `add_operation` commit. The kind press passes `StoredSettings::own(kind, copy.defaults.settings(), None).to_value()`. One step "Add operation" (criteria 4, 20); a redo replays it (W11).
   - **(b) A step that changes the copy's pair** (Default power, Default speed, Max feed lowering the speed, Pick, Reset from profile): before the new copy is written, the same commit writes the derived numbers of the copy **before** the change into every unfilled operation of that manufacturing (known kind, no register, not `op_grid`). `write_profile` takes these writes as plain values, computed by the session with laser-core. Undo returns copy and registers together; the visible numbers never change (criteria 8, 22, W12). No fill when the pair does not change.
   - **(c) A changing save:** `pack_for_save` checks `is_dirty()` first; when true, every unfilled operation of every laser manufacturing gets its derived numbers in the save's reserved commit `refresh_material_numbers` (ADR 0054 §3, no step, no History row), together with the material refresh (Proposal: one commit per save and no new reserved label; the label is a published file word, so a new one would be a format word for no reader). A save of an unchanged project fills nothing (criterion 16).
   - **Why not a session pin (the lead's suggestion):** a map of pinned numbers in the session is lost on reload, recovery copy and on another device of a shared workspace, and the row would show what the document does not hold. With (b), the derived value at any later moment equals the value at open on every device: every writer is a format-59 build (decision 7), so a peer's pair edit carries its own fill. History restore (criterion 21) restores the copy of that version too, so its derived numbers are those of that step.
5. **Undo and history free of the empty state.** With 4 (a) no step creates an operation without numbers. The No-settings draft goes from `curvyo-editor-wasm/src/session/operations/` (`settings_draft`, `type_into_draft`, `type_interval_into_draft`, `draft_interval`, the `SettingsRead::None` branches in `settings.rs`, `options.rs`, `refresh.rs`, `settings_view.rs`); a typed number, a material pick and "Own numbers" start from the derived read. New step labels `edit_default_power` ("Edit default power") and `edit_default_speed` ("Edit default speed") as `ProfileChange::EditDefaultPower`/`EditDefaultSpeed` in `manufacturing.rs`, registered in `history/rows.rs`, ui-core `history_names.rs` and `step_scope.rs` (manufacturing scope).
6. **Removed** (criterion 23): `SettingsState::NoSettings`, `NumbersRead::NoSettings`, `Cause::NoSettings` with its text " has no power and speed." and its rank (the other ranks keep their order), the arm in `frame_ignores` (`session/output/frame.rs`) and in `raster_view.rs`; frontend `"no_settings"` in `operationSettingsView.ts` (fields never `null`), the "No settings" lines in `OperationRow.tsx`, `OperationSettingsBlock.tsx`, `operationSettingsRows.ts` and the "–" time of `operationsView.ts`. **Kept:** `FixtureNoMaterial`, `FixtureNoSet`, `NumbersRead::blocks()` for them and the fixture "No settings" of `FixtureOutputPart.tsx`/`fixtureJobsView.ts` (Frage 339 A); `Cause::DamagedSettings` stays a warning, rank 25 (spec criterion 24 corrected to the build).
7. **`format_version` 59** (next free at merge; main is at 58), no migration. A build of 58 would drop the two copy keys on its next Machine block edit, silently changing the derived numbers, and would block old operations as "No settings"; ADR 0033 §7 refuses that by a bump. A file of 58 opens unchanged. Doc block in `document.rs` beside version 56.
8. **The frame (`0118`, PR #460).** `build_frame_job` stays: operations never read as blocking any more, so `build_frame_job` equals `build_job` for an operation-only job and its outline is unchanged (criterion 25). `frame_stand_in` keeps only the fixture arm (a fixture block without pick or set, Frage 339 A); its `BlockSource::Operation` arm is dead and goes, and the doc comments of `frame_stand_in`, `build_frame_job` and `Built::unpriced` say "fixture block". `frame_goldens_0118.rs` builds its numberless operation from an absent register with defaults; its outline goldens stay byte-equal.
