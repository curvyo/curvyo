# Path box: turn any closed path (a heart, a shield, a coffin) into a laser-cut box, as a generator

Status: Done (slice 1 PR #476, slice 2 PR #481) (revision 7, 2026-11-25, aligned to ADR 0083 §13; the architect and the UX engineer revise `adrs.md`, ADR 0083 and `ux-notes.md` for it in parallel; before it: `adrs.md` (ADR 0083, Proposed, the architect may still revise it) and `ux-notes.md` revision 1 exist; Fragen 364 to 366 open, all with defaults A (Frage 367 fell away); the UX questions are decided as Proposals (kinks built in slice 1, layout beside the source); slice 2 (curves) is Ready on slice 1's code and does not need `0148`)
Priority: Could
Origin: Customer (2026-11-24, translated, an idea added to `0147`): "turn ANY path into a box": a heart, a shield, a coffin. The corners of the path become finger joints, the curves are always slit-bent; "if the radius is too small: own fault, we just do it". Everything marked **(Proposal)** is the product owner's and vetoable at the demo.

## User value

As a maker I want to draw a closed outline, press "Box from path" and get a cut-ready box with that outline (walls, base, lid), so that a heart-shaped gift box, a shield for a prop or a coffin for a tiny pet needs no hand-made finger joints and no hand-drawn slits.

**What other tools do.** Boxes.py and MakerCase build boxes from rectangles or a few fixed polygons; Inkscape's Tabbed Box Maker is rectangular only; Fusion 360 / "Slicer" style add-ins and some LightBurn community scripts make finger-jointed polygons but no bending walls; none turns a drawn outline with mixed corners and curves into one box. We do better: one outline in, a live object (`0141`) out; sharp corners become joints, curves become living hinges, from the same parameter block as the Rounded box.

## Relation to neighbouring specs

- **`0147` rounded-box:** the parameter block, the five parts, the slit rules (no uncut band, kerf plus 0.3 mm slots, Straight, Offset, Wave), per-part thickness fields and the group-like unit (one operation for all parts; Detach part when `0147` slice 3 ships it) are reused unchanged; the Rounded box is the special case "rectangle with four equal arcs". Slice 1 of `0147` (strip, tabs, plates) comes first.
- **`0148` slit-bend-pattern (idea, no folder):** **not a prerequisite of slice 2.** The wall piece is slit flat, with the column pattern of `0147`'s `slit_bend.rs` that slice 1 already uses for kinks (ADR 0083 §4); a curved span only gives it a longer strip and a local radius per column. `0148` may reuse `slit_bend.rs` later.
- **`0141` parametric-generators:** another kind through the same contract (ADR 0080); the finger-joint code is extended from 90 degree corners to any corner angle (mitred fingers).
- **`0101` laser operations:** parts share one operation by default, Detach part as in `0147` criterion 15 once its slice 3 exists (not in slice 1).

## Slices

- **Slice 1 (Ready): outlines of straight edges.** Criteria 1 to 5, 8, 10 to 12 (a coffin, a star, a hexagon): corners become finger joints at any angle, base and lid. **A join that turns by less than Corner angle (a kink: a 20-gon, a coffin at Corner angle 90) is built as a bent span with slits**, as the arcs of `0147` (ADR 0083 §3; a loop with no corner left gets the seam and tab windows, criterion 9); slice 1 refuses only an outline with a curved cubic (criterion 1). The customer can try it end to end. **(Proposal, UX question 1 A)**
- **Slice 2 (Ready): curves.** Criterion 1's refusal of curved outlines is lifted; criteria 6, 7, 9 and 11 hold for curved cubics (slice 1 already built them for kinks); new criteria 13 to 16. The customer can try it end to end with a **heart, a shield, an ellipse and a circle**: curved spans always slit-bent, tangent-continuous junctions stay one piece, a smooth outline is one strip, a too small radius is built with a warning, the finger hollow also sits on a bent span. Needs the arc fit (`arc_fit.rs`, ADR 0083 §3) and nothing else new: no slit pattern along a curve, no `0148`, no new crate or dependency, no `format_version`.
- **Later:** a linked (not copied) source path (Frage 364 B), outlines with holes or islands, a different wall height per edge, a sloping wall (cone or taper), curve-aware tab placement options.

## Acceptance criteria (as far as clear)

1. Given a selected single closed object (a path, or a shape such as a rectangle, ellipse or polygon; its outline in the document's units), when the customer presses **Box from path** in the Generators tool (a segment next to Box, Rounded box and Gear; offered as a command on a selected closed outline), then a generated object (kind `path-box`) is built with default parameters, selected, one undo step "Add path box". The button is a command in the Select bar, shown only for one closed outline; the Generators segment is a pick mode (click the outline, or Enter on the selection), UX notes section 1. The outline is the **inner face of the wall** (cover and lid are the outline offset outward by the wall thickness; **Proposal**, ADR 0083). **The flat layout lies to the right of the source's bounding box, Gap apart, top edges aligned; the source stays untouched and clickable** (**Proposal**, UX question 2 B; replaces "over the outline's position"). **Refusals** (a notice ending "Nothing was changed.", no error dialog, nothing built): nothing selected, an open path, a group, several objects or any other ineligible object: "Select one closed outline. Nothing was changed."; a self-intersecting outline: "The outline crosses itself. Nothing was changed."; an outline too small (area below (4 t)^2, t the default Wall thickness): "The outline is too small for a box with 3 mm walls. Nothing was changed."; **from slice 2 on there is no refusal for curves** (slice 1 refused a curved cubic with "This outline has curves. Box from path builds straight outlines only so far."; that text is removed with slice 2). Mixed outlines (a shield: straight top and sides, curved bottom) and smooth ones (circle, ellipse) are accepted
2. Given the new object, then its parameters are the block of `0147` criterion 2 (**Inner height** (the same key and label as the Rounded box), the five thickness fields (Wall thickness, Inner base, Cover, Ring and Lid thickness), Kerf compensation, Slit pattern, Wall slits is not offered: curves are always slit-bent, Slit width, Slit spacing and Slit rows automatic, Base inset, Base cover, Lid, Lid seat, Finger hollow, Layout and Gap) **plus** **Corner angle (degrees, a slider, default 25 (Frage 366 A), range 5 to 90)**; no Corner radius (the radii are the path's). The outline itself is stored as a parameter (criterion 8) and has no row; there are no size knobs on the canvas. The Slit rows field stays shown in slice 1 too (a kink has slits). The automatic Slit spacing shows the smallest spacing used.
3. Given the outline, then it is cut into **spans** at its **corners**: a node, or a **cusp** of a curve (a point where the curve's derivative vanishes, ADR 0083 §13.2), is a corner when the direction of travel changes there by more than Corner angle (a smooth node, a line meeting a curve tangent-continuously, or a curve's gentle bend inside a cubic is not: the bend of a smooth lobe is never a corner at any Corner angle). A span is **straight** when it stays within 0.1 mm of its chord and **curved** otherwise. Tests (fixtures in `tests/fixtures/`): a coffin (six straight edges) gives six corners and no curved span; a shield (flat top, two straight sides, curved bottom) gives its two top corners and the junctions to the curve as no corner; a heart gives two corners (the top notch and the bottom tip) and two bent spans (its four curved cubics merge tangent-continuously into one span per side, ADR 0083 §10); a circle gives no corner and one curved span.
4. Given a straight span, then its wall piece is a **plain plate** (text or a hole may be placed on it, as the straight wall of `0147`), of the Wall height, joined to the next piece at each corner by **finger joints** of the box of `0141` (finger width 2 times the wall thickness, odd count, one owner per corner, ADR 0082 §1), **cut square, at the corner's angle** (a corner of 60 degrees or 120 degrees as well as 90): off a right angle a finger tip stands proud of or short of the outer face by up to t |cot δ| / 2 (t the wall thickness, δ the turn angle at the corner, 90 degrees for a right angle, as in ADR 0083 §5, so the overshoot is t |cot δ| / 2 and zero at 90 degrees). A test mates every pair of neighbouring pieces at a 60, 90 and 135 degree corner at the given thickness and asserts finger and gap centres agree and every tip lies within t |cot δ| / 2 of the outer face (within the kerf value at 90 degrees, as `0141` criterion 3).
5. Given a corner whose interior angle is below 30 degrees (a spike), then the box is **built anyway** with a status notice naming the wall pieces by their numbers (criterion 12): "The corner between pieces 3 and 4 is very sharp: 22 degrees.", several: "2 corners are very sharp: between pieces 3 and 4, and 6 and 1."; the corner also carries a red cross on the canvas; nothing is refused (customer: "own fault, we just do it"; **Proposal** for the notice).
6. Given a curved span, then its wall piece is **always slit-bent** with the slit pattern of `0147` (criterion 4: parallel to the bend axis, running out through the top or bottom edge alternately, no uncut band, closed slots of kerf plus 0.3 mm), the spacing automatic **from the span's local radius** (0.22 times the radius, so a tight curve gets denser slits than a wide one; a stored 0 means automatic as in `0147`). A **curved span that runs tangent-continuously into a straight span stays one piece** (the strip is not cut at a smooth junction); a solid margin of z + t between the slits and a finger edge (z the finger's depth into the overlap zone; ADR 0083 §5), which is 2 times the wall thickness at 90 degrees and longer at sharper corners. Tests as in `0147` criterion 4 per curved span, and the **neutral-line length** of a piece is its inner-face length plus half the wall thickness times its signed total turn (convex adds, concave subtracts; ADR 0083 §13.5). The test compares it with the geometry-core length of the same stretch of the stored cubics plus t/2 times its turn, less each flat's and window's chord-for-arc difference, within 0.1 mm.
   **Beside a corner on a curve the wall runs flat for f = z + t (the solid margin, ADR 0083 §13.7):** that first stretch of the curve is replaced by its chord, so the wood that does not bend is straight and the finger joint is cut as the plates meet it. **(Proposal, the deviation from the drawn outline):** the wall face there lies inside the drawn curve by the flat's sagitta f squared over 8 R (0.5 mm for f = 9 mm, R = 20 mm), and the joint may be cut up to 10 degrees off the corner's tangent turn (a heart tip of 145 degrees may be cut at up to 155 degrees and then carries the sharp-corner notice of criterion 5, which is true of the joint as cut); f is shortened until the turn keeps its sign, stays at least Corner angle and within 10 degrees, and is at most a third of the run. Vetoable at the demo.
7. Given a radius too small to bend (below 2 times the wall thickness, as in a sharp curve of a heart), then the box is **built anyway** with a status notice naming the wall piece by its number: "Piece 2 bends to 2.5 mm; below 6 mm the wall may crack.", several: "Pieces 2 and 5 bend below 6 mm; the wall may crack there." (6 mm is 2 times the Wall thickness); the slits are laid by the same rules and no value is clamped (customer: "if the radius is too small: own fault, we just do it"). No error dialog, no refusal, no change of the path.
8. Given the outline as a parameter, then **the object keeps a copy of the path** (default, Frage 364 A): a list of segments in mm in the object's own frame, stored in the file with the other parameters (`0141` criterion 7: the file opens exactly as stored, the outline survives). The source object stays untouched in the document (Frage 365 A); editing it later does not change the box. Resizing the box is proportional (as `0147` criterion 7) and scales the stored path (the thicknesses, Kerf and Gap stay, so the slit count follows the new length). `format_version`: none of its own (ADR 0083): the outline is a new `Path` parameter, additive; the box takes `format_version` only through Detach part (`0147` slice 3).
9. Given an outline with no corner (a circle, an ellipse, a 20-gon, a rounded square), then the wall is **one strip** that joins the plates by tabs on flat windows of the arc, the seam between two tabs (`0147` criterion 5; the mating test on a curve allows the window sag plus the kerf). The seam sits at the middle of the **longest straight span** if there is one (never in a bent span, slice 1), else (an ellipse, a circle) at the middle of the back side, the point of the outline with the least y (as the exact circle of `0147`), where the strip is slit-bent: **no slit within 2 times the wall thickness of the seam cut** (the same margin as at a finger edge, **Proposal**, revision 5; the ends meet closed, the seam is a butt as in `0147`). Test: circle, ellipse (wider than high and higher than wide) and the 20-gon: one strip, seam where named, no slit inside the margin, strip length equals the outline's perimeter on the neutral line within 0.1 mm.
10. Given the plates (inner base, base cover, seat ring, lid), then they are generated to the **outline** (cover and lid to the outline offset by the wall thickness) and join the wall by tab and slot as in `0147` criteria 5, 10, 12 and 13 (with Base inset, Lid seat, Finger hollow; the hollow sits at the middle of the longest straight span that holds it, else **at the middle of the lid's longest convex curved stretch that holds the hollow width plus 2 times the wall thickness and is convex across the hollow's width, built from slice 2 on** (ADR 0083 §13.10; slice 1 left the lid without it and said "No place for the finger hollow on this lid."; the heart has no straight and now gets it; only a lid with no such stretch still gets that notice), **Proposal**); **where the exact cover cannot be built** (it crosses itself, or the outline has a concave arc of radius below the wall thickness) **the cover and the lid are the geometry-core round offset of the inner face by the wall thickness, without the hollow** (criterion 16); on a curved span on flat windows (ADR 0082 §5), on a straight span never in the first or last piece. The mating tests of `0147` run on the coffin (slice 1) and the heart (slice 2).
11. Given any parameter change, then the paths regenerate within 100 ms of thread CPU time for the coffin and **300 ms for the heart with at least 400 slots (the test asserts the count)**, edit and commit included (Proposal; fixtures; measured as `0141` criterion 11, budget factor as in Frage 368); **the pick-mode hover and the Select bar check, which build a draft (marks and refusals, no slits), take at most 100 ms for the heart, and the ghost of a drag then shows the ring full and the exact cover** (ADR 0083 §13.13); invalid and dependent values are clamped with a status notice, never an error dialog; move, rotate, align, Object to path and operations of `0101` work as for every generated object, and **the box is a group-like unit with Detach part exactly as `0147` criterion 15**.
12. Given the flat layout, then the wall pieces lie in a row (in outline order) with Gap, the plates below in the order of `0147`; nothing overlaps; the wall pieces are numbered in walk order from the outline's start, each number a **badge** on the piece in the layout and one on the inner base at the stretch that piece stands on (a piece shorter than 24 px on screen leaves its badges out; the badges are never cut or exported; **Proposal**, UX notes section 3; replaces "labelled in the Generator block"), so the maker matches pieces to places and the notices can name them.
13. (Slice 2) Given a **heart, a shield, an ellipse and a circle** as the selected outline (fixtures in `tests/fixtures/`, drawn with curved cubics), when the customer presses Box from path, then a box is built, with no refusal: the heart as two bent spans meeting at a notch and a tip (both finger-jointed corners, criterion 3), the shield as two pieces and two corners: the flat top, and one piece that runs from corner to corner through the two straight sides and the bent bottom (the sides meet the curve tangent-continuously, criteria 6 and 14, so they add no cut; three spans, two pieces), the ellipse and the circle as one strip. Test: each fixture builds; pieces, corners and bent spans are counted as named; every wall piece is within 0.5 mm in height-line of the Wall height; nothing overlaps in the layout (criterion 12).
14. (Slice 2) Given a curved span, then its **neutral-line length** and its **local radius per slit column** come from the arc fit of ADR 0083 §3 (the curve replaced by arcs within **0.01 mm**; a span may have several radii, as a heart lobe does), the piece length equals the span's length within 0.1 mm (criterion 6), and **a junction whose direction changes by 1 degree or less** (a line into a curve, a curve into a curve, a smooth node) **adds no cut and no corner** (the piece runs through it; slice 1 already treats a join of at most 1 degree as tangent). Test: the shield's two junctions and the heart's two lobes each give one piece; a circle drawn as four cubics and as eight cubics gives the same strip length within 0.1 mm and the same slit count (the node count of the drawing does not matter).
15. (Slice 2) Given a curved outline with **Corner angle** changed, then the spans follow at the **nodes and cusps** (a smooth bend inside a cubic never becomes a corner): a join turning by more than Corner angle is a finger joint, one turning by at most that (and more than 1 degree) is a kink built as a bent span with a fillet (ADR 0083 §13.3), so the heart's counts change only where a node of the drawing turns by an angle between 5 and 90 degrees; the notch and the tip stay joints; an outline of tangent junctions is unaffected. A slider change regenerates within the budget of criterion 11. Test: the heart at 5, 25 and 90 degrees gives the named corner counts (fixture; the counts are recorded in the golden, not guessed).
16. (Slice 2) Given a curved outline with the plates (base, cover, ring, lid), then every plate follows the outline (cover and lid offset outward by the wall thickness, criterion 10), the tabs sit on flat windows of the arc, each tab fits its slot of the wall within the window sag plus the kerf, the lid's finger hollow sits on the longest bent span when no straight holds it (criterion 10), and **a cover whose exact offset crosses itself, or an outline with a concave arc below the wall thickness in radius (a peanut's waist, a heart drawn with a narrow V notch such as 53 degrees; the default heart with a notch of about 100 degrees keeps its exact cover and its hollow, criterion 10), gives the notice "The notch is too narrow for the cover at 3 mm walls: the cover and lid are rounded there, without a finger hollow." (the current Wall thickness; with Lid or Lid seat off "the cover is rounded there", with Finger hollow off it ends at "rounded there."; UX notes section 6) and a cover and lid cut by the geometry-core round offset of the inner face by the wall thickness, without the finger hollow** (ADR 0083 §13.9, built anyway as criterion 7; **Proposal** for the notice, which then replaces the hollow notice of criterion 10; fixture: a peanut of two convex lobes and a concave waist). Test: the mating tests of `0147` on the heart and the shield; the heart lid carries the hollow.

## Out of scope

- Outlines with holes, islands or several paths; open paths; self-intersecting paths (a notice, criterion 1).
- A linked source path that updates the box when the original is edited (Frage 364 B, later), and re-picking another outline for an existing box (later).
- Different wall heights per edge, tapered or sloping walls, a curved top, a rolling lid, compartments, text on the walls, 3D preview, assembly drawing.
- Choosing per span whether it is bent or jointed (the corner angle slider is the only control) and freehand editing of single spans.
- Material databases, automatic kerf, bend simulation.

## Open questions for the customer (all with defaults)

- **Frage 364 (PATH-SOURCE):** how the box knows its outline. A: it keeps a **copy** of the path (a parameter; later edits of the source do not change the box). B: it **links** the source path and follows its edits (a change of the generator contract, later). **Recommendation and default: A now, B as a follow-up.**
- **Frage 365 (PATH-ORIGINAL):** what happens to the source path when the box is made. A: it stays where it is, untouched. B: it is hidden. C: it is consumed (replaced by the box). **Recommendation and default: A (it is the only copy of the drawing; delete it by hand).**
- **Frage 366 (PATH-ANGLE):** the default Corner angle above which a bend is a joint. A: 25 degrees. B: 45 degrees. C: 15 degrees. **Recommendation and default: A** (a heart's smooth lobes stay curves, a hexagon's 60 degree turns are joints; the slider changes it per box).

## Decisions

Customer 2026-11-24 (translated): corners of the path become finger joints; curves are always slit-bent; a radius that is too small is not refused. Product owner (all **(Proposal)**): Corner angle slider, default 25 degrees; the sharp-corner and too-small-radius notices; the spacing from the local radius; the 2 times thickness margin between slits and finger edges; the hollow's place; the CPU-time budgets; piece numbers. Revision 4 (2026-11-24, slice-1 build, all **Proposal**): criterion 4 uses δ as the turn angle at the corner; the heart has two bent spans (four cubics merge); on an outline with no corner the seam sits at the middle of the longest straight, never in a bent span; a kink's fillet radius is raised so its arc holds 2 slit columns at the minimum gap. Revision 5 (2026-11-25, slice 2 Ready, all **Proposal**, vetoable at the demo): curved outlines accepted, the curves refusal removed; `0148` is no prerequisite; criterion 9 now covers smooth outlines without a straight (seam at the middle of the back side, no slit within 2 t of it); the finger hollow is built on the longest bent span when no straight holds it; the heart's narrow notch is built with a notice; new criteria 13 to 16; heart, shield and ellipse added to the CPU budget. Kept from slice 1: the seam in the longest straight, the kink fillet radius max(2 t, 2 g / |δ|), the hollow fallback is now built. No new question: Fragen 364 to 366 stay answered by their defaults A (built so in slice 1), the next free number is 369 (368 is on `main`). Revision 6 (2026-11-25, aligned to ADR 0083 §13, all **Proposal**, vetoable at the demo): criterion 1 has no curved refusal; a corner of a curve is a node or a cusp only (criteria 3, 15; a smooth lobe never becomes a corner, so criterion 15 no longer promises corners at Corner angle 5); the neutral length counts the signed turn and the flats beside corners (criterion 6); the flats deviate from the drawn outline by f squared over 8 R and may cut a joint up to 10 degrees off the tangent turn (criterion 6, the one real change a maker can see); the hollow sits on the lid's longest convex curved stretch, and the exact cover falls back to a round offset without hollow (criteria 10, 16); the heart's edit budget is 300 ms with at least 400 slots, the pick hover 100 ms in a draft (criterion 11; the shield and ellipse are no longer budgeted). Issue 7 of `adrs.md` is resolved by this revision. Revision 7 (2026-11-25, UX notes revision 4 section 10, all **Proposal**, vetoable at the demo): criterion 13 counts the shield as 2 pieces and 2 corners (the golden records 2 pieces; the criterion speaks of spans), criterion 16's notice says what was built and the wall thickness, during a value drag the ring shows full and gets its opening on release (the one difference from `0147` section 2), and the pick hover's band gaps are approximate while crosses, red and seam match. Revision 3 (2026-11-24, lead decisions, all **Proposal**, vetoable at the demo): slice 1 builds kinks as bent spans and refuses only curved cubics (UX question 1 A); the layout lies right of the source's bounding box, Gap apart, top edges aligned (question 2 B); criteria 1, 2, 5, 7 and 12 aligned with the UX notes (question 3: the refusal "Select one closed outline" with "Nothing was changed.", piece numbers, "Inner height", reworded notices).
