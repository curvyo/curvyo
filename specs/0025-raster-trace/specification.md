Status: Ready for slices 2 and 3 (slice 1 Done (#213, merged by the customer 2026-10-28); slices 2 and 3 specified 2026-10-28; the four presets of criteria 59 to 69 are decided by the customer, Frage 64 A, 2026-10-28; their numbers stay Proposal until the harness fixes them)
Priority: Must (MVP slice 12, R-VEC-001)
Origin: Customer. Request of 2026-10-14 ("a new tool, settings in the top-middle area, not a property, not the right side; start with Inkscape's functions, check Illustrator and VectorCraft; beat Inkscape: smoother, fewer nodes, fewer wobbly lines, fewer micro paths; bitmap import first"). Decided by the customer on 2026-10-14: the UX mockup of the Trace tool (`ux-mockup.html`) is accepted; 2 to 4 s of compute for a 2000 x 2000 px image is acceptable; the licence is AGPL-3.0-or-later; "VectorCraft" is `storytold/vectorcraft`; tracing should be extensible later (not in the first slice). Everything marked **Proposal** is the product owner's (or the ux-engineer's, from `ux-proposal.md`) and vetoable at the demo.
Depends on: `0058-bitmap-import` (done: image objects, the working image and the pixel-to-millimetre map, criteria 42 and 43), `0050-layers-and-objects-panel`, `0023-groups`, `0054-combine-open-paths` and `0016-boolean-operations` (compound paths with holes), `0020-undo-redo`, `0049`/`0057` not needed.
Inputs: `research.md` (PO, field research and the 8 + 6 questions), `ux-proposal.md` and `ux-mockup.html` (ux-engineer, accepted mockup).

## User value

As a maker with a logo, a scan, a drawing or a photo as a bitmap, I want to select the image, start the Trace tool, see the outline it would make over my picture while I adjust a few settings in millimetres, and press one button to get clean, smooth, light paths, so that I can cut, engrave, plot or edit the shape without redrawing it by hand and without cleaning up a wobbly, node-heavy Inkscape result.

**Field reference (full text in `research.md`).**

- *Inkscape Trace Bitmap.* A docked dialog with Single scan and Multiple scans; potrace underneath (binary bitmap; colour is many threshold passes). Settings in pixels. Known complaints: wobbly curves from staircase input, many micro paths, too many nodes, hairline gaps between colour layers or duplicate edges when stacked.
- *Illustrator Image Trace.* A panel with presets, a live "tracing object" and an Expand step; Noise and Paths in pixels; Abutting and Overlapping layering.
- *CorelDRAW PowerTRACE, Vectorizer.ai, Vector Magic.* Closed benchmarks only; Vectorizer.ai is cloud, so it is never used with customer images (R-SYS-007). Affinity has no automatic trace.
- *VectorCraft (`storytold/vectorcraft`, crate `trace`) and VTracer (visioncortex).* Open source (MIT OR Apache-2.0) tracers in Rust. Customer decision of 2026-10-27 (Frage 49 B): VTracer/visioncortex is the tracer **for now, if it works**, as a dependency; we add our own pre- and post-processing around it; an own pipeline is the fallback only if VTracer misses the quality criteria. VectorCraft stays a benchmark and idea source.
- *What is better here.* (1) Settings in millimetres of the placed image, with a pixel hint, not in pixels. (2) Pre-processing (threshold on luma, despeckle, optional upscale) and post-processing (speck removal in mm, node reduction at a stated deviation, closing, hole structure) around the tracer give fewer nodes at the same deviation than Inkscape. (3) The preview is an outline over the dimmed picture at real size, in the bar above the canvas, not a thumbnail in a window. (4) Path and node counts are shown, because they decide how long a job runs on the machine. (5) Presets are named after the job (Logo, Photo, Silhouette for cutting, Engraving). (6) No live object to expand: the result is plain paths and one undo step.

## Terms

- **Image object, working image, pixel map:** as `0058` (Terms and criterion 42): the working image is RGBA8, row-major, at most 16 Mpx and 8192 px on the long side; the pixel map maps pixel coordinates to document millimetres including rotation, skew and flip. The trace reads the working image only, never the file.
- **Foreground:** the pixels the trace turns into shapes. In black and white mode: pixels whose luma is below the **Threshold**. Luma is `0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B` of the stored sRGB values (0 to 255); transparent pixels are composited over white first (`0058` criterion 7 gives straight alpha).
- **Outline:** one closed boundary of a foreground region (outer) or of a hole in it. **Paths** in the status line means the number of outlines. **Nodes** means anchor points of all outlines.
- **Specks:** foreground regions and holes smaller than the Specks size. The size is the side, in millimetres, of a square of the same area (Frage 51 and `ux-proposal.md` TRACE-UX-UNIT), computed through the pixel map (a rotated or stretched image uses the true area in mm²).
- **Smooth:** 0 to 100; higher means fewer nodes and a larger deviation. It maps monotonically to the tracer's curve settings and our node reduction (the mapping is the architect's and the implementer's and is documented in the crate).
- **Trace result:** a plain data value: closed outlines in document millimetres with hole structure and the counts. It is what the preview draws and what Create paths inserts.
- Lengths in millimetres; computed geometry is compared with the tolerances named in each criterion (pixel distances are measured in pixels of the working image).

The button's tooltip is its name and key only: "Trace tool (I)".
2. Given the Trace tool is chosen with **one image selected** (several: criterion 46), then at once (a) the Select bar is replaced in place by the Trace bar in the overlay row, (b) the picture is dimmed to 35 % (a white wash on the light theme, the canvas colour at 65 % on the dark theme), and (c) the preview of the remembered settings is computed and drawn as a hollow outline (criterion 8). 3. Given the Trace tool is chosen with **nothing selected**, then the picture and canvas are untouched and the bar is one row: "Click an image to trace it." and a text button "Import image..." that does what the rail Import button does (`0058` criterion 1). Click an image to trace it." and nothing is refused. A locked or hidden image cannot be selected (`0050`) and so is not traced.
4. Given the bar with an image, then it has these groups in this order and wraps by whole groups, the first row never moving (**Proposal**, `ux-proposal.md` section 3; the ux-engineer measures at 800 x 600): **Threshold** (value field 0 to 255, label inside, starts at the image's automatic value and shows it, see criterion 9), **Smooth** (0 to 100, default 80 (**Proposal**: the final default is set in step 0 as the lowest value at which criterion 21 holds), see `adrs.md`), **Specks** ("Ignore below", mm, 0 to 10, default 0.5, with a muted pixel readout such as "0.50 mm  ~ 2 px" that follows the image's size on the canvas), and the **result row** (status text and the **Create paths** button, 112 px, accent fill); the state label "Trace" (criterion 1) opens row 1 before Threshold. At 800 x 600 with the panel open this is 4 rows, with the panel collapsed 2 rows (ux-engineer measures at build). In slice 1 the Mode is black and white only and no Mode control is drawn (a control with one choice is not drawn, `ux-proposal.md` 3.3). The right-hand panel keeps its state.
5. Given the bar's fields, then Up/Down change by 1, Shift+Up/Down by 10 (Specks: 0.1 and 1 mm; Ctrl 0.01 mm), Home/End go to the limits, a typed value commits on Enter, an out-of-range typed value is clamped silently to the range, and Enter in a field commits the value and returns focus to the canvas (the next Enter creates the paths, criterion 12). Changing a value is not a history step. The three values are remembered between uses as an app setting, not in the project file.
6. Given the settings of criteria 4 and 5, then they are described by **one plain data schema** (name, type, unit, range, default, step, group, advanced flag) from which the bar's controls are generated; there is no trait, registry or plugin type for tracers (Frage 55, default A; `CLAUDE.md` §5). Test: a unit test reads the schema and compares it with the defaults and ranges of criteria 4 and 5.

### Slice 1, Part B: the preview

7. Given the preview, then it is an overlay layer, never a document node; it is dropped when the maker leaves the tool (another tool, Escape, selecting a non-image) and it does not appear in the History, in Layers, in a saved file or in any output.
8. Given the preview outline, then it is drawn 1.5 px screen-space wide in the "new" blue of the design system (`--preview-new`) over a 1 px white casing, holes outlined too, no fills, at the real position over the picture; pan and zoom redraw a cached copy culled to the viewport and never trace again.
9. Given a first use on an image, then Threshold starts at the **automatic value** (Otsu's method on the luma histogram of the working image, rounded to a whole number) and the field shows that number; for a picture of one grey level the value is 128. Test: a 100 x 100 px picture with the left half luma 40 and the right half luma 200 gives a value between 41 and 199.
10. Given a change of any value, then the preview is recomputed after **150 ms** without a further change (debounce); while a value is being dragged it is computed on a reduced copy of the working image (longest side 1000 px, area-averaged) and on release at full size; a newer value cancels a stale job. While a job runs the previous outline stays; after 400 ms the status shows "Tracing..." with a 2 px progress line; after 1.5 s a text button **Stop** appears in the result row (a stopped job draws the old outline at 50 % with the state text "Preview is out of date", criterion 53); the status text is announced politely once per settled result, not per tick. The first preview after choosing the tool uses the reduced copy, the full-size one follows.
11. Given a settled result, then the status reads "7 paths, 412 nodes, 0.4 s" (tabular figures; the time is the compute time of the last full trace), plus a muted line "38 specks ignored" when specks were dropped. Test: a fixture with 3 foreground regions, one hole and 5 one-pixel specks at Specks 0.5 mm shows the exact counts.

### Slice 1, Part C: creating the paths

12. Given an image selected in the Trace tool, then the paths are created by (a) the **Create paths** button (tooltip "Create paths (Enter)"), (b) Enter with focus on the canvas or on a bar button (Ctrl+Enter from inside a field), or (c) a double-click on the image. When only the reduced-copy outline has settled, Create paths **waits for the full-size trace** (status "Tracing...", the button `aria-busy`, a further press ignored) and inserts the group when it finishes. **Escape** stops a running job (first press) or leaves the tool and drops the preview (second press); there is no Cancel button and no dialog, because nothing was changed.
13. Given Create paths, then **one history step "Trace image"** (`0020`) does all of this: it inserts **one group** named "Trace of <image name>" (cleaned name, 128 characters) **directly above the image** in the same layer or group; inside it, in black and white mode, **one compound path** with the holes as outlines of opposite direction (`0016`, `0054`), filled black (#000000, opacity 1), stroke off (**Proposal**); **the source image stays visible and unchanged** under the paths (customer, Frage 54 A); it selects the new group and switches to the **Select tool**. A success notice (Action notice next to the rail, `role="status"`, 3 s) reads "Traced: 7 paths, 412 nodes." Ctrl+Z removes the group; Ctrl+Y brings it back. Test: one history step, the group's position directly above the image, the image's visible flag still true, the tool afterwards.
14. Given an image that is inside a group or has an opacity, a rotation, a skew or a flip, then the group is inserted in the same parent and the paths sit on the picture pixel for pixel through the pixel map of `0058` criterion 42: for a 4 x 2 px fixture imported into a 40 x 20 mm frame, rotated 90 degrees and flipped horizontally, every outline point equals the point obtained by mapping its pixel position through the hand-computed affine within 1e-6 mm. The image's own opacity is not copied to the paths.
15. Given a result with **0 paths** at the current settings, then the status reads "0 paths at this Threshold: too low." (or "too high."; the direction is computed from the histogram), Create paths is `aria-disabled` and the status says why. Given a result that is **one path covering the whole image** (a full black page), then the status reads "1 path covering the whole image." and Create paths works (permit over refuse).
16. Given the tool, then nothing in it is refused on size, format or unsuitable content: an image of any size within the working limits of `0058` traces; the status says what will happen. One exception: a single foreground region with 65,000 or more holes after speck removal is not traced; the status reads "Too much detail: raise Specks." and Create paths is `aria-disabled`; nothing crashes (`adrs.md` decision 3). Test: a fixture region with 65,000 hole pixels returns this status.

### Slice 1, Part D: the tracer

17. Given the tracer, then it lives in the crate `curvyo-vectorize-core` (the slot reserved by ADR 0011; ADR 0023) as **one pure function** `trace(image, pixel_map, params, poll) -> result` that wraps VTracer's library `visioncortex` (Frage 49 B) with our pre- and post-processing: no file system, network, clock or threads of our own; builds for `wasm32-unknown-unknown`; our code has no `unsafe` (a dependency must pass `cargo deny`; the architect lists what it needs). Cancelling and progress are what the tracer allows from outside: `poll` is called between our own passes (every 64 rows) and before each region; one `visioncortex` call (about 0.1 s native at 2000 x 2000 px at most) cannot be interrupted, and a stale job is stopped by terminating the Web Worker (criterion 58). Test: wasm build; a native test shows that a stop request between stages returns `Cancelled` within 250 ms of thread CPU time on the 2000 x 2000 px fixture, or the ADR records why only the worker kill applies.
18. Given the same image and settings, then the result is identical on every run of the same build (native goldens are exact, golden files in `tests/fixtures/trace/` hold the counts, bounding boxes and a handful of points). Native and wasm builds agree in **equal path count, node count within 1 %, and bounding boxes within 0.01 mm**, until the upstream `acos` fix in `visioncortex` is released; then the tolerance returns to 1e-6 mm (`adrs.md` decision 6). No result depends on hash-map order, the clock, the locale or thread scheduling.
19. Given the pipeline, then (the architect fixes the stages and what the tracer is given) the foreground is the pixels below the Threshold (criterion in Terms); what the tracer returns is post-processed to these guarantees from the outside: all outlines are closed; outlines are cubic Beziers and lines in document millimetres; sharp corners of the source stay sharp within the deviations of criteria 20 and 21; foreground specks and hole specks below the Specks size are removed (a foreground speck becomes background, a hole speck is filled); the result has no outline with an area below the Specks size when Specks is above 0; holes have the opposite direction of their outer outline. Outlines follow the thresholded pixel border, smoothed; anti-aliasing gives no sub-pixel position. Otherwise only the measurable result counts.
20. Given the black-on-white rectangle fixture (200 x 100 px, a black rectangle at x 50 to 150, y 25 to 75, placed at 96 dpi, so 0.264583 mm per pixel), then at the defaults the result is **one closed outline of at most 8 nodes** whose corners are within 0.25 px of (50, 25), (150, 25), (150, 75), (50, 75) in pixel space (13.229, 6.615), (39.688, 6.615), (39.688, 19.844), (13.229, 19.844) mm within 0.07 mm.
21. Given the anti-aliased disc fixture (a black disc of radius 100 px on a 500 x 500 px white picture, edges anti-aliased), then at the defaults the result is one closed outline of **at most 16 nodes** and every point of the outline is within 0.75 px of the true circle, the mean distance at most 0.3 px (the outline follows the thresholded pixel border, smoothed; the mean holds at the default Smooth, which step 0 sets accordingly, **Proposal**).
22. Given the "ring with a dot" fixture (an outer ring, a hole, a dot inside the hole), then the result has three outlines, the hole in the opposite direction of the ring, the dot in the direction of the ring, and the filled area of the compound path (nonzero) equals the foreground area of the source within 1 %.
23. Given Smooth, then a higher value never produces more nodes: on each fixture of the quality set, nodes at Smooth 80 are no more than at Smooth 60, and those at 60 no more than at 20 (monotone). Given Threshold, then raising it never removes foreground pixels from the result: the filled area at Threshold t+10 is at least that at t within 0.5 %.
24. Given a transparent PNG (alpha 0 around a black logo), then transparent pixels count as white (background) and the result is the logo outline; given a grey scan with uneven lighting the result is whatever the Threshold gives (no hidden adaptive step in slice 1).
25. Given the budgets (customer decision of 2026-10-14: 2 to 4 s is acceptable for a 2000 x 2000 px image), then a full-size black and white trace of a 2000 x 2000 px working image of the line-art fixture takes **under 4.0 s of thread CPU time** (native release, `#[ignore]` benchmark on the build host, as `0056` criterion 27); the gate is native because it is measurable in CI, but the desktop app runs the same wasm in the webview (ADR 0001 §3), so the PR also reports the wasm time in a Web Worker on the same fixture; if it is above 4 s it goes to `docs/technical-debt.md` and a native-thread follow-up ADR is proposed (not a gate). The target (not gated) for black and white is 0.5 s. Preview timings (reduced copy) are reported.
26. Given an image of 16 Mpx (the working limit), then the trace completes with a memory use of at most the working image plus 16 bytes per pixel of intermediate data, measured by a counting global allocator in a native `#[ignore]` test and reported in the PR (if VTracer needs more, the number goes to `docs/technical-debt.md` and the architect decides, no self-made limit); it never aborts the tab (the one exception is the "Too much detail: raise Specks." status of criterion 16, a region with 65,000 or more holes, which returns instead of crashing); the long run is stoppable as criterion 17 states.

### Slice 1, Part E: quality against Inkscape

27. Given the **quality harness** (a test binary in the tracer crate, no UI; the fixtures in `tests/fixtures/trace/`), then it computes for each image of the black and white test set the metrics of `research.md` section 5 and compares them with a stored baseline of Inkscape's Trace Bitmap (potrace, default settings and best manual settings), kept as a JSON file of numbers only (no Inkscape output is committed, `research.md` section 7 item 5). Metrics: node density (nodes per 100 mm of path), micro paths (outlines below 0.25 mm²), max and mean deviation from the thresholded reference rendered at 4x, area error (XOR as % of foreground), wobble (curvature sign flips per 100 mm), closedness.
28. Given the black and white test set (images 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 11, 12 of `research.md` section 5: flat logo, black text at 300 dpi, small low-resolution text, line art scan, silhouette with fine holes, pixel art, thin-line stencil, technical drawing; plus the 3 to 5 images the customer sends, Frage 53), then at the **default settings** the quality is a **regression guard on the measured state** (customer, Frage 62 A, 2026-10-28; the earlier absolute and Inkscape-relative targets of Frage 53 are loosened): the numbers of step 0 stored as `candidate` in `curvyo-vectorize-core/tests/fixtures/trace/baseline.json` (`plan.md`, "Step 0") are the reference, and on each image and metric (node density, micro paths, maximum and mean deviation, area error, wobble, closedness) the result is **no worse than the stored number plus 5 % (plus 0.001 absolute)**; closedness stays 100 % and micro paths (outlines below 0.25 mm²) stay 0. The Inkscape columns stay in the file as information; the harness prints the gap to them per image and metric but a gap is not a miss. A change that improves a number updates the stored candidate in the same PR (the PR description lists the table before and after). Fixtures that are photographs or customer files carry a note on their origin; none is copyright-sensitive.
29. Given the first task of the build ("step 0", Frage 49 B: "for now, if it works"), then VTracer wrapped with our pre- and post-processing has run through the harness and its numbers are stored beside Inkscape's (done, `plan.md`; VectorCraft's `trace` may be run as a benchmark only). The measured gaps to Inkscape's default run are accepted by the customer for this story (Frage 62 A and 63, 2026-10-28: "try it first") and recorded under "Later": maximum deviation 2.54 px against Inkscape's 0.93 px (means over the set), the 300 dpi text image 9.46 against 1.19 px, line-art area error 31.9 against 11.3 %. The own-pipeline fallback is **not** built in this story. The shipped dependency is `visioncortex` (the library under VTracer; MIT OR Apache-2.0), not the `vtracer` crate, which fails `cargo deny` advisories; `cargo deny` clean, notices kept, transitive dependencies listed in `adrs.md`.

### Slice 2, Part G: presets and Advanced (black and white only)
