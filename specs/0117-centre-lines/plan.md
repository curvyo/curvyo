# Plan for centre lines

Implementer, 2026-11-15. Algorithm, constants and tests: [ADR 0063](../../docs/adr/0063-centre-lines.md).

## Affected crates/modules

- `curvyo-geometry-core`: new dependency `spade` 2.15.1 (brings `robust` 1.2); new modules
  `medial_axis/` (CDT, densify, boundary index, chord search, graph clean-up) and `centre_line/`
  (chains, fit, branch_trim, blob, inner); public entries `CentreSkeleton::build` and
  `CentreSkeleton::finish`.
- `curvyo-ui-core`: `centre_lines_plan` (subjects, refusals, node limit, request, document change).
- `curvyo-geometry-core`: `centre_line/stages.rs`, `CentreStages` (the staged memo per subject).
- `curvyo-render-core`: `build_centre_line_preview` (centre lines and node dots of the preview).
- `curvyo-editor-wasm`: `session/centre_lines.rs` (the open bar, the shown preview, Apply in
  slices and one `centre_lines` step), `session/centre_lines_draw.rs` (wash, kept parts, inner
  areas, outline, centre lines, no Node marks), `centre_lines_glue.rs` (the worker's state),
  `centre_lines_wire.rs` (the `f64` wire), `wasm_centre_lines.rs`, `tests/` budgets.
- `frontend`: `lib/centreLineJobs.ts` (the second worker: one job in flight, one pending slot,
  generations), `traceWorker.ts` messages `centre-subjects`, `centre-finish`, `centre-result`,
  the Node bar group and context-menu item, `CentreLinesBar` (sliders, switch, i, Cancel,
  Apply), `CentreInfoPage`, `useTraceInfo` with a tab name.
- `docs/design-system.md`: the rows of `ux-notes.md` §8.

## Validation

Golden text fixtures compared within 0.01 mm; strokes generated from known centre lines checked
against `T(w)`; property tests for rotation, scale, translation and determinism; the goldens also
in wasm (Node); thread CPU time tests at half the wasm budgets; a headless Chromium check at
800×600 and 1440×1000 on drawn line art and the customer's whale.

## Sizes (CLAUDE.md §5)

`centre_line/fit.rs` keeps the curve and profile fit; the density merge is `density.rs`, the
cut `thick.rs` and the kept outline `kept.rs` (each under 500 lines). `medial_axis/prune.rs` is split into `trim.rs`, `spurs.rs` and `extract.rs`, `centre_line/chains.rs`
into `junctions.rs` and `chains.rs`; `apply_centre_lines` hands the document write to
`write_centre_lines`. Kept over 60 lines, justified: `cut_branch` (one root search over one
parameterisation, its closures share the branch's samples), `Walker::walk` (one walk with its
exits), `chains` (the pipeline of one axis, step by step), `prune_spurs` (one loop whose passes
share the radii) and `centre_lines_drawing` (one
drawing, its children in stacking order). Each reads top to bottom with no branch that would
stand alone. Round D splits `cut_branch` into `Extension` (the extended branch) and `Butt` (its corners
against the parent); `fit.rs` (719 lines with its piece tests) gains the legs' cuts and moves the
new stray test to `safe_fit.rs`; `centre_line/quality_tests.rs` and `junction_tests.rs` are test
modules. Round A adds, justified the same way: `fit_curve` (100 lines: the corner cuts,
the tight and loose pieces and the stretch merge of one path), `merge_anchors` (110: one
closest-first loop with its projection and zone test), `merge_points` (66), `finish_into` (78:
the region's cases in order), `finish_chains` (67), `kept::parts` (67: one boolean and its
grouping), `kept::restored` (69: one walk round a ring) and `fit_profile` (61). `fit.rs` is 530
lines with its 75 lines of piece tests. Round B adds `CentreLinesWorker::finish` (62); since
round C `RegionInput::finish` (69 lines: the early outs and the stages in order) calls one
method per stage (`run_cut`, `run_through`, `run_branches`, `run_inner`). Also over 60, justified
the same way: `medial_axis/trim.rs` `settle_end` (87: one end's walk back along its edge with
its three exits, each walk bounded by the node count) and `centre_lines_wire.rs` `decode_results` (61: one bounds-checked read of
the wire, field by field in wire order). Test modules over 500 lines: `centre_line/tests.rs`
(about 950: the analytic fixtures of criteria 5 to 14 share their stroke builders and truth
functions) and `medial_axis/tests.rs` (561: the axis fixtures and their error measures); each
is one fixture family, so a split would only move the shared helpers into a third file; the session's bar and slices are `session/centre_lines_bar.rs`, apart from
`session/centre_lines.rs`.

Round E adds `centre_line/tips.rs` (376 lines) apart from `chains.rs` (464) and keeps over 60 lines,
justified as above: `outline_tips` (66: one scan of each ring, its turns and their maxima),
`legs_to_tip` (68: the two legs of one tip, built by one closure) and `corner_tip` (65: one
removal walk either way round the corner, then the spur); `chains` hands the junctions' tips to
`junction_tips` to stay under 100. `centre_line/tip_tests.rs` (about 580 lines) is one fixture
family: the outline's tip finder `tips` (99) and `tip_measures` (94: one tip's four measures over
every path) read top to bottom.
