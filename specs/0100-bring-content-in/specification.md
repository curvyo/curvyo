# Bring content in: drag designs onto a laser page as live linked clones or placed pages

Status: Ready (2026-11-09; `adrs.md` and `ux-notes.md` exist; ADR 0045 is Proposed and is accepted with the PR; Frage 163 answered B by the customer 2026-11-09, criterion 25)
Priority: Must
Origin: Customer (2026-11-05, translated): "Content arrives as linked clones by drag, or is made directly there. Outputs are compositions with connecting elements. Easy, no unnecessary steps." "You position the pages of the documents on the work area; they snap in by fitting size, can be dragged there, etc. You can still scale and rotate there, without changing the original drawing. Changes in the original drawing carry over live" (2026-10-17). Slice 3 of `specs/0063-manufacturing-workbenches/` (concept A, `concept-output-pages.md` §2.3, §2.7, §5 flows 2 and 3, §8, §9; sketches b, d, i). Frage 150 A (a drag makes a linked clone with its own position) and 155 A (an object moved onto a laser page is its own item by position, Not assigned). Builds on `0098` (#279) and `0099` (#287), whose page-row drag is reused. Details marked **(Proposal)** are the product owner's and vetoable at the demo.

## User value

As a maker preparing a laser job I want to drag my design (a path, a text, a photo, a whole group, or a whole drawing page) onto my laser page and get a live copy there that I can move, turn and scale on the bed, so that I lay out the job in seconds, my drawing is never changed by it, and every later fix in the drawing shows on the bed at once.

**What other tools do.** LightBurn imports or pastes copies onto the bed: a fix in the design means deleting and importing again. xTool Creative Space and the Glowforge app do the same. Inkscape's clone (Alt+D) is live but lands on top of the original, works per object, and a clone of a group is a clone of the whole `<g>` with no per-bed placement. Fusion 360 references the design live, but through a setup dialog and a manual workspace switch. **We do better:** one drag, no dialog; the result is live and has its own place, so moving the original never pushes the job out of position; a badge at the pointer always says what the release will do (Linked clone, Move, Copy); a whole page can be laid on the bed the same way.

## Split of slice 3 (Proposal)

The epic's slice 3 ("bring content in, move versus edit") is two independent changes: a model change (clones of groups, texts and images; movable placed pages; a `format_version` bump) and an interaction change across every edit tool (what an edit tool does to an object outside the active page's context). Each is demoable alone, and only the first is on the road to "engrave with Curvyo". So:

| Part | Spec | Meaning in one line |
|---|---|---|
| Bring content in | `0100` (this spec) | A drag onto a laser page makes linked clones (Alt moves, Ctrl copies), a dragged drawing page row makes a placed page; clones of groups, texts and images; placed pages move, turn, scale and export. |
| Move versus edit | `0106` `move-versus-edit` (reserved) | Objects outside the active page's context only select and move (edit tools show nothing on them), a double-click activates their context (156 A: a pasteboard object activates the last drawing page), the handle-less clone box of `0099` criterion 8 gets its handles, and the laser page's own Layers tree. |

Until `0106`, every object stays editable from every page kind exactly as built (`0098`, `0099`); 156 A's first half (pasteboard objects edit while a drawing page is active) already holds today.

## Acceptance criteria

"Laser page" below means a page of kind Laser whose extension is on; a "Laser (off)" or "Unknown kind" page (`0098` criterion 18) is never a drop target. "Member" is the membership read of ADR 0043 §1 (by position for drawing and laser pages; placed pages by ownership).

### Dragging objects onto a laser page

1. Given a drawing page active and a selection whose objects are members of no laser page (they lie on drawing pages, the pasteboard or over a sketch page), when the maker drags the selection with Select and releases with the pointer over a laser page and no modifier held, then (a) nothing of the selected objects is written: every original keeps every register, its outline within 1e-9 mm and its place in the stacking order; (b) for each selected object exactly one linked clone is created, with own placement on (`0057`; Frage 150 A) and a matrix equal to the drag's translation after snapping (`0060`, as for a Select move), so each clone covers the place its object's ghost had at release; (c) the clones lie on top of the active layer, outside any entered group, in the selection's stacking order; (d) the clones are the selection, the laser page is active and focus is on the canvas; (e) it is one undo step "Place linked clones" (Proposal), whose undo removes every clone, leaves the originals as they were and clears the selection. The clones are own items of the laser page by position and Not assigned (`0098` criterion 11, Frage 155 A). Test: three objects on a drawing page, one on the pasteboard, dragged 300 mm right onto a laser page: four clones, four unchanged originals, one history row.
2. Given the drag of criterion 1 in flight, then while the pointer is over a laser page a badge beside the pointer reads **Linked clone**, with Alt held **Move**, with Ctrl held **Copy** (sketch i; glyph and word, never colour alone); pressing or releasing the modifier with the pointer at rest changes the badge in the same frame. Over any other place there is no badge and the drag behaves as built. While the badge reads Linked clone, the originals are drawn where they started and a ghost of the selection (the "Drag ghost" look of `0099`) follows the pointer (Proposal, UX notes). Nothing is written before the release; Escape cancels the drag as built and writes nothing.
3. Given that drag released over a laser page with Alt held, then it is the plain move of today: the objects themselves move onto the laser page, are its own items by position and Not assigned (155 A), and are drawing objects again wherever they are dragged later; one undo step with the move's label. Alt keeps its `0060` meaning during the drag (it inverts snapping), so an Alt release is a move with inverted snapping (Proposal: the two meanings are kept together; a maker who wants a clone without snapping switches snapping off in the Snapping tab, and the badge says Move whenever Alt is held).
4. Given that drag released over a laser page with Ctrl held (also Ctrl together with Alt, Proposal), then it is the Ctrl copy of a move of `0010`: independent copies land on the laser page as its own items, a copy of a clone is a sibling clone (`0049` criterion 19), the originals are unchanged; one undo step with the copy's label.
5. Given a drag whose selection holds at least one member of a laser page (an own item or a placed page), or one released over a drawing page, a sketch page, the pasteboard or a "Laser (off)" or "Unknown kind" page, then it is a plain move (Ctrl: a copy) exactly as built, with no badge and no clone. An object that overlaps a drawing page and a laser page is a member of the laser page, so dragging it moves it.
6. Given the drag of criterion 1, then each selected object is cloned whatever its kind: path, compound path, primitive (not converted: the original stays a primitive, epic criterion 4 "preparing a job never writes the drawing"), text, image, group (with everything in it, criterion 9) and clone (the new clone follows the dragged clone, a chain, `0049` criterion 3). If any selected clone has depth 8, nothing is created, the objects return, and the refusal of `0049` criterion 52 is shown. Guides (`0046`) and dimensions (`0070`) in the selection get no clone and stay where they are, and a notice of at most 70 characters counts them ("2 guides were not cloned. Guides never run."; Proposal); a selection of only guides and dimensions creates nothing and adds no history step.
7. Given a laser page holding a clone (made by criterion 1, turned by 30 degrees and scaled by 50 %) of a group G on a drawing page that holds a text "ANNA", a rectangle, a circle and an image, when the maker on the drawing page (a) drags a node of the rectangle, (b) changes the text to "ANNE", (c) sets the circle's fill to red, (d) draws a path into G (entered group), (e) moves the circle out of G in the Layers tab, (f) moves and rotates G as a whole, then after each commit the clone shows (a) to (e) in the next frame under its matrix (outlines within 0.01 mm of G's outlines under that matrix), and (f) changes nothing on the laser page (own placement, `0057` criterion 3).

### Clones of groups, texts and images (amends `0049`)

8. Given a selection that holds a group, a text or an image, then **Linked clone** in the Select bar is offered and makes a clone of each as `0049` criterion 1 does (identity matrix, directly above, Follows original). A primitive selected directly is still converted first by the button (`0049` criterion 2 unchanged); a primitive inside a group is never converted. A selection that holds a layer still does not offer Linked clone. A text clone made here (a picture under a matrix, chains allowed, Placement row, no own style) is a different thing from the text clone of `0080` (`text_source`: shared string, own carrier, no chains, its own command); both stay, and the UI names them apart: this one is "Linked clone", the `0080` command keeps its name.
9. Given a clone whose root original is a group, a text or an image, then it draws exactly what its original draws (every visible leaf with its own style, opacity and pixels; a hidden member is not drawn; the original group's own Hide does not hide its clones, only members hidden below the group are left out, ADR 0045 §2), under its composed matrix; stroke widths, dash lengths and markers are not scaled by the matrix (`0049` criterion 9); guides and dimensions inside a group are not drawn by its clones (Proposal). It is hit where its original would be hit, mapped by the matrix, and its box is the bounds of what it draws. Its Style tab block reads "Linked clone of Group 3f9a1c." (text: "Text 3f9a1c", image: "Image 3f9a1c") with the Placement row of `0057`; the Style row (own style), the greyed Stroke, Fill and Markers rows, Spread colours and the Guide on/off flag are not in the tree for such a clone (own style stays for path and primitive roots). Every new clone kind, text clones of this spec included, has the Placement row. Commands that need one outline (Boolean, Combine, Offset, Stroke to path, text carrier, dimension slots, node snapping) treat such a clone as they treat its root's kind: a group, a text or an image (ADR 0045 note 5). A primitive root behaves as a path root: Style row kept, own style allowed. The Node tool shows no nodes on it, with the sentence of `0049` criterion 26.
10. Given such a clone, when the maker presses **Unlink clone**, then it becomes an independent copy of its root with the composed matrix applied, in its place in the stacking order and selected: a group becomes a group of copies of its drawn members (a clone inside follows the same original as before, `0049` criterion 48), a text a text, an image an image; a member whose kind cannot carry the matrix (a skewed text or primitive) is converted silently as `0047` converts on skew; a clone of a primitive becomes a primitive when the matrix keeps it one, else a path (ADR 0045 §5). The drawn result is unchanged (outlines within 0.01 mm). One step "Unlink clone".
11. Given a group G with clones, when G is deleted, then each clone becomes the independent copy of criterion 10 in the same step (`0049` criterion 31, "path" read as "copy"); a clone in the middle of a chain re-points as built. Undo restores G and the clones as clones. Deleting one member of G only changes what the clones show.
12. Given a clone of group G (directly or through a chain), when any command would make a clone draw itself, that is, put it (or a group holding it) inside its original or inside a group its original draws (a Layers drag into G, tiling apply, or any other command that moves nodes into a group), then the command is refused whole, nothing changes, and a notice reads "A clone cannot go inside its own original." (Proposal). Test, besides the self-containment: a clone C1 of G1 inside G2, then a clone C2 of G2 dragged into G1 is refused. A file in which such a clone lies inside its original opens without hang or crash, and that clone is an orphan, removed by the repair of `0049` criterion 35.

### Placing a drawing page

13. Given a drawing page with content, when the maker drags its row out of the page list (the row drag of `0099` criterion 8) and releases it over a laser page, then a placed page (the placement of `0093`, a live reference) of that drawing page is created with scale 1 and rotation 0, the centre of the source page's frame under the pointer, snapped as a Select move snaps the frame box (`0060`); it lies above the laser page's earlier placed pages, is selected, the laser page is active and focus is on the canvas; the drawing page and its objects are unchanged; one undo step "Place page" (Proposal). During the drag the ghost is the source page's frame and the outline of its content, the laser page gets the frame cue of `0099` and the badge reads **Placed page** (Proposal). A release inside the list reorders as built; nothing is written before the drop. **Without fixtures** (until `0102`): the placed page lands where it was dropped; there is no slot and no fit-by-size snap, and the engraved grid is not a snap target.
14. Given that row drag, when the pointer is over a drawing page, a sketch page, the pasteboard or a "Laser (off)" or "Unknown kind" page, then the badge reads "Only on a laser page" with the not-allowed cursor, and a release there (or Escape) creates nothing and adds no history step. A laser page row and an "Unknown kind" row only reorder; a sketch page row keeps `0099` criteria 8 to 10. An empty drawing page may be placed: it draws nothing, and its frame box shows when selected (Proposal).
15. Given a laser page is active and the project has a drawing page, then its Document tab has a **Place page** button between the Machine block and Fixtures (Proposal: the keyboard route for criterion 13) that opens an inline list (no popup) of the drawing pages in list order, each row showing the page name and the page size (for example "125 × 75 mm"; every row is a drawing page, so a kind chip would tell nothing); choosing one (click, Enter or Space) places it with the rules of criterion 13 at the laser page's centre; Escape closes the list, changes nothing and returns focus to the button. The button is hidden (not disabled) when the project has no drawing page.
16. Given a placed page, then it shows every member of its source page live under its matrix (`0093` criterion 6): a rectangle drawn on the source page appears on the laser page in the next frame, an object moved off the source page disappears, and a placed page larger than the work area is drawn in full (`0093` criterion 8).

### Moving placed pages (amends `0093` criterion 7, `0098` criterion 12)

17. Given a laser page with a placed page, when the maker presses anywhere inside the placed page's frame box with Select (Proposal: the whole frame area hits, as an image is hit by its frame, `0058`), then the placed page is selected, never a source object; an own item of the laser page lying above the placed page in the stacking order wins the press where they overlap (topmost wins). A selected placed page's Style tab shows one block, "Placed page of <name>." (name cut at 40 characters; "3 placed pages." for several), and no buttons. Its box is the source page's frame under the placed page's matrix, with the handles of `0019`: move, scale, rotate, skew, Flip (`0056`), the typed X, Y, W, H and angle, arrow keys, align and distribute (`0061`). Each writes only the placed page's link, one step with the transform's label; the source page and its objects are never written. Test: rotated by 90 degrees and scaled to 50 %, every drawn outline equals the source outline under that matrix within 0.01 mm, and the source registers are unchanged.
18. Given a drag, a typed X, Y, W, H or angle, an arrow key, an align or a distribute that would leave a placed page's box with no overlap (area greater than 1e-9 mm^2; an edge-only touch is off the page) with its own laser page's frame, then the whole command is refused: a drag returns at release to where it was, a typed field reverts, nothing is written, no history step, and a notice reads "A placed page stays on its laser page." (Proposal; a placed page belongs to its laser page by ownership, ADR 0042 Consequences). To put a page on another laser page the maker places it there again.
19. Given a selected placed page, then Duplicate and the Ctrl copy of a move (`0010`) make a second placed page of the same source page on the same laser page with the same matrix (displaced by the move for Ctrl), one step each; Delete removes only the reference (`0098` criterion 12). Group is not offered for a selection that holds a placed page (Proposal), and Ctrl+G with one selected shows the notice "A placed page cannot be grouped." Double-clicking inside a placed page keeps the notice of `0093` criterion 7 until `0106`.

### Export (moved here from `0098` criterion 17)

20. Given a project with a drawing page holding a group G (a text, a path and an image) and a laser page holding a clone of G turned by 30 degrees, a placed page of the drawing page turned by 90 degrees, an own rectangle and an engraved grid, when the maker exports SVG (`0024` slice 4), then (a) with **Active page only** and the laser page active, the file holds the rectangle, the clone's drawn leaves and the placed page's drawn members, each at its place on the laser page; the fixture, the origin marker, the placement box and the work-area text are not in it; (b) a whole-plane export writes G on its drawing page and, in addition, the clone and the placed page's members at their placed coordinates; (c) every leaf is written as the export writes that kind (`0024`), under its composed matrix, and Inkscape 1.3 shows every outline within 0.01 mm of the canvas (outlines only: texts and images are written with a `transform`, so the stroke width of a text in a scaled clone scales in the file while the canvas keeps it, a known difference); (d) a clone of a group on a drawing page is written the same way (`0049` criterion 42 extended). Golden files in `tests/fixtures/` for (a), (b) and (d).

### Files, budgets, no I/O

21. Given a project with a group clone (members: text, image, rectangle primitive, path) with own placement on a laser page, a chain clone of that clone, a text clone and an image clone on a drawing page, two placed pages of one drawing page (one turned and scaled), and an object moved onto the laser page with Alt, when it is saved and reopened, then every clone's original and matrix, every placed page's source and matrix, and every own item are identical, every outline draws as before within 1e-9 mm, and the file passes the golden test `bring_content_in_v38.curvyo` (`curvyo-document-core/tests/fixtures/`; goldens as ADR 0045 §9 and §8 list them). A build of format 37 refuses the file with the "saved by a newer version" message; a format-37 file opens unchanged and is not rewritten on open.
22. Given a document of 1,000 objects, then (a) each pointer move of the cloning drag of criterion 1 with a selection of a group of 200 paths, and (b) each pointer move of the row-drag ghost of criterion 13 for a source page of 1,000 objects, takes within 16 ms of thread CPU time in `curvyo-editor-wasm` (as `0099` criterion 20; test under `curvyo-editor-wasm/tests/cpu_time/`); (c) reading 100 clones of a group of 100 paths of 100 nodes (10,000 drawn leaves) takes within 300 ms, the budget of `0049` criterion 43, and a node edit in the group is drawn within 150 ms after the commit (`0049` criterion 44 and its note). Budgets are confirmed in `adrs.md` note 2.
23. Given the whole slice, then there is no network access, no device I/O, no G-code and no telemetry (`R-SYS-002`, `R-SYS-007`).
24. Given a drawing page with placed pages on one or more laser pages, when the maker deletes the drawing page, then its placed pages are removed in the same step, on laser pages too, and one undo restores the drawing page and all its placed pages.
25. Given objects or groups selected on a drawing page (or the pasteboard) and a project with at least one laser page, when the maker presses **Place on…** in the Select bar and picks a laser page from its inline list (keyboard: Tab to the button, Enter, arrows, Enter; Escape closes and returns focus to the button), then linked clones of the selection are made on that laser page exactly as a drop with the default mode would make them (criteria 1 and 2: own placement, placed at the same position relative to the laser page's top-left as the selection's relative to its page, clamped inside the page if it would not fit; one undo step "Place on <page>"), the laser page becomes active and the clones are selected with focus on the canvas. With no laser page the button is not shown (hidden, not disabled). Customer, Frage 163 B, 2026-11-09; list rows show page name and size (Proposal).

This slice changes accepted criteria; the old text is superseded by the criterion named here.

| Spec | Criterion | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0049` | 4, Out of scope | Linked clone not offered for groups; texts and images are no originals | groups, texts and images are originals for the button and the drag; layers stay out (8, 9) |
| `0049` | 28, 31 | Unlink and the delete repair give a path | for a group, text or image root, an independent copy of the root (10, 11) |
| `0049` | 41, 42 | a clone is handed to output as one path | a clone of a group, text or image is handed over as its original's leaves under its matrix (9, 20) |
| `0049` | 2 | a directly selected primitive is converted before cloning | unchanged for the button; the drag clones a primitive without converting it (6), and a primitive root behaves as a path root (9, 10) |

| `0080` | text clones (`text_source`) | the only text clone: shared string, own carrier, no chains | stays unchanged; a Linked clone of a text (8) is a second kind, a picture under a matrix with chains, named apart in the UI |

| `0093` | 7 | moving the placement is out of this slice | placed pages move, turn and scale on their laser page (17 to 19) |

| `0098` | 17 (placed-page part), Out of scope ("a drag across pages is a plain move") | placed pages in export moved to `0100`; any cross-page drag moves | placed pages export (20); a drag onto a laser page clones by default (1 to 5) |

## Out of scope

- Everything of `0106` `move-versus-edit` (split above): move-only selection of objects outside the active page's context, edit tools showing nothing on them, double-click to activate an object's or a placed page's context (156 A), handles on the `0099` pasteboard clone box, the laser page's own Layers tree (placed items expandable, own items, Fixtures layer).
- Slots, fit-by-size snapping and fixture snap targets (`0102`); operations, assignment and the Not assigned look (`0101`).
- Paste as linked clone (Ctrl+Shift+V, concept §5 flow 2): there is no object clipboard yet; it comes with the clipboard story.
- A canvas drag of a whole sketch off its sketch page (`0099` split table: the row drag covers it).
- Placed pages on drawing pages, moving a placed page to another laser page, grouping placed pages with own items, clipping a placed page to its frame.
- Own style for clones of groups, texts and images; clones of layers; changing Linked clone's silent conversion of a directly selected primitive (`0049` criterion 2).
- (moved into scope by the customer's answer to Frage 163, see criterion 25)
- Auto-pan during a drag; dragging content from another project or from the file system onto a laser page.

## Open customer question

**Frage 163 (PLACE-ON): A button to put objects on a laser page by keyboard?** **Answered B by the customer, 2026-11-09 (criterion 25).** A drawing page can be placed with the Place page button (15), but an object reaches a laser page only by drag, or by Linked clone and then typed X/Y.
- A: no, the drag stays the only route for objects (and the typed-position route stays).
- B: a "Place on…" button in the Select bar, opening an inline list of laser pages, making the linked clone there.
- Recommendation: A (one drag already does it; a second route adds a control every maker sees).
- Default if unanswered: A.
