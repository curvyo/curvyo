# Image trace mask: draw the mask on the picture with Rectangle and Lasso, keep it with the image

Status: Draft (Ready after the architect's ADR and the UX notes; the behaviour below is decided by the customer, 2026-11-12)
Priority: Must
Origin: Customer (2026-11-12, German, verbatim): "ich möchte zusätzlich zum rectangle einen lasso haben, dass man mit mehreren mausclicks eine form mit geraden stücken bauen kann. wenn man sie nicht zu macht wird sie automatisch geschlossen. ich möchte, dass du das mask genau so auch in curvyo einbaust, also das select object kommt weg. ist vom workflow eher hakelig. die mask hängt an dem bild, wenn man das nochmal mit trace anclickt, ist die maske wieder da. wenn man das bild löscht wird auch die mask mit gelöscht". Details marked **(Proposal)** are the product owner's and vetoable at the demo.
Model: the sister app Tracyo (`~/tracyo`, `specs/0002-trace-parts/`, read-only): regions drawn on the picture with Rectangle and Lasso and a Subtract toggle; the Mask group sits in the Parts row of Advanced. Where Tracyo and Curvyo differ, this spec says so.
Replaces: the mask routes of `specs/0094-masked-composite-trace` (slice 1, 1b: mask paths as objects, selection route, Shift+click, Pick mask paths) and its rule "parts last while the Trace tool is open" (Frage 133 A). Everything else of 0094 stays: exact stencil edge, Inside / Outside, parts, stacking, Join, analysis per part, text and shapes per part.

## User value
As a maker who traces only a piece of a picture I want to draw that piece directly on the picture (a rectangle, or a shape of straight sides set click by click) and find it again whenever I trace that picture, so that I never draw helper paths, select them together with the image, or build the mask a second time.

## Terms
- **Region**: a rectangle or a lasso shape drawn on the picture, stored in the picture's pixel coordinates. A region is **added** (default) or **subtracted** (drawn while Subtract is pressed). A part's **mask** = union of its added regions minus union of its subtracted ones, independent of drawing order; overlaps count once; a part with only subtracted regions has an empty mask; the mask is cut to the picture.
- **Lasso**: a closed polygon of straight sides from clicks.
- **Inside | Outside**, **Part**, **Join**, **Stencil edge**: as 0094 (Inside traces the mask, Outside the picture minus the mask; the result is cut exactly on the edge).
- **Trace mask** (of an image): the parts of that image: their regions, mode, per-part settings (Proposal below).

## Behaviour
1. **Region tools in the Trace tool.** Two toggle buttons, **Rectangle** and **Lasso**, and the toggle **Subtract**, then Inside | Outside and Clear mask: the Mask group, placed in the Parts row of Advanced behind the chips, Add part and the trash, exactly as Tracyo's ux-notes (§1 to §3 there); the first row of the bar loses its Mask group. One region tool is pressed at a time; Subtract is independent. A tool stays pressed until pressed again, Esc, or the tool is left.
2. **Rectangle.** A drag draws it from corner to corner (Shift: from the centre; Ctrl: a square); under 2 px draws nothing; corners land on whole picture pixels. Edges may lie outside the picture. Not pressed: a click selects a region of the active part (topmost, last drawn), 8 handles resize (Shift about the centre, Ctrl keeps proportions at a corner), a drag inside moves, arrow keys move 1 px (Shift 10 px), Delete or Backspace removes it.
3. **Lasso.** Each click sets a corner; a rubber band shows the next side. The shape is finished and closed by a click on the first point, a double click (the second click adds no corner) or Enter. A **right click** finishes the shape and closes it with one straight line from the last to the first point; no context menu opens while a lasso is in progress. **Esc discards** the lasso in progress (nothing is stored, no History step; Lasso stays pressed, the next Esc follows the Escape cascade) (customer, 2026-11-13). A shape that is not closed when the tool ends any other way (another region tool or rail tool, a chip pick, Add part, Remove part, the pipette, the Trace button, leaving the Trace tool, opening another image) is **closed automatically with one straight line** from the last to the first point. Fewer than 3 points when it ends: dropped, nothing is stored. Backspace while drawing removes the last point **(Proposal)**. Shift while clicking is not special **(Proposal)**. A lasso that crosses itself counts by the non-zero winding rule, as the Boolean operations of 0016 do **(Proposal)**.
4. **Lasso editing.** With no tool pressed a click inside a lasso selects it (dashed selection box, the Node tool's corner square on every corner); a drag inside moves it; dragging a corner moves that corner; Delete removes the whole shape. Adding or deleting a single corner is not part of this story. Selection, hover and the 12 % wash follow Tracyo's ux-notes §5; the region outline is Curvyo's mask path look (black 0.25 mm, at least 1 px; subtracted dashed).
5. **Removed.** Pick mask paths, the selection route (image plus paths), Shift+click on a path, "the image selected together with paths" as a trace input, and the bar state "Use Object to path for text; one image at a time." (`TRACE_MIXED`): a selection of an image with other objects now simply traces the image or images as today (the mask is no longer read from the selection), so the message has no case left and is deleted. Messages of the Trace bar that concern a non-image selection ("The selection is not an image...") stay.
6. **The mask belongs to the image.** The Trace mask is stored on the image object in the document: in the file, in sync, copied with the image (copy/paste, duplicate, drag-copy, also into another document) and moved, scaled, rotated and skewed with it because regions are in picture pixels. Clicking the image with Trace again, also after save and reopen or on another device, shows its regions and parts as they were left. Deleting the image deletes its mask; undo of the delete brings both back. **Linked clone (Proposal):** a linked clone of an image shows the original's mask and parts, and edits made in Trace on the clone change the original's (one mask per source image).
7. **History.** Every finished edit of the mask is one History step: "Draw rectangle", "Draw lasso", "Move region", "Resize region", "Move lasso corner", "Delete region", "Clear mask", "Mask: Inside" / "Mask: Outside" (when it changes a stored part), "Add part", "Remove part", and "Change trace settings" for per-part settings **(Proposal)**. A drag is one step, not one per frame. Undo and redo work while the Trace tool is open and reshow the regions and the preview.
8. **Parts belong to the image too (Proposal).** The whole parts set is stored with the image: for each part its regions, mode and its trace settings, plus which part is active need not be stored. Reason: a mask without its parts' settings would come back with other settings than the maker left. Stored only if the image has at least one region or two or more parts; an untouched image stores nothing. Part 1 without regions keeps today's behaviour (its settings are the remembered global ones, 0089). *Alternative, if the architect finds the settings too heavy for the document:* regions and modes only; a part's settings come back at their defaults. Customer decides only if the architect proposes the alternative.
9. **What ends parts now.** Leaving the Trace tool no longer ends them, and Create paths no longer ends them. Parts end only when: the maker removes them with the trash, the image is deleted, or Clear mask clears the active part's regions (the part itself stays; with several parts the trash removes it). Opening the tool shows the stored parts, the first part active.
10. **Create paths.** As today (one group "Trace of ...", one History step, cut exactly on the region edge, parts joined), and the regions **stay on the image**: Create paths changes no region, part or setting of the image.
11. **Preview.** The preview wash (picture shown at 12 % outside the active part's mask) and the outline only inside the mask are unchanged from 0094 and Tracyo.
12. **Notices** (tooltip of the dimmed Trace button, hidden status region, no visible notice): "0 paths: the mask is outside the image.", "0 paths: the mask paths could not be combined." (one sentence, counts are regions), "0 paths: no part has anything to trace." Chip tooltips and status segments as 0094 / Tracyo ux-notes §4 (the toggles say "Rectangle on" / "Lasso on", "Subtract on" / "Subtract off").

## Reference fixtures
M1 and M2 of 0094 (1000 x 1000 px, 100 x 100 mm; disc centre (25, 50) mm radius 15 mm, square, bar, grey square). **R1**: rectangle x 0 to 50 mm, y 0 to 100 mm. **L1**: lasso with corners (0, 0), (60, 0), (0, 100) mm (its slanted side crosses the disc). **L1c**: L1 with the closing click on the first point.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given an image in the Trace tool, then the first row has no Mask group; the Parts row of Advanced holds, in order, the chips, Add part, trash, then Rectangle, Lasso, Subtract, Inside | Outside, Clear mask (names and tooltips as in 1); no Pick mask paths button exists anywhere in the app.
2. Given Rectangle pressed, when the maker drags on the picture, then a rectangle region is stored (corners on whole pixels), the preview is limited to it and the 12 % wash shows outside; Shift draws from the centre, Ctrl a square, a drag under 2 px draws nothing.
3. Given a selected rectangle, when a handle is dragged (Shift, Ctrl) or the body is dragged, then it resizes (not below 1 px) or moves, and arrow keys and Delete act as in Behaviour 2.
4. Given Lasso pressed, when three clicks and then a click on the first point are made, then a closed lasso region L1c is stored with three corners. A double click after the third corner gives the same shape with three corners (not four); Enter likewise.
5. Given Lasso pressed and the corners of L1 set by three clicks and not closed, when the tool is ended by each of a right click, pressing Rectangle, picking another part, leaving the Trace tool and opening another image, then in each case L1 is stored closed by a straight side from the last to the first corner; and the trace and the stored geometry are identical to L1c.
6. Given Lasso with two clicks, when the tool ends, then nothing is stored and no History step exists. Esc discards a lasso of any length: nothing is stored, no History step. Backspace removes the last point while drawing.
7. Given a selected lasso, when it is moved, a corner is dragged, or Delete is pressed, then the region moves, the corner moves, or the shape is gone; the preview retraces after each.
8. Given Subtract pressed, when a rectangle or a lasso is drawn, then it is subtracted (dashed outline, Remove badge while drawing); the mask is the same whichever region was drawn first.
9. Given M1 with R1 Inside and Threshold 50, when Create paths runs, then no node lies more than 0.01 mm outside R1 and the result is exactly the result of 0094 criterion 2 for R1; with Outside, 0094 criterion 3.
10. Given M1 with L1 Inside, then every vertex of a shape cut by L1's slanted side lies within 0.005 mm of that side and no node lies more than 0.01 mm outside L1; the disc is cut there into straight segments; with L1 Outside the union of both results differs from the unmasked trace by less than 0.01 mm² in area, and the two overlap by less than 0.01 mm² (no gap, no overlap).
11. Given the same image, regions and settings, when Create paths runs twice, then the two results are byte-identical (the document serialisation compared), also after save and reopen of the file.
12. Given an image with R1 and L1 in part 1 and a second part with a region and other settings, when the file is saved and reopened (also on another device through sync), and the image is clicked with Trace, then the regions, modes, number of parts and each part's settings are as left, and the preview and Create paths give the same result as before saving.
13. Given an image with a mask, when it is moved, scaled non-uniformly and rotated by 30 degrees, then Trace shows the regions on the same picture content, and Create paths cuts the same picture content (the traced result transformed by the placement matches within 0.01 mm).
14. Given an image with a mask, when it is copied and pasted (also into a second document) or duplicated, then the copy has the same regions and parts; editing the copy's mask changes only the copy.
15. Given a linked clone of an image with a mask, then Trace on the clone shows the original's regions; an edit made there is visible on the original; deleting the clone does not delete the mask (Behaviour 6).
16. Given an image with a mask, when the image is deleted, then no mask data remains in the saved file; one undo brings back the image and its mask exactly.
17. Given a mask edit (each kind listed in Behaviour 7), then exactly one History step with that label is added; undo restores the previous regions, redo reapplies; a drag adds one step.
18. Given two peers that open the same image and each draws a different region offline, when they sync, then both peers converge to the same document (byte-identical serialisation) without a panic; **target (Proposal):** both regions are present; if the architect's model keeps the whole mask as one value, the later write wins on both peers and the loss is stated in the ADR and `docs/technical-debt.md`.
19. Given Create paths, then the image's regions, parts and settings are unchanged (compared before and after), and leaving the Trace tool and entering it again shows the same parts and the same active part 1.
20. Given Clear mask, then all regions of the active part are removed in one step and the preview shows the whole image; the part and its settings stay.
21. Given a selection of an image and a text, a path or a second image, then the Trace button works as for the images alone, no mask is read from the other objects, and the string "Use Object to path for text; one image at a time." does not exist in the code or the docs.
22. Given an old file (format 44 or lower), then it opens, images have no mask, nothing is migrated; a file saved by this version carries `format_version` 45 (or the architect's number), and an older build refuses it with the existing "newer file" message.
23. Given a lasso that crosses itself, then the mask is the non-zero-winding area and the result is deterministic (criterion 11).
24. Given 8 parts with 20 regions each (rectangles and lassos, added and subtracted, overlapping), then editing and retracing work without a panic and without a frame over 50 ms on the UI thread; invalid geometry gives the notice of Behaviour 12.
25. Given the reference pictures of the UX notes (`reference/`, re-captured from the build), then the Trace bar states match the new layout; a test fails when the Mask group is in the first row. Curvyo has no committed Trace bar screenshots, so the check is the browser cases B1 to B5 of the UX notes plus those pictures.

## Document model, format and sync (flag for the architect)
- Changes the document model (the image object gains the trace mask: parts, regions as pixel polygons with a sign, mode, per-part settings), persistence (new field in the container, `format_version` change, see criterion 22) and sync (merge rules for concurrent mask edits, criterion 18). Under CLAUDE.md §3 this is an ADR on document model, persistence and sync: **needs-customer** by the rule, but the customer has already decided the behaviour (mask with the image, back after reopening, deleted with it); the architect writes the ADR and asks only if they leave that behaviour (the settings alternative of Behaviour 8, or whole-value merge in criterion 18).
- Migration: none; masks of the old kind were never stored. Mask paths a maker drew under 0094 stay ordinary paths.
- Every importer/exporter touched (the container read/write; SVG export is unchanged since the mask is not artwork) gets a golden-file test in `tests/fixtures/`.

## Out of scope
- Ellipse region, rotated rectangles, adding or deleting a single lasso corner, curved lasso sides, freehand lasso, feathered masks, SVG mask import, regions as artwork, a mask for sketch pages, masks for several images at once.
- Joining finished trace groups (0094 "Later").
- Telemetry, accounts, anything leaving the machine: none.

## Open questions (each has a safe default; none blocks the build)
- **Frage 1 (CLONE-MASK): Linked clone and the mask.** A: the clone shows and edits the original's mask (default). B: each clone has its own mask. C: the clone cannot be traced.
- **Frage 2 (MASK-SETTINGS): Parts' settings in the file.** A: store the whole parts set (default, Behaviour 8). B: regions and modes only, settings at defaults on return.
- **Frage 3 (SYNC-MASK): Concurrent mask edits.** A: regions merge individually, both survive (default target). B: the whole mask is one value, last write wins.
- **Frage 4 (ELLIPSE-REGION):** ellipse region later, as its own story (default: not now). **SVG mask import** (default: not now). **Rotated rectangle** (default: draw a lasso).
- **Frage 5 (ASSET-SWAP): The picture's pixels change** (any operation that replaces the asset or crops it, if one exists now or later). Default: the mask is dropped with a visible History step, never moved silently; the architect names the operations.

## Advanced block (UX notes §2.3, §2.5, customer 2026-11-13)
The Advanced block is one flowing line (Output row, Denoise, Upscale, Corners, the Parts and Mask unit, Analyse) that wraps only when the width does not suffice, the unit as a whole first; with Advanced open the bar is as wide as the wider of its first row and the Advanced line, at most the available width.
