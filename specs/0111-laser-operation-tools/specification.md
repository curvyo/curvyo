# Laser operation tools: operation buttons in the Select bar, groups, the Operations view toggle, settings unfolded in the row

Status: Draft (Ready after the customer merges this PR)
Priority: Must
Origin: Customer (laser redesign after the PR #333 demo, 2026-11-12: Fragen 197 A, 198, 205 B, the added decisions on operation settings and the motorised Z axis; screens "die sehen gut aus"). Slice 1 of 3 of the redesign (`0112` `test-grid-and-materials`, `0113` `fixture-sets-and-pins` follow). Details marked **(Proposal)** are the product owner's and vetoable at the demo; the P-numbers refer to `docs/design-drafts/2026-11-laser-redesign/README.md`.

## User value

As a maker preparing a laser job I want to give what I have selected an operation with one press next to the object, to give a whole group one operation or one object inside it its own, and to set an operation's numbers and finishing options right under its row, so that I never switch to a tab and back to say what is cut or engraved, never fight a member list, and the list stays short.

**What other tools do.** LightBurn assigns by colour and edits a layer in a separate dialog; the cut settings sit in a panel away from the list row. Fusion 360 picks geometry per operation in a modal dialog. xTool sets the process per object, no list. **We do better:** the five kinds are symbols at the selection, a repeated press reuses the operation the maker already has (Shift for a new one), a group is one thing until the maker enters it, and the numbers unfold under the row they belong to. Crosshatch, bidirectional fill, scan angle, overscanning and Z offset are kept on the operation where they act, not in the material (customer).

## Words (shown in the UI)

- **Kind buttons:** five symbol-only buttons in the Select bar: Cut, Score, Engrave, Engrave + Outline, Cut-out. The name is the tooltip and the accessible name.
- **Last operation of a kind:** the operation of that kind lowest in the list, not generated by a test grid (`0112`) and not an Unknown operation.
- **Entered group:** a group opened by a double-click, read-only; the objects inside can be selected and given operations, nothing else.
- **Operations view:** the toggle at the page name on the canvas (symbol `ListTree`); pressed = every item in its operation look, unpressed = the drawing's own style (the former "View: Operations / Drawn").
- **Unfolded row:** the one operation row opened in place, showing its settings.
- **Operation-only settings:** Crosshatch, Bidirectional fill, Scan angle, Overscanning, Z offset. They belong to the operation, never to a material.
- **Motorised Z axis:** a capability of the laser's machine profile; with it the laser can move the focus height by itself.

## Acceptance criteria

"Laser page", "item", "leaf", "Not assigned" and "nearest rule" are as in `0101`; "in context" is as in `0106`.

### Kind buttons in the Select bar

1. Given a laser page is active and the selection holds only objects in context (own items, groups, clones, placed pages), then the Select bar shows the five kind buttons, symbol only, in the order Cut, Score, Engrave, Engrave + Outline, Cut-out, right after the settings switches (P1). Given no selection, a selection on a drawing, sketch or "Laser (off)" page, or a selection that holds an out-of-context object (`0106` criterion 4), then the buttons are absent (hidden, not disabled). Test: select a rectangle on a laser page, the five buttons exist; select a rectangle on a drawing page, none exist.
2. Given a kind button, then its tooltip and accessible name are the kind name ("Engrave + Outline"), the tooltip has no second line (no operating tips; Shift+press of criterion 5 is a shortcut without tip text), and the buttons are reachable by Tab in bar order. No visible text caption. Kinds differ by symbol, never by colour alone (`0101` criterion 18).
3. Given a selection and a press on a kind button, when the page has a last operation of that kind (Words), then the whole selection is added to that operation in one undo step "Assign to <name>" and no operation is created (Frage 205 B). Test: Engrave 1 and Engrave 2 exist, rectangle A selected, Engrave pressed: A is in Engrave 2 only and the list still has two Engrave rows.
4. Given a press on a kind button and no last operation of that kind, then one operation "<Kind> <N>" (naming of `0101` criterion 2) is created at the end of its section, Show on, Output on, the selection is assigned to it, in one undo step "Assign to <name>".
5. Given a press with Shift held, then a new operation of that kind is always created, named as in criterion 4, and the selection is assigned to it, even when operations of that kind exist.
6. Given the only operation of a kind is generated by a test grid (`0112`) or is an Unknown operation, then it is not the last operation of that kind: the press creates a new one (criteria 4). Generated rows never receive hand-assigned objects through a kind button.
7. Given a selection whose objects are all already in the last operation of that kind, then the press changes nothing and writes no history step. Given a selection that holds guides, dimensions or fixtures, then they are skipped as in `0101` criterion 10 (notice "N guides not assigned. Guides never run.", at most 70 characters); a selection of only such objects creates no operation and writes no step.
8. Given the Operations tab, then it has no add row of kind buttons and no way to create an empty operation: operations are created by assignment (criteria 3 to 5) or by a test grid. The "Test grid" button of `0028` criterion 1 stays on the tab until `0112` replaces it by the tool (Proposal; keeps the demo whole). The tick, the drag onto a row and the number keys 1 to 9 of `0101` criterion 8 stay as built.

### Groups

9. Given a laser page and a group of its own items, when the maker clicks it once, then the group is selected as one item (box and handles of the group, as built) and the bar shows the kind buttons. A press on a kind button assigns the group (criterion 3 to 5) so that **every leaf in the group runs in that operation**: the operation holds the group's key, and every node inside the group (any depth) that already holds any assignment of its own is added to the operation too, so the nearest rule of `0101` criterion 12 does not shadow it. Memberships those nodes hold in other operations stay (Proposal). One undo step "Assign to <name>". Test: group G with letters A, B; B is in Engrave 1; select G, press Cut: A and B both run in Cut 1, B still runs in Engrave 1.
10. Given a group, then in the Operations tab it counts as one item. Its tick in an operation row shows checked when every leaf of the group runs in that operation, mixed (dash) when some do, unchecked when none; a mixed row shows "Group 3 in part" in its second line; a press on the mixed tick adds the rest as in criterion 9; unticking removes the group key and the member keys of that operation from the group, in one undo step "Remove from <name>" (P2, Proposal). The members are never named in the tab.
11. Given a group, when the maker double-clicks it, then it is entered read-only: the crumb reads "<page> › <group> · Read-only · Esc: leave", everything outside is dimmed to 35 %, the group outline is in the accent colour, no handles, no drag, no nudge, no edit command, no node edit. The bar holds only the five kind buttons (P1, Proposal: transform and other buttons are hidden, not disabled, while inside). On a laser page this replaces the editable group entry of `0106` criterion 13 (second double-click). A nested group is entered one level per double-click (Proposal). Given a group on a drawing page, then entering is as built.
12. Given an entered group, when the maker presses an object inside it (or marquees inside it, Proposal), then that object is selected and a kind button assigns it as an item of its own, by criteria 3 to 5, with the operation of the nearest rule: an object with its own assignment runs there, not with the group's (`0101` criterion 12 unchanged). The Operations tab ticks follow the selected object.
13. Given an entered group, when the maker presses Escape, or presses an object or empty canvas outside the group (Proposal), then the group is left, the crumb and dimming go, and the group (Escape) or the pressed object stays selected. Entering and leaving are not history steps.
14. Given the Operations tab, then it lists operations only and never lists the members of a group or of a clone. Given a clone, then it takes assignments as a whole only; its member rows no longer exist in the Layers tree and cannot be assigned (customer: clone members leave operations; reverses Frage 167 B). A leaf inside a clone runs with the operations of the clone (`0101` criterion 12 with the chain ending at the clone). **Frage 212** decides what happens to clone-member keys of old files; **Frage 213** covers inner rows of placed pages.

### Operations view toggle

15. Given a laser page, then its canvas label (page name and Laser chip) carries one toggle button with the symbol `ListTree`, 20 px, name and tooltip "Operations view", `aria-pressed`. Pressed (default): every item is drawn by its operation look and symbols as `0101` criterion 18. Unpressed: every item in the drawing's own style, no symbols, Not assigned look off. The state is per laser page, session state, not saved, not a history step.
16. Given the Operations tab, then the view switch is gone from it; it holds Cuts last, the list, the summary and (until `0112`) the Test grid button. Cuts last stays above the list.

### Operation settings unfolded in the row

17. Given the Operations tab and one operation row, when the maker presses the row, then it is selected and unfolds directly under itself; any row unfolded before folds (one at a time, Frage 197 A); a press on the unfolded row's own area folds it (Proposal). The row keeps Show, Output, armed trash, arrows, rename (`0101` criteria 3 to 5, 16) in every state. The block below the list of `0028` criterion 24 is gone for one selected row. Given several rows selected, then nothing unfolds and the block of `0028` criterion 27 (material pick only) stays as built.
18. Given an unfolded Cut, Score, Engrave or Engrave + Outline row, then its upper part shows what `0028` criteria 24 to 26 define, unchanged in meaning: the material pick (inline list), Power, Passes, Speed (and for Engrave + Outline the outline set), the states live / stored / own, "Differs from <name>" with the material's number and "Reset to material". An operation without numbers shows "No settings" in that place. Plain fields, no sliders.
19. Given an unfolded Engrave or Engrave + Outline row, then its numbers also hold **Interval** in mm (the distance between hatch lines): range 0.01 to 2.00, two decimals, default 0.10 (Proposal). Interval is stored in `op_settings.main` (`interval_mm`) because `0112` makes it a material number. In this slice no material has an interval: a material pick, a refresh of stored numbers and "Reset to material" keep the stored value, and a typed interval on a linked operation is written to `main` on the operation, not as an override. `0112` needs no shape change and then follows the `0028` pointing rules.
20. Given an unfolded row, then under a hairline it shows the operation-only settings (Words). **Engrave and Engrave + Outline:** Crosshatch (an icon toggle with the symbol `#`, off), Bidirectional fill (an icon toggle with two stacked arrows, on), Scan angle (degrees, -90.0 to 90.0, one decimal, default 0), Overscanning (percent, whole numbers 0 to 50, default 2), and Z offset. **Cut, Score and Cut-out:** Z offset alone. Defaults: Z offset 0.0 mm, range -20.0 to 20.0, one decimal (Proposal). The two toggles are 28 px icon buttons with tooltip and accessible name, no caption; the other values are plain fields with their unit. The operation-only settings are stored in their own register `op_options` of the operation, not in `op_settings`, so no material path can touch them and "No settings" can still carry them (ADR 0056). Meanings: Crosshatch hatches a second time at the scan angle plus 90 degrees; Bidirectional fill alternates the hatch direction line by line (off: one direction); Scan angle turns the hatch direction (for wood grain); Overscanning lets the head run past the shape's edge by that percentage of the run-out the speed needs and switches off exactly at the edge (P11, as LightBurn).
21. Given the laser's machine profile has a motorised Z axis, then Z offset is shown; without it the field is absent (not disabled) and no value runs. A Z offset already stored when the capability is off is kept and saved back unchanged, runs nothing and is shown again when the capability returns. A focus test is out of scope.
22. Given a machine profile, then it has the capability **Motorised Z axis** (yes or no, default no), edited where the profile's other machine values are edited (one undo step "Motorised Z axis on/off"), stored in the machine profile record (profile record format 4; an older record reads it as off) and copied into the project with the profile copy (ADR 0039), so the page reads it without the profile file. Test: a profile with the capability on and a laser page of it show Z offset; the same page after the capability is switched off in the project's profile copy does not.
23. Given a material pick, "Use material", "Reset to material", a refresh of stored numbers (ADR 0054 §3) or a material edit, then none of them changes an operation-only setting or Interval (customer: the material set holds power, speed, passes and interval only). Test: Engrave with Crosshatch on and Scan angle 15, pick a material, edit the material: both stay.
24. Given any typed value in an unfolded row outside its range, then it is refused with the range in the field's message and nothing is written; a valid value is one undo step "Edit settings"; a toggle is one undo step "Edit settings" too. Several operations are edited one at a time.

### Files, budgets, no output

25. Given a laser page with operations carrying Interval and operation-only settings, a Z offset with the capability off, and a profile copy with the capability, when it is saved and reopened, then everything is identical; the golden `operation_tools_v44.curvyo` passes, and `operation_options_damaged_v44.curvyo` (options not a map, an unknown key, scan angle 90.1, overscan 51, Crosshatch on a Cut, an interval on a Cut, interval 0.005) reads each as damaged settings and saves back unchanged (`format_version` 44, ADR 0056 §9). A format 43 file opens unchanged, shows defaults for the new settings and is not rewritten on open. An unknown key in the new fields reads as damaged settings as `0028` criterion 28 and ADR 0054 §1 define.
26. Given the budgets (thread CPU time, `curvyo-editor-wasm/tests/cpu_time/`; the architect confirms the limits): a press on a kind button assigning 1,000 items, and a press assigning a group of 1,000 leaves, each take within 16 ms; entering a group of 1,000 leaves within 16 ms; unfolding a row on a page with 50 operations within 16 ms.
27. Given the whole slice, then there is no network access, no device I/O, no G-code, no output file and no telemetry (`R-SYS-002`, `R-SYS-007`). The new settings are stored and shown; `0029` reads them for the output.

| ADR 0038 / 0039 | machine profile and its project copy | no Z capability | plus Motorised Z axis in the profile record (format 4) and the copy (22) |

## Out of scope

- Test grid object and tool, test ids, Materials tab, materials shared across lasers, photo and note, judging (`0112`); fixture sets, pins, centre marks, job fixtures (`0113`).
- Material sets holding Interval (`0112`); the other operation-only settings in materials: never.
- Focus test; image engraving with dithering and a gradient rectangle; a back-side aid.
- Output, preview and run order of the new settings (`0029`).
- Operations on other machine kinds; a Z axis for other machines.
- Renaming or recolouring kinds; a keyboard shortcut per kind (Proposal: later).

## Open customer questions

Defaults apply until the customer answers; none blocks the build.

**Frage 212 (CLONE-MEMBER-KEYS): What happens to clone-member assignments already saved in files (`member.<clone>/<member>` keys, Frage 167 B)?**
- A: kept in the file, ignored (run nothing, shown nowhere, counted nowhere) and saved back unchanged, like a dangling key (`0101` criterion 27). The member then runs with the clone's operations. No migration.
- B: on open, a clone that holds member keys is shown a one-time notice, keys still ignored.
- C: removed on the next save (data loss, no undo).
- Recommendation: A (nothing lost, no format change for this part, reversible).
- Default if unanswered: A.

**Frage 213 (INNER-ROWS): Do the inner rows of placed pages (`0101` criterion 11, assignment by source object id) stay?** The customer's decision names group members and clone members only.
- A: stay as built; only clones and groups change.
- B: also leave: a placed page is assigned as a whole.
- Recommendation: A (not part of the decision; one source object may need another operation than its page).
- Default if unanswered: A.
